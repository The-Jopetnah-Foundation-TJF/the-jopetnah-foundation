# Volunteer Onboarding Workflow — The Jopetnah Foundation

Thank you for your interest in supporting Jopetnah. We want the process to be clear, respectful of your time, and aligned with our values of integrity, transparency, and community ownership.

## Step-by-Step Process

**1. Express Interest**
Open an "Offer of Support" issue in this repository, or email us at `thejopetnahfoundation@gmail.com`.

**2. Initial Review**
A team member reviews your submission within 5–7 business days and responds to acknowledge receipt.

**3. Short Conversation**
We schedule a brief call (20–30 minutes) to understand your skills, availability, and interests, and to answer your questions about Jopetnah.

**4. Role Matching & Agreement**
If there's a good fit, we agree on:
- Specific tasks or project
- Time commitment
- Reporting / check-in rhythm
- Any required confidentiality or safeguarding acknowledgements

**5. Onboarding**
- Welcome message and relevant background documents
- Access to shared tools (only as needed)
- Introduction to key contacts
- Brief orientation on our values, safeguarding, and communication norms

**6. Active Engagement & Feedback**
Regular light-touch check-ins. We value your feedback and will adjust as needed.

**7. Completion or Transition**
When a project ends or your availability changes, we conduct a short closing conversation and, with your permission, may provide a reference or acknowledgement of your contribution.

## Important Notes

- All volunteer roles are unpaid.
- We prioritize roles that directly advance our mission and pilots.
- Safeguarding and confidentiality are non-negotiable — see our [Safeguarding Policy](governance/TJF_Governance_Bundle_FINAL.pdf) for details.
- You may step back at any time — just let us know.

We are grateful for every person who chooses to walk with us in building whole communities, one life at a time.
