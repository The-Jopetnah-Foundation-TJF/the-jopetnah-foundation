About TJF

What is The Jopetnah Foundation?

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) is a nonprofit initiative being established in Uganda. It brings education, health, livelihoods and environmental care together, because families do not face one problem at a time. Our slogan is “Building whole communities, one life at a time.”

Is TJF registered?

TJF is in the final stages of registration as a Company Limited by Guarantee and NGO in Uganda. We will publish the registration number here as soon as it is issued. Until then, please describe TJF as a nonprofit initiative being established.

What are TJF’s four pillars?

Education & Youth Development; Health & Wellbeing; Livelihoods; and Environment. We work across them together, with the family and community at the centre. See our programmes.

Who leads TJF?

TJF was founded by Mr Okello Carlos Torach, who is also its Executive Director. Read more on the founder page.

Is TJF connected to Jopetnah International Limited?

The founder is also Managing Director of that company. TJF is a separate and independent organisation.

Read to Lead

What is Read to Lead?

Read to Lead is TJF’s first project: two community centres with libraries at their heart, in Pakele (Adjumani District) and Teopok (Omoro District). Together they are planned to serve about 1,000 children and adults, roughly 500 at each. See the community centre page.

Has the land been acquired?

Yes. Land has been acquired at both sites.

Why Pakele and Teopok?

The two sites were chosen because of land availability, real need and local interest.

When will the libraries open?

We do not yet have an opening date, and we will not announce one until it is real. The next step is community consultation, in local languages, once registration is complete.

How will communities be involved?

We plan to ask each community what it needs before anything is designed. Consultation comes first.

Giving and helping

Can I donate?

Not yet. Online donations are not active. Please do not send money to personal accounts or respond to any payment request that does not come from the official channels on this website. See how to help now.

Can I give books or equipment?

Please contact us first so offers can be matched to real needs. See giving in kind.

Can I volunteer?

TJF is not yet placing volunteers on site. We welcome applications for advice and preparation roles, such as librarians, educators, designers and finance or data-protection advisers. Apply here.

How do I partner with TJF?

Start with “here is what our organisation is good at.” See partnerships or contact us.

Trust and safety

How does TJF protect children?

TJF publishes a Child Protection Policy and safeguarding standards, and does not publish photographs of identifiable children. Read our safeguarding page.

What happens to my data?

We collect only what you send us, to reply or to send the newsletter. Read the privacy notice.

Where can I read TJF’s policies?

On the transparency page, including the Constitution, Board Charter, Conflict of Interest, Whistleblower and Safeguarding policies.

How do I raise a concern or complaint?

Use the feedback and complaints page or contact us.

Looking ahead

Will TJF work outside Uganda?

Our work begins in Uganda. As our model matures and the evidence supports it, TJF hopes to share what it learns with communities across East Africa and, in time, across the African continent. That is a hope, not a promise: we will grow only as fast as our governance, safeguarding and results allow.

How can I follow progress?

Read our news and subscribe to TJF-The Next Page, our newsletter.
