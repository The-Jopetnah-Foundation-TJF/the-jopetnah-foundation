# Partner Due Diligence Process

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) is committed to establishing partnerships that are ethical, transparent, responsible and aligned with its mission and values.

This Partner Due Diligence Process provides a structured approach for verifying potential partners before formal collaboration.

Due diligence enables TJF to assess partner suitability, identify risks, confirm organizational capacity and establish appropriate safeguards before entering partnership agreements.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Process is to:

- Verify potential partners.
- Protect TJF's reputation.
- Reduce partnership risks.
- Confirm legal compliance.
- Assess organizational capacity.
- Strengthen accountability.
- Support informed partnership decisions.

---

# 2. Scope

This Process applies to:

- Strategic partners.
- Programme implementation partners.
- Funding partners.
- Technical partners.
- Research institutions.
- Community organizations.
- Service providers.

The level of due diligence should be proportionate to the size, nature and risk level of the partnership.

---

# 3. Due Diligence Principles

The process shall be guided by:

## Proportionality

The depth of assessment should reflect:

- Partnership value.
- Risk exposure.
- Programme complexity.
- Geographic context.

---

## Objectivity

Assessments should be based on evidence rather than assumptions or personal relationships.

---

## Confidentiality

Information collected during due diligence should be protected and used only for legitimate organizational purposes.

---

## Transparency

Partners should understand:

- Assessment requirements.
- Approval processes.
- Expectations.

---

# 4. Due Diligence Process Overview

The standard process is:

```
Partner Identification
          │
          ▼
Information Request
          │
          ▼
Document Review
          │
          ▼
Organizational Assessment
          │
          ▼
Risk Screening
          │
          ▼
Due Diligence Report
          │
          ▼
Approval Decision
          │
          ▼
Partnership Agreement
```

---

# 5. Information Required from Partners

Potential partners may be requested to provide:

## Legal Documentation

Including:

- Registration certificate.
- Operating licenses.
- Organizational constitution or governing documents.
- Tax compliance documentation where applicable.

---

## Governance Information

Including:

- Board or leadership structure.
- Management arrangements.
- Organizational policies.
- Decision-making processes.

---

## Financial Information

Including:

- Recent financial statements.
- Audit reports where available.
- Financial controls documentation.
- Funding information.

---

## Programme Capacity Information

Including:

- Previous project experience.
- Technical expertise.
- Staffing structure.
- Implementation capacity.

---

## Policy and Compliance Information

Including:

- Code of Conduct.
- Safeguarding policies.
- Anti-fraud measures.
- Data protection practices.
- Risk management procedures.

---

# 6. Due Diligence Assessment Areas

## 6.1 Legal Compliance Review

Assessment questions:

- Is the organization legally registered?
- Does it have authority to operate?
- Are required permits available?
- Are there regulatory concerns?

---

## 6.2 Governance Review

Assessment questions:

- Does the organization have effective leadership?
- Are roles and responsibilities clear?
- Are governance systems functional?

---

## 6.3 Financial Review

Assessment questions:

- Are financial controls adequate?
- Are financial records maintained?
- Are audits conducted where appropriate?
- Are there financial risks?

---

## 6.4 Operational Capacity Review

Assessment questions:

- Can the partner deliver agreed activities?
- Does it have qualified personnel?
- Does it have appropriate systems?

---

## 6.5 Reputation Review

Assessment questions:

- Does the organization have a positive reputation?
- Are there public concerns?
- Has it previously managed partnerships successfully?

---

## 6.6 Safeguarding Review

Assessment questions:

- Does the partner protect vulnerable groups?
- Are safeguarding procedures established?
- Are reporting mechanisms available?

---

# 7. Risk Classification

Partners may be classified as:

## Low Risk

Characteristics:

- Strong governance.
- Good reputation.
- Established systems.
- Limited exposure.

Approval:

Standard review process.

---

## Medium Risk

Characteristics:

- Some capacity gaps.
- Limited previous partnership experience.
- Additional monitoring required.

Approval:

Enhanced review and risk mitigation plan.

---

## High Risk

Characteristics:

- Significant weaknesses.
- Compliance concerns.
- Reputation issues.
- Large financial exposure.

Approval:

Senior management review and enhanced controls.

---

# 8. Due Diligence Report

The assessment report should include:

- Partner profile.
- Assessment findings.
- Strengths.
- Weaknesses.
- Identified risks.
- Recommended controls.
- Approval recommendation.

---

# 9. Approval Decision

Possible outcomes:

## Approved

Partner meets requirements.

---

## Approved with Conditions

Partner may proceed subject to:

- Additional controls.
- Capacity strengthening.
- Increased monitoring.

---

## Deferred

Further information is required.

---

## Rejected

Partner does not meet TJF requirements.

---

# 10. Enhanced Due Diligence

Additional assessment may be required where:

- Partnership value is significant.
- Partner operates in high-risk environments.
- Public funding is involved.
- Safeguarding risks exist.
- Reputation concerns are identified.

Enhanced measures may include:

- Reference checks.
- Site visits.
- Additional documentation review.
- Independent verification.

---

# 11. Records Management

Due diligence records should include:

- Assessment checklist.
- Supporting documents.
- Risk analysis.
- Approval decisions.
- Partnership agreement.
- Review history.

Records should be securely maintained.

---

# 12. Continuous Monitoring

Due diligence does not end after approval.

TJF should monitor:

- Partner performance.
- Compliance.
- Emerging risks.
- Significant organizational changes.

Reassessment may be required when circumstances change.

---

# 13. Conflict of Interest

Personnel conducting assessments must disclose any conflict of interest.

Individuals with conflicts should not participate in final approval decisions.

---

# Conclusion

Effective due diligence enables The Jopetnah Foundation to build partnerships based on trust, accountability and shared values.

By carefully assessing partners before collaboration, TJF protects its mission, strengthens programme delivery and ensures responsible stewardship of resources.

---

> **Responsible partnerships begin with responsible choices. Due diligence ensures that every collaboration strengthens trust, impact and integrity.**
