# Stakeholder Engagement Policy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that meaningful stakeholder engagement is fundamental to achieving sustainable development outcomes. Effective engagement ensures that the voices, experiences and expectations of stakeholders inform organizational planning, programme implementation, monitoring and evaluation.

This Policy establishes a structured approach to identifying, engaging and maintaining relationships with stakeholders in a transparent, inclusive and accountable manner.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Policy aims to:

- Promote inclusive participation in decision-making.
- Strengthen trust and accountability.
- Improve programme relevance and effectiveness.
- Support transparency and collaboration.
- Foster long-term strategic relationships.
- Ensure stakeholder perspectives inform organizational learning.

---

# 2. Scope

This Policy applies to engagement with:

- Beneficiaries.
- Local communities.
- Government institutions.
- Development partners.
- Donor organizations.
- Civil society organizations.
- Community-based organizations.
- Corporate partners.
- Academic institutions.
- Media organizations.
- Suppliers and service providers.
- Staff and volunteers.

---

# 3. Guiding Principles

Stakeholder engagement should be:

- Inclusive.
- Respectful.
- Transparent.
- Timely.
- Accessible.
- Evidence-informed.
- Culturally sensitive.
- Responsive.
- Non-discriminatory.

---

# 4. Stakeholder Identification

Stakeholders should be identified through a structured mapping process based on:

- Interest in TJF's work.
- Level of influence.
- Potential impact of TJF activities.
- Vulnerability.
- Legal or regulatory responsibilities.
- Opportunities for collaboration.

A stakeholder register should be maintained and reviewed regularly.

---

# 5. Stakeholder Categories

Examples include:

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| Primary Stakeholders | Beneficiaries, communities |
| Government | Ministries, departments, local governments |
| Development Partners | Bilateral and multilateral agencies |
| Donors | Foundations, philanthropic organizations, individual donors |
| Civil Society | NGOs, CBOs, faith-based organizations |
| Private Sector | Corporate partners, suppliers |
| Academia | Universities, research institutions |
| Internal Stakeholders | Board, staff, volunteers |

---

# 6. Engagement Methods

Engagement may include:

- Community meetings.
- Workshops.
- Consultations.
- Surveys.
- Focus group discussions.
- Public forums.
- Stakeholder roundtables.
- Digital engagement.
- One-to-one meetings.
- Joint planning sessions.

Methods should be appropriate to the stakeholder group and context.

---

# 7. Engagement Planning

Each significant engagement should define:

- Objectives.
- Stakeholders involved.
- Key messages.
- Responsibilities.
- Timeline.
- Resources required.
- Risks and mitigation measures.
- Expected outcomes.

---

# 8. Communication

Communication with stakeholders should be:

- Clear.
- Honest.
- Timely.
- Accessible.
- Respectful.

Information should be shared in formats and languages that are appropriate to the audience where feasible.

---

# 9. Feedback and Grievances

TJF should provide accessible channels for stakeholders to:

- Offer feedback.
- Ask questions.
- Raise concerns.
- Submit complaints.
- Suggest improvements.

Feedback should be acknowledged, documented and addressed in a timely manner.

Where appropriate, issues should be referred to TJF's Complaints Handling Mechanism or Safeguarding procedures.

---

# 10. Participation and Inclusion

Special consideration should be given to ensuring the participation of:

- Women and girls.
- Children (where appropriate and safeguarded).
- Older persons.
- Persons with disabilities.
- Marginalized or underserved communities.
- Minority groups.

Engagement activities should seek to remove barriers to participation.

---

# 11. Partnerships and Collaboration

Stakeholder engagement should encourage:

- Joint planning.
- Shared learning.
- Co-design of programmes.
- Knowledge exchange.
- Innovation.
- Resource sharing where appropriate.

Collaborative relationships should be based on mutual respect and clearly defined expectations.

---

# 12. Documentation

Records should be maintained for significant engagement activities, including:

- Participant lists.
- Agendas.
- Minutes.
- Feedback received.
- Agreed actions.
- Follow-up activities.

Records should be managed in accordance with the Records Management Policy.

---

# 13. Monitoring and Evaluation

Stakeholder engagement may be assessed using indicators such as:

| Indicator | Measurement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Engagement activities conducted | Number |
| Stakeholder participation | Number / Percentage |
| Feedback received | Number |
| Issues resolved | Percentage |
| Stakeholder satisfaction | Survey results |
| Partnerships initiated | Number |

Findings should inform programme improvement and organizational learning.

---

# 14. Roles and Responsibilities

## Board of Trustees

- Oversees strategic stakeholder relationships.
- Promotes accountability and transparency.

---

## Executive Director

- Provides leadership for significant stakeholder engagement.
- Approves major engagement initiatives where required.

---

## Partnerships & External Relations Department

Responsible for:

- Stakeholder mapping.
- Engagement planning.
- Relationship management.
- Coordination of consultations.
- Maintenance of stakeholder records.

---

## Programme Teams

Responsible for:

- Day-to-day stakeholder engagement.
- Community consultation.
- Documentation of engagement outcomes.
- Escalation of issues where necessary.

---

## Communications Department

Responsible for:

- Supporting public communications.
- Preparing stakeholder information materials.
- Coordinating communication strategies.

---

## All Staff

All personnel should:

- Treat stakeholders respectfully.
- Listen actively.
- Respond professionally.
- Protect confidential information.
- Comply with this Policy.

---

# 15. Review

This Policy should be reviewed:

- Every three years.
- Following significant organizational changes.
- After major stakeholder consultations.
- When legal or regulatory requirements change.

---

# Conclusion

Meaningful stakeholder engagement strengthens trust, improves decision-making and enhances the effectiveness of TJF's programmes.

By following this Policy, The Jopetnah Foundation ensures that stakeholder perspectives are valued, respected and integrated into its work, contributing to sustainable, inclusive and accountable development.

---

> **Strong organizations are built not only through effective programmes, but through meaningful relationships with the people they serve and the partners who support them.**
