# Partner Selection Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that the quality of its partnerships directly influences programme effectiveness, organizational reputation and long-term sustainability.

This Partner Selection Framework establishes a structured approach for identifying, assessing and approving organizations that seek to collaborate with TJF.

The Framework ensures that partnerships are formed with organizations that demonstrate alignment with TJF values, adequate capacity, ethical standards and the ability to contribute positively toward shared objectives.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Framework is to:

- Establish consistent partner selection criteria.
- Ensure strategic alignment.
- Reduce partnership risks.
- Promote transparency.
- Support informed decision-making.
- Strengthen partnership quality.

---

# 2. Scope

This Framework applies to:

- Strategic partners.
- Programme implementation partners.
- Technical partners.
- Funding partners.
- Research partners.
- Community-based organizations.
- Service delivery partners.

---

# 3. Partner Selection Principles

Partner selection shall be guided by:

## Strategic Alignment

The potential partner should support TJF's:

- Vision.
- Mission.
- Strategic objectives.
- Programme priorities.

---

## Shared Values

Partners should demonstrate commitment to:

- Integrity.
- Accountability.
- Inclusion.
- Respect.
- Sustainability.

---

## Capability

Partners should possess appropriate:

- Technical expertise.
- Human resources.
- Operational systems.
- Financial capacity.
- Delivery experience.

---

## Risk Awareness

Partners should demonstrate appropriate:

- Governance systems.
- Compliance practices.
- Safeguarding standards.
- Financial controls.

---

## Complementarity

Partners should provide capabilities that strengthen TJF's impact.

---

# 4. Partner Eligibility Criteria

A potential partner should normally demonstrate:

## Legal Status

Evidence of:

- Registration or incorporation.
- Valid operating authorization.
- Compliance with applicable laws.

---

## Organizational Capacity

Assessment areas:

- Leadership structure.
- Staff capability.
- Programme experience.
- Operational systems.

---

## Financial Management

Assessment areas:

- Financial controls.
- Budget management.
- Audit practices.
- Resource management.

---

## Reputation and Integrity

Assessment areas:

- Public reputation.
- Ethical conduct.
- Previous partnership history.
- Compliance record.

---

## Safeguarding Commitment

Partners should demonstrate commitment to:

- Protection of vulnerable groups.
- Prevention of exploitation and abuse.
- Responsible conduct.

---

# 5. Partner Selection Categories

TJF may classify partners as:

## Strategic Partner

Long-term relationship supporting major organizational objectives.

---

## Implementation Partner

Responsible for delivering agreed programme activities.

---

## Technical Partner

Provides specialized expertise or services.

---

## Community Partner

Represents local groups or community structures.

---

## Resource Partner

Provides funding, equipment or other resources.

---

# 6. Partner Assessment Process

The selection process follows:

```
Partner Identification
          │
          ▼
Initial Screening
          │
          ▼
Partner Profile Review
          │
          ▼
Capacity Assessment
          │
          ▼
Risk Review
          │
          ▼
Due Diligence
          │
          ▼
Approval Decision
          │
          ▼
Partnership Agreement
```

---

# 7. Partner Assessment Criteria

Potential partners may be assessed using the following criteria:

| Assessment Area | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Strategic Alignment | 20% |
| Technical Capability | 20% |
| Governance & Integrity | 15% |
| Financial Capacity | 15% |
| Operational Capacity | 15% |
| Safeguarding & Compliance | 10% |
| Innovation & Added Value | 5% |
| Total | 100% |

---

# 8. Assessment Scoring

Each criterion may be scored:

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 5 | Excellent |
| 4 | Strong |
| 3 | Acceptable |
| 2 | Weak |
| 1 | Unsuitable |

Partners should normally achieve a minimum acceptable threshold before approval.

---

# 9. Exclusion Criteria

TJF should not enter partnerships with organizations that:

- Engage in illegal activities.
- Have serious unresolved fraud allegations.
- Violate human rights principles.
- Fail safeguarding requirements.
- Demonstrate serious governance weaknesses.
- Conflict with TJF values.

---

# 10. Partner Approval Process

Approval requirements depend on partnership significance.

## Standard Partnerships

May require:

- Department review.
- Executive approval.
- Completed assessment documentation.

---

## Strategic Partnerships

Require:

- Executive Director approval.
- Board notification or approval where appropriate.

---

## High-Risk Partnerships

Require:

- Enhanced due diligence.
- Senior management review.
- Risk mitigation planning.

---

# 11. Documentation Requirements

Partner files should contain:

- Partner profile.
- Registration documents.
- Assessment checklist.
- Due diligence records.
- Risk assessment.
- Approval record.
- Signed agreement.
- Performance reviews.

---

# 12. Conflict of Interest

Personnel involved in partner selection must disclose:

- Personal relationships.
- Financial interests.
- Other conflicts that may affect impartial decisions.

Conflicts should be managed according to TJF policies.

---

# 13. Review of Partner Selection Decisions

Partner selection decisions should be periodically reviewed to assess:

- Effectiveness of criteria.
- Partnership outcomes.
- Risk management performance.
- Lessons learned.

---

# Conclusion

Effective partnerships begin with careful selection.

Through a transparent and evidence-based selection process, The Jopetnah Foundation ensures that collaborations are built with organizations capable of contributing to sustainable impact while maintaining high standards of integrity and accountability.

---

> **Strong partnerships begin with shared values, proven capability and a common commitment to positive change.**
