# Partnership Due Diligence Policy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) is committed to developing partnerships that uphold integrity, transparency, accountability and responsible development practices.

Before entering into any strategic relationship, TJF shall conduct appropriate due diligence to assess the suitability, credibility, capacity and risks associated with potential partners.

This Partnership Due Diligence Policy establishes the standards and procedures used to evaluate prospective partners and ensure that all collaborations protect TJF’s reputation, resources and mission.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Policy aims to:

- Establish a consistent partnership assessment process.
- Reduce legal, financial and reputational risks.
- Ensure alignment with TJF values.
- Promote responsible partnership decisions.
- Strengthen donor and stakeholder confidence.

---

# 2. Scope

This Policy applies to:

- NGOs.
- Governments.
- Donors.
- Corporations.
- Foundations.
- Universities.
- Research institutions.
- International organizations.
- Strategic alliances.

It applies before:

- Signing agreements.
- Receiving funding.
- Sharing resources.
- Implementing joint programmes.

---

# 3. Due Diligence Principles

TJF due diligence shall follow:

---

## Integrity

Partners must demonstrate ethical conduct.

---

## Transparency

Information provided by partners must be accurate and verifiable.

---

## Risk Awareness

Potential risks must be identified and managed.

---

## Objectivity

Assessments shall be evidence-based and impartial.

---

## Proportionality

The level of review shall match partnership size and risk level.

---

# 4. Due Diligence Process

TJF follows:

```
PARTNER IDENTIFICATION
          ↓
INITIAL SCREENING
          ↓
DOCUMENT REVIEW
          ↓
RISK ASSESSMENT
          ↓
APPROVAL DECISION
          ↓
AGREEMENT DEVELOPMENT
          ↓
ONGOING MONITORING
```

---

# 5. Initial Partner Screening

The first assessment shall review:

- Organization name.
- Legal status.
- Country of operation.
- Purpose and activities.
- Proposed collaboration.
- Organizational reputation.

---

# 6. Legal Verification

TJF shall verify:

## Registration Status

Including:

- Registration certificates.
- Licenses.
- Operating authorization.

---

## Ownership and Governance

Including:

- Leadership structure.
- Board composition.
- Decision-making arrangements.

---

## Regulatory Compliance

Including:

- Legal obligations.
- Required permits.
- Regulatory history.

---

# 7. Financial Due Diligence

Financial assessment may include:

- Financial statements.
- Audit reports.
- Funding sources.
- Financial management systems.
- Previous grant management experience.

Assessment areas:

| Area | Review |
|---|---|
| Financial stability | Ability to meet obligations |
| Controls | Internal financial systems |
| Transparency | Reporting practices |
| Accountability | Use of resources |

---

# 8. Reputation Assessment

TJF shall review:

- Public reputation.
- Previous partnerships.
- Media references.
- Stakeholder feedback.
- Ethical history.

Potential concerns include:

- Fraud allegations.
- Corruption concerns.
- Human rights violations.
- Serious misconduct.

---

# 9. Safeguarding Assessment

Partners should demonstrate commitment to:

- Protection of children.
- Prevention of exploitation and abuse.
- Respect for vulnerable groups.
- Safe programme delivery.

Review areas:

- Safeguarding policies.
- Reporting mechanisms.
- Staff conduct standards.

---

# 10. Anti-Fraud and Anti-Corruption Review

TJF shall assess whether partners maintain:

- Anti-corruption policies.
- Conflict-of-interest procedures.
- Financial controls.
- Reporting mechanisms.

Partners must not engage in:

- Bribery.
- Fraud.
- Misuse of funds.
- Unethical practices.

---

# 11. Environmental and Social Responsibility Review

Where relevant, TJF shall assess:

- Environmental practices.
- Community impact.
- Labour standards.
- Social responsibility.

---

# 12. Risk Classification

Partners shall be categorized as:

## Low Risk

Characteristics:

- Strong reputation.
- Established systems.
- Low compliance concerns.

Approval:

Standard approval process.

---

## Medium Risk

Characteristics:

- Limited history.
- Moderate operational concerns.
- Additional monitoring required.

Approval:

Enhanced review.

---

## High Risk

Characteristics:

- Significant concerns.
- Weak controls.
- Reputation issues.

Approval:

Executive review required.

---

# 13. Partnership Approval Process

The approval pathway:

```
Due Diligence Completed
          ↓
Risk Assessment Conducted
          ↓
Partnership Recommendation Prepared
          ↓
Management Review
          ↓
Approval Decision
          ↓
Agreement Signing
```

---

# 14. Documentation Requirements

Partner files should contain:

- Registration documents.
- Organizational profile.
- Financial information.
- Assessment results.
- Risk evaluation.
- Signed agreements.
- Monitoring records.

---

# 15. Ongoing Partner Monitoring

Due diligence does not end after approval.

TJF shall monitor:

- Performance.
- Compliance.
- Financial accountability.
- Reputation changes.
- Partnership outcomes.

---

# 16. Red Flags Requiring Review

Examples include:

- False information.
- Unclear ownership.
- Financial irregularities.
- Legal violations.
- Negative reputation issues.
- Safeguarding concerns.

---

# 17. Roles and Responsibilities

## Board of Directors

Responsible for:

- Oversight of major strategic partnerships.

---

## Executive Director

Responsible for:

- Final approval of significant partnerships.

---

## Legal and Compliance Department

Responsible for:

- Due diligence standards.
- Compliance review.

---

## Partnerships Department

Responsible for:

- Partner assessment coordination.
- Documentation.

---

## Finance Department

Responsible for:

- Financial evaluation.

# 19. Review and Updating

This Policy shall be reviewed:

- Every three years.
- After significant regulatory changes.
- Following partnership risk incidents.

---

# Conclusion

Effective due diligence protects TJF’s mission, reputation and resources while ensuring that partnerships create meaningful and sustainable impact.

Through responsible screening and continuous monitoring, The Jopetnah Foundation builds trusted relationships that strengthen global development cooperation.
