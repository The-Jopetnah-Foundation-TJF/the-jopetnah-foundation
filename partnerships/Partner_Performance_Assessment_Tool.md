# Partner Performance Assessment Tool

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that effective partnerships require continuous assessment, learning and improvement.

This Partner Performance Assessment Tool provides a structured approach for reviewing partner performance, identifying improvement opportunities and strengthening long-term collaboration.

The assessment supports evidence-based decisions regarding:

- Partnership continuation.
- Partnership improvement.
- Capacity strengthening.
- Risk management.
- Partnership renewal.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this tool is to:

- Evaluate partner performance.
- Measure partnership effectiveness.
- Identify strengths and weaknesses.
- Improve collaboration quality.
- Support accountability.
- Inform management decisions.

---

# 2. Assessment Frequency

Partner assessments should normally be conducted:

- Annually for strategic partnerships.
- At project completion.
- Before partnership renewal.
- When performance concerns arise.

---

# 3. Assessment Process

The assessment process includes:

```
Assessment Planning
        │
        ▼

        │
        ▼
Performance Review
        │
        ▼
Partner Discussion
        │
        ▼
Improvement Actions
        │
        ▼
Follow-up Review
```

# 6. Performance Rating

## Excellent Partner

Score:

90% - 100%

Characteristics:

- Strong performance.
- High trust.
- Strategic value.
- Suitable for expansion.

Recommended action:

Continue and explore deeper collaboration.

---

## Good Partner

Score:

75% - 89%

Characteristics:

- Effective partnership.
- Minor improvement areas.

Recommended action:

Continue with improvement actions where required.

---

## Developing Partner

Score:

60% - 74%

Characteristics:

- Potential exists.
- Capacity improvements required.

Recommended action:

Develop improvement plan.

---

## Unsatisfactory Partner

Score:

Below 60%

Characteristics:

- Significant performance concerns.
- High risk.

Recommended action:

Review partnership continuation.
