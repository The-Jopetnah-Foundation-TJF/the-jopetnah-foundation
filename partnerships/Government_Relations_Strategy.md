# Government Relations Strategy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes governments as important partners in advancing sustainable development, strengthening communities and improving access to essential services.

Effective government engagement enables TJF to align programmes with national priorities, contribute technical expertise, strengthen coordination and improve the sustainability of development interventions.

This Government Relations Strategy establishes TJF’s framework for engaging government institutions at local, national, regional and international levels while maintaining independence, integrity and accountability.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Strategy aims to:

- Establish TJF’s government engagement approach.
- Strengthen collaboration with public institutions.
- Support alignment with development priorities.
- Promote effective coordination.
- Enhance policy dialogue and knowledge sharing.

---

# 2. Scope

This Strategy applies to engagement with:

- National governments.
- Local governments.
- Ministries.
- Government agencies.
- Regulatory authorities.
- Public institutions.
- Development coordination platforms.

It applies to:

- Board Members.
- Executive Management.
- Programme teams.
- Partnership teams.
- Technical specialists.

---

# 3. Government Engagement Principles

TJF government relations shall be guided by:

---

## Respect for Sovereignty

TJF recognizes the authority and responsibilities of governments.

---

## Independence

TJF maintains its organizational independence while collaborating constructively.

---

## Transparency

All government engagements should be open, documented and ethical.

---

## Alignment

Programmes should complement relevant development priorities.

---

## Non-Partisanship

TJF engages governments as institutions and does not participate in partisan political activities.

---

# 4. Objectives of Government Engagement

TJF seeks to:

- Strengthen programme coordination.
- Support national development goals.
- Improve community outcomes.
- Share technical knowledge.
- Build institutional capacity.
- Promote sustainable solutions.
