# Corporate Partnership Strategy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes the private sector as a powerful driver of innovation, economic growth and sustainable development.

Corporate partnerships enable TJF to combine social impact expertise with business resources, technology, networks and innovation capabilities to address community challenges and create measurable transformation.

This Corporate Partnership Strategy establishes TJF’s approach to building meaningful relationships with companies, business associations, investors and corporate foundations through responsible, transparent and mutually beneficial collaboration.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Strategy aims to:

- Establish TJF’s corporate partnership approach.
- Develop sustainable private-sector relationships.
- Promote Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) collaboration.
- Encourage Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) partnerships.
- Mobilize resources for community impact.

---

# 2. Scope

This Strategy applies to engagement with:

- Local companies.
- Multinational corporations.
- Corporate foundations.
- Business associations.
- Private investors.
- Industry networks.

It applies to:

- Board Members.
- Executive Management.
- Partnerships Department.
- Programme Teams.

# 3. Corporate Partnership Principles

TJF corporate partnerships shall follow:

---

## Shared Value Creation

Partnerships should create benefits for:

- Communities.
- Corporate partners.

---

## Sustainability

Partnerships should support long-term impact.

---

## Transparency

All corporate engagements must be clearly documented.

# 15. Risk Management

Potential risks include:

## Reputation Risk

Association with unsuitable companies.

Mitigation:

- Due diligence.
- Ethical screening.

---

## Dependency Risk

Over-reliance on one corporate partner.

Mitigation:

- Diversified partnerships.

---

## Misalignment Risk

Different objectives between partners.

Mitigation:

- Clear agreements.
- Regular reviews.
