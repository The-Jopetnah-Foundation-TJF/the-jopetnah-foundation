# UN and Multilateral Engagement Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes the importance of multilateral cooperation in addressing complex global challenges and advancing sustainable development.

Engagement with United Nations agencies, international financial institutions, regional organizations and global development platforms provides opportunities for knowledge exchange, technical cooperation, programme collaboration and increased impact.

This UN and Multilateral Engagement Framework establishes TJF’s approach to building professional relationships with multilateral institutions while maintaining organizational integrity, independence and accountability.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Guide TJF’s engagement with multilateral institutions.
- Strengthen participation in international development initiatives.
- Improve access to global partnerships and opportunities.
- Promote knowledge exchange and collaboration.
- Support alignment with global development priorities.

---

# 2. Scope

This Framework applies to engagement with:

- United Nations agencies.
- Multilateral development institutions.
- Regional organizations.
- International cooperation platforms.
- Global development networks.

It applies to:

- Board Members.
- Executive Management.
- International Relations Department.
- Programme teams.
- Technical specialists.

# 3. Engagement Principles

TJF’s multilateral engagement shall be guided by:

## Alignment with Global Development Goals

TJF programmes should contribute toward internationally recognized development priorities.

Engagement shall demonstrate:

- Technical competence.
- Institutional credibility.

---

## Transparency

All engagements shall maintain:

- Proper documentation.
- Ethical standards.
- Accountability.

## Collaboration

TJF seeks partnerships based on:

- Shared objectives.
- Complementary expertise.
- Mutual benefit.

## Local Ownership

International partnerships should strengthen local communities and institutions.
