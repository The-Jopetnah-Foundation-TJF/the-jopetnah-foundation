# Partnership Register

## Purpose

This Partnership Register provides a centralized record of all partnerships entered into by The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF).

The register supports:

- Partnership governance.
- Executive oversight.
- Risk management.
- Performance monitoring.
- Audit readiness.
- Timely renewals and reporting.

The register should be maintained by the Partnerships & External Relations Department and updated whenever a partnership is initiated, amended, renewed, suspended or concluded.

# Conclusion

Maintaining a comprehensive and up-to-date Partnership Register enables TJF to manage relationships strategically, monitor performance consistently and demonstrate accountability to partners, donors and governing bodies.

The register supports informed decision-making and ensures that every partnership contributes effectively to the Foundation's mission and long-term impact.

> **Strong partnerships are sustained through clear records, regular review and a shared commitment to measurable results.**
