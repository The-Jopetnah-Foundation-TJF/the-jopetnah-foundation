# Academic Partnership Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes the value of partnerships with academic institutions in advancing research, innovation, evidence-based programming and capacity development.

This Framework establishes the principles, governance arrangements and operational procedures for collaboration with universities, colleges, research institutes and think tanks, ensuring that academic partnerships support TJF's mission while maintaining high standards of ethics, quality and accountability.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Promote evidence-based decision-making.
- Strengthen research collaboration.
- Support innovation and knowledge exchange.
- Facilitate student learning and professional development.
- Improve programme design through research findings.
- Build long-term relationships with academic institutions.

---

# 2. Scope

This Framework applies to partnerships with:

- Universities.
- Colleges.
- Technical and vocational institutions.
- Research institutes.
- Think tanks.
- Academic networks.
- Professional training institutions.

---

# 3. Guiding Principles

Academic partnerships should be:

- Ethical.
- Independent.
- Transparent.
- Inclusive.
- Evidence-based.
- Mutually beneficial.
- Respectful of academic freedom.
- Focused on societal impact.

---

# 4. Objectives

Academic partnerships may support:

- Joint research.
- Programme evaluation.
- Policy analysis.
- Innovation.
- Curriculum development.
- Capacity building.
- Student internships and placements.
- Knowledge dissemination.

---

# 5. Areas of Collaboration

Potential areas include:

## Research

- Baseline and endline studies.
- Operational research.
- Impact evaluations.
- Applied research.
- Action research.

---

## Education and Training

- Student internships.
- Graduate placements.
- Professional development.
- Guest lectures.
- Joint workshops.

---

## Knowledge Exchange

- Conferences.
- Publications.
- Seminars.
- Learning forums.
- Technical exchanges.

---

## Innovation

- Pilot projects.
- Technology development.
- Social innovation.
- Digital transformation.
- Evidence generation.

---

# 6. Ethics

All research involving people, personal data or sensitive information should:

- Respect human dignity.
- Obtain informed consent where required.
- Protect confidentiality.
- Minimize risk to participants.
- Comply with applicable legal and ethical standards.

Where required, research should receive approval from an appropriately constituted ethics review body before commencement.

---

# 7. Data Management

Research data should be:

- Collected lawfully.
- Stored securely.
- Used only for approved purposes.
- Shared in accordance with agreements.
- Retained and disposed of in line with TJF's Records Management and Data Protection policies.

---

# 8. Intellectual Property

Partnership agreements should clarify:

- Ownership of research outputs.
- Licensing arrangements.
- Use of datasets.
- Copyright.
- Patents (where applicable).
- Attribution requirements.

Jointly developed materials should acknowledge the contributions of all parties unless otherwise agreed.

---

# 9. Publications

Publications arising from partnerships should:

- Meet recognized academic and professional standards.
- Accurately acknowledge contributors and funding sources.
- Respect confidentiality obligations.
- Undergo appropriate quality assurance before release.

Where publication approval processes are agreed in partnership documents, they should be followed without restricting legitimate academic independence.

---

# 10. Student Engagement

Student internships and placements should include:

- Clear learning objectives.
- Defined supervision arrangements.
- Appropriate safeguarding measures.
- Health and safety orientation.
- Performance feedback.

Students should comply with TJF's policies during their placement.

---

# 11. Governance

Each academic partnership should define:

- Objectives.
- Roles and responsibilities.
- Governance structure.
- Communication arrangements.
- Reporting requirements.
- Financial responsibilities (where applicable).
- Monitoring and evaluation processes.

Formal partnerships should normally be documented through an MoU or other appropriate agreement.

---

# 12. Monitoring and Evaluation

Academic partnerships may be assessed using indicators such as:

| Indicator | Measurement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Joint research projects | Number |
| Publications produced | Number |
| Students hosted | Number |
| Training activities conducted | Number |
| Research findings applied in programmes | Number |
| Partner satisfaction | Survey results |

Evaluation findings should inform future collaboration.

---

# 13. Roles and Responsibilities

## Board of Trustees

- Provides oversight for major strategic academic collaborations.

---

## Executive Director

- Approves significant academic partnerships.
- Represents TJF in high-level institutional engagements.

---

## Partnerships & External Relations Department

Responsible for:

- Identifying academic partnership opportunities.
- Coordinating agreements.
- Managing institutional relationships.
- Monitoring partnership performance.

---

## Programme Teams

Responsible for:

- Identifying research priorities.
- Supervising collaborative activities.
- Applying research findings to programme improvement.

---

## Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning (MEAL) Team

Responsible for:

- Supporting research design.
- Ensuring methodological quality.
- Facilitating data analysis where appropriate.
- Promoting organizational learning.

---

## Communications Department

Responsible for:

- Coordinating publication approvals.
- Supporting dissemination activities.
- Managing public communications relating to research outputs.

---

# 14. Review

This Framework should be reviewed:

- Every three years.
- Following major research collaborations.
- When relevant legal or ethical standards change.
- When organizational priorities evolve.

---

# Conclusion

Academic partnerships enhance TJF's ability to generate evidence, improve programme quality and contribute to sustainable development through research, innovation and learning.

By applying this Framework, The Jopetnah Foundation ensures that collaborations with academic institutions are ethical, well-governed and focused on delivering measurable value for communities, researchers and development partners.

---

> **Knowledge becomes transformative when research, practice and community experience come together to solve real-world challenges.**
