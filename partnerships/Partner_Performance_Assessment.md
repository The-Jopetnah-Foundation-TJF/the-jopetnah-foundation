# Partner Performance Assessment

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) is committed to maintaining high-quality partnerships that deliver measurable value to communities, donors and other stakeholders.

This Partner Performance Assessment provides a structured methodology for evaluating the effectiveness, accountability and strategic value of partnerships throughout their lifecycle. Assessment findings support continuous improvement and informed decisions regarding renewal, expansion or conclusion of partnership arrangements.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Assessment Framework aims to:

- Evaluate partner performance objectively.
- Strengthen accountability.
- Encourage continuous improvement.
- Identify partnership risks.
- Inform renewal and future collaboration decisions.
- Capture lessons learned and good practices.

---

# 2. Scope

This Assessment applies to:

- Strategic partnerships.
- Programme partnerships.
- Donor-funded collaborations.
- Research partnerships.
- Corporate partnerships.
- Government collaborations.
- Academic partnerships.
- Consortium arrangements.

Assessments may be conducted annually, at project completion or before partnership renewal.

---

# 3. Assessment Rating Scale

| Rating | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| 5 | Excellent – Consistently exceeds expectations |
| 4 | Very Good – Meets expectations with minor improvements identified |
| 3 | Satisfactory – Generally meets expectations |
| 2 | Needs Improvement – Significant gaps identified |
| 1 | Unsatisfactory – Performance does not meet expectations |
