# Due Diligence Checklist

## Executive Summary

This Due Diligence Checklist provides a structured process for evaluating prospective partners before The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) enters into a formal partnership or collaborative arrangement.

The checklist supports informed decision-making by identifying legal, financial, operational, ethical and reputational risks while confirming alignment with TJF's mission and governance standards.

---

# Instructions

Complete this checklist before recommending approval of a new partnership.

For each item, indicate:

- ☐ Yes
- ☐ No
- ☐ Not Applicable (N/A)

Provide comments or supporting evidence where appropriate.
