# NGO Collaboration Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that collaboration among civil society organizations is essential for addressing complex social, economic and environmental challenges.

Partnerships with local NGOs, international NGOs, community-based organizations and humanitarian actors enable organizations to combine expertise, share resources, strengthen coordination and deliver greater impact.

This NGO Collaboration Framework establishes TJF’s principles, processes and standards for developing, managing and sustaining effective partnerships with civil society organizations.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Guide TJF collaboration with NGOs and civil society organizations.
- Strengthen collective development impact.
- Promote knowledge sharing.
- Improve programme coordination.
- Encourage sustainable partnerships.

---

# 2. Scope

This Framework applies to collaboration with:

- Local NGOs.
- International NGOs.
- Community-based organizations.
- Faith-based organizations.
- Humanitarian organizations.
- Civil society networks.

It applies to:

- Partnership teams.
- Programme teams.
- Technical departments.
- Field personnel.

---

# 3. NGO Collaboration Principles

TJF collaboration shall be guided by:

---

## Shared Mission

Partners should demonstrate alignment with TJF’s vision and values.

---

## Complementarity

Partnerships should combine different strengths and expertise.

---

## Respect and Equality

All partners should be valued as contributors.

---

## Accountability

Partners should fulfill agreed responsibilities.

---

## Community-Centered Action

Collaboration should prioritize community needs and participation.

---

## Transparency

Partnership processes should be open and documented.

---

# 7. NGO Partnership Development Process

TJF follows:

```
IDENTIFY COLLABORATION OPPORTUNITY
              ↓
PARTNER ASSESSMENT
              ↓
DISCUSS SHARED OBJECTIVES
              ↓
DESIGN COLLABORATION MODEL
              ↓
FORMALIZE AGREEMENT
              ↓
IMPLEMENT ACTIVITIES
              ↓
MONITOR PERFORMANCE
              ↓
DOCUMENT LEARNING
```

# 8. NGO Partner Assessment

Potential partners should demonstrate:

- Legal registration.
- Relevant experience.
- Good governance.
- Technical capacity.
- Financial accountability.
- Ethical practices.

---

# 9. Partnership Due Diligence

TJF shall assess:

## Organizational Capacity

Including:

- Leadership structure.
- Programme experience.
- Operational systems.

---

## Financial Management

Including:

- Financial controls.
- Audit history.
- Resource management capacity.

---

## Safeguarding Standards

Including:

- Protection policies.
- Child safeguarding measures.
- Prevention of exploitation and abuse.

---

## Reputation and Integrity

Including:

- Public reputation.
- Previous partnerships.
- Compliance record.

---

# 10. Collaboration Agreements

NGO partnerships may be formalized through:

- Memoranda of Understanding.
- Partnership agreements.
- Consortium agreements.
- Project cooperation agreements.

Agreements should define:

- Objectives.
- Roles.
- Resources.
- Timelines.
- Reporting requirements.
- Accountability mechanisms.

---

# 11. Partnership Coordination

TJF shall promote:

- Regular coordination meetings.
- Joint planning.
- Shared reporting.
- Information exchange.
- Problem-solving mechanisms.

# 12. Programme Quality Standards

Collaborating organizations should ensure:

- Quality service delivery.
- Ethical implementation.

# 13. Knowledge Sharing and Learning

Partners may share:

# 14. NGO Partnership Risk Management

Potential risks include:

## Operational Risks

Failure to deliver agreed activities.

Mitigation:

- Clear agreements.
- Performance monitoring.

---

## Financial Risks

Poor resource management.

Mitigation:

- Financial reviews.
- Accountability requirements.

---

## Reputation Risks

Association with unsuitable organizations.

Mitigation:

- Due diligence.
- Ethical screening.
