# Donor Engagement Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that sustainable donor relationships are essential for expanding programmes, strengthening communities and achieving measurable development impact.

Effective donor engagement requires trust, transparency, accountability, professional communication and demonstrated results.

This Donor Engagement Framework establishes TJF’s approach to identifying, engaging, managing and retaining donors and funding partners who share TJF’s commitment to sustainable transformation.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Establish TJF’s donor engagement standards.
- Strengthen fundraising relationships.
- Improve donor communication.
- Increase resource mobilization opportunities.
- Promote transparency and accountability.

---

# 2. Scope

This Framework applies to:

- Institutional donors.
- Foundations.
- Philanthropic organizations.
- Government funding agencies.
- Corporate donors.
- Development partners.
- Individual major donors.

It applies to:

- Board Members.
- Executive Management.
- Fundraising teams.
- Programme teams.
- Finance teams.

---

# 3. Donor Engagement Principles

TJF donor relationships are guided by:

---

## Trust

Building relationships based on honesty and reliability.

---

## Transparency

Providing accurate information about:

- Funding use.
- Programme results.
- Challenges.
- Achievements.

---

## Accountability

Demonstrating responsible stewardship of resources.

---

## Alignment

Seeking donors whose priorities align with TJF’s mission.

---

## Partnership

Viewing donors as strategic partners rather than only funding sources.

# 5. Donor Identification Process

Potential donors shall be identified through:

- Research.
- Funding databases.
- Partnership networks.
- Conferences.
- Development forums.
- Strategic introductions.

Assessment should consider:

- Funding priorities.
- Geographic focus.
- Grant size.
- Eligibility requirements.

# 7. Donor Communication Strategy

TJF shall maintain regular communication through:

- Progress updates.
- Impact reports.
- Newsletters.
- Success stories.
- Programme visits.
- Strategic meetings.

Communication should demonstrate:

- Results.
- Impact.
- Responsible resource management.

---

# 8. Funding Proposal Development

All donor proposals should include:

## Executive Summary

Overview of the proposed initiative.

---

## Background and Need

Description of:

- Community challenge.
- Evidence.
- Context.

---

## Programme Description

Including:

- Objectives.
- Activities.
- Expected results.

---

## Monitoring and Evaluation Plan

Including:

- Indicators.
- Measurement approaches.
- Reporting methods.

---

## Budget and Financial Plan

Including:

- Cost estimates.
- Resource requirements.
- Financial controls.

---

## Sustainability Strategy

Explaining how benefits will continue.

---

# 9. Donor Due Diligence

Before accepting funding, TJF shall assess:

- Donor reputation.
- Funding conditions.
- Ethical compatibility.
- Compliance requirements.
- Potential conflicts of interest.

TJF reserves the right to decline funding inconsistent with organizational values.

---

# 10. Grant Management and Stewardship

TJF shall ensure:

- Proper utilization of funds.
- Compliance with agreements.
- Accurate financial reporting.
- Programme monitoring.
- Timely communication.

---

## Narrative Reports

---

## Financial Reports

# 12. Donor Relationship Management

TJF shall strengthen donor relationships through:

- Regular engagement.
- Appreciation and recognition.
- Transparency.
- Joint learning.
- Strategic discussions.

---

# 13. Donor Retention Strategy

TJF shall retain donors by:

- Demonstrating impact.
- Maintaining trust.
- Providing quality reporting.
- Sharing success stories.
- Creating long-term partnerships.

---

# 14. Ethical Fundraising Standards

TJF fundraising shall:

- Respect donor intentions.
- Avoid misleading information.
- Protect beneficiary dignity.
- Maintain transparency.
- Follow legal requirements.
