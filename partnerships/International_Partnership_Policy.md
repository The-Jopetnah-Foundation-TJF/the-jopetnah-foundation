# International Partnership Policy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes strategic partnerships as essential for achieving sustainable development impact.

Through collaboration with international organizations, governments, institutions, corporations, foundations and civil society actors, TJF seeks to combine resources, expertise, innovation and networks to address complex global challenges.

This International Partnership Policy establishes TJF’s principles, standards and procedures for developing, managing and sustaining partnerships that advance the Foundation’s mission and strategic objectives.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Policy aims to:

- Establish TJF’s partnership principles.
- Guide partnership development and management.
- Ensure ethical and transparent collaboration.
- Strengthen institutional relationships.
- Support sustainable impact through cooperation.

---

# 2. Scope

This Policy applies to:

- International partnerships.
- Strategic alliances.
- Programme collaborations.
- Funding relationships.
- Technical cooperation agreements.
- Knowledge-sharing partnerships.

It applies to:

- Board Members.
- Executive Management.
- Staff.
- Consultants.
- Partner organizations.

---

# 3. Definition of Partnership

A partnership is a structured relationship between TJF and another organization where parties collaborate toward shared objectives through:

- Shared resources.
- Complementary expertise.
- Joint activities.
- Mutual accountability.

---

# 4. Partnership Principles

TJF partnerships shall be guided by:

---

## Shared Vision

Partners should have aligned objectives and values.

---

## Mutual Respect

All partners should recognize each other's contributions.

---

## Transparency

Partnership decisions and responsibilities should be clear.

---

## Accountability

Partners must fulfill agreed commitments.

---

## Sustainability

Partnerships should create lasting value.

---

## Integrity

Partnerships must comply with ethical standards.

---

## Funding Partnerships

Relationships with:

## Knowledge Partnerships

- Research.
- Training.
- Innovation.

# 7. Partnership Development Process

TJF follows:

```
IDENTIFY OPPORTUNITY
        ↓
PARTNER ASSESSMENT
        ↓
DISCUSSION AND NEGOTIATION
        ↓
DUE DILIGENCE
        ↓
AGREEMENT DEVELOPMENT
        ↓
APPROVAL
        ↓
IMPLEMENTATION

MONITORING AND REVIEW
```

---

# 8. Partner Selection Criteria

Potential partners should demonstrate:

- Alignment with TJF mission.
- Strong reputation.
- Ethical practices.
- Technical capability.
- Financial credibility.
- Commitment to impact.

---

# 9. Partnership Due Diligence

Before engagement, TJF shall assess:

## Organizational Background

Including:

- Registration status.
- Governance structure.
- Leadership information.

---

## Financial Capacity

Including:

- Financial stability.
- Funding sources.
- Audit records.

---

## Compliance Standards

Including:

- Legal compliance.
- Anti-corruption practices.
- Safeguarding policies.

---

## Reputation Assessment

Including:

- Public reputation.
- Previous partnerships.
- Ethical history.

---

# 10. Partnership Agreements

Formal partnerships should be documented through:

- Memoranda of Understanding (MOUs).
- Partnership Agreements.
- Grant Agreements.
- Cooperation Agreements.

Agreements should define:

- Objectives.
- Roles.
- Responsibilities.
- Resources.
- Reporting requirements.
- Duration.
- Termination conditions.

---

# 11. Partnership Management

TJF shall maintain partnerships through:

- Regular communication.
- Joint planning.
- Progress reviews.
- Performance monitoring.
- Relationship management.

---

# 12. Partnership Monitoring

Partnership performance may be assessed through:

- Achievement of objectives.
- Programme results.
- Financial compliance.
- Stakeholder satisfaction.
- Sustainability outcomes.

---

# 13. Partnership Risk Management

Potential risks include:

- Reputation risks.
- Financial risks.
- Compliance risks.
- Operational risks.

TJF shall manage risks through:

- Due diligence.
- Monitoring.
- Clear agreements.
- Corrective actions.

---

- Partnership development.

Responsible for:

- Implementing partnership activities.
- Reporting results.
