# Partnerships & Stakeholder Management

## Introduction

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that sustainable development requires collaboration, shared responsibility and collective action.

No single organization can address complex social, economic and environmental challenges alone.

Through strategic partnerships, TJF works with governments, communities, civil society organizations, private sector actors, academic institutions, donors and international development partners to expand impact and strengthen sustainability.

This folder contains the Foundation's partnership management frameworks, procedures and tools.

---

# Purpose

The Partnerships & Stakeholder Management system exists to:

- Establish consistent partnership practices.
- Strengthen collaboration with strategic stakeholders.
- Ensure responsible partner selection.
- Manage partnership risks.
- Improve accountability.
- Promote transparency.
- Increase programme impact.
- Support long-term institutional relationships.

---

# Partnership Philosophy

TJF believes effective partnerships are built on:

## Shared Purpose

Partners should have aligned objectives and complementary strengths.

---

## Mutual Value

Successful partnerships create benefits for all participating organizations and communities.

---

## Trust and Transparency

Relationships should be based on honesty, openness and accountability.

---

## Respect

Partners should respect each other's expertise, culture and responsibilities.

---

## Sustainability

Partnerships should contribute to long-term development outcomes beyond individual projects.

# Partnership Management Principles

## Strategic Alignment

Partnerships should support TJF's:

- Vision.
- Mission.
- Strategic Plan.
- Programme priorities.

---

## Complementarity

Partners should contribute:

- Expertise.
- Resources.
- Networks.
- Technology.
- Knowledge.

---

## Accountability

Each partnership should clearly define:

- Roles.
- Responsibilities.
- Deliverables.
- Reporting requirements.
- Decision-making processes.

---

## Risk Management

Partnership decisions should consider:

- Reputation risk.
- Financial risk.
- Operational risk.
- Compliance risk.
- Safeguarding risk.

---

# Partnership Governance

## Board of Trustees

Provides oversight for:

- Major strategic partnerships.
- High-risk collaborations.
- Institutional relationships.

---

## Executive Director

Responsible for:

- Approving strategic partnerships.
- Representing TJF at senior partnership levels.
- Ensuring alignment with organizational strategy.

---

## Partnerships Department

Responsible for:

- Partnership development.
- Relationship management.
- Documentation.
- Performance monitoring.
- Stakeholder coordination.

---

## Programme Teams

Responsible for:

- Technical implementation.
- Partner coordination.
- Reporting results.

---

# Partnership Documentation

Partnership records should include:

- Partner profile.
- Due diligence assessment.
- Partnership agreement.
- Work plans.
- Performance reviews.
- Meeting records.
- Risk assessments.
- Lessons learned.

---

# Partnership Performance Management

Partnerships should be assessed based on:

- Achievement of agreed objectives.
- Quality of collaboration.
- Communication effectiveness.
- Compliance with agreements.
- Resource utilization.
- Sustainability potential.

---

# Ethical Requirements

All partnerships should comply with:

- TJF Code of Conduct.
- Anti-fraud requirements.
- Safeguarding standards.
- Data protection requirements.
- Applicable laws and regulations.

---

# Partnership Learning

TJF will continuously improve partnership practices through:

- Partner feedback.
- Performance reviews.
- Lessons learned.
- Relationship assessments.
- Stakeholder consultations.
