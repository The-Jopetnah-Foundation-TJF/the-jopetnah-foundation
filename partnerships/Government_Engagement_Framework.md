# Government Engagement Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that constructive engagement with government institutions is essential for achieving sustainable development outcomes. Government partnerships enable policy dialogue, coordinated programme implementation, regulatory compliance and the scaling of successful initiatives.

This Framework establishes principles and procedures for engaging with national and local government institutions in a transparent, accountable and non-partisan manner while preserving TJF's independence and integrity.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Promote effective collaboration with government institutions.
- Ensure compliance with applicable laws and regulations.
- Support evidence-informed public policy.
- Strengthen coordination in programme implementation.
- Maintain transparency, accountability and institutional independence.
- Foster long-term relationships that contribute to sustainable development.

---

# 2. Scope

This Framework applies to engagement with:

- National ministries.
- Government departments and agencies.
- Local governments.
- Regulatory authorities.
- Public commissions.
- State-owned institutions.
- Public universities and research bodies.
- Intergovernmental forums where government entities participate.

---

# 3. Guiding Principles

Government engagement shall be:

- Lawful.
- Non-partisan.
- Transparent.
- Respectful.
- Evidence-based.
- Inclusive.
- Accountable.
- Focused on the public interest.

---

# 4. Objectives of Government Engagement

TJF may engage government institutions to:

- Support programme implementation.
- Contribute technical expertise.
- Participate in policy consultations.
- Share research and evidence.
- Strengthen public service delivery.
- Coordinate humanitarian or development responses.
- Advocate for sustainable and inclusive policies.

---

# 5. Forms of Engagement

Appropriate forms of engagement include:

- Formal meetings.
- Technical working groups.
- Public consultations.
- Joint planning sessions.
- Workshops and conferences.
- Capacity-building initiatives.
- Research collaborations.
- Memoranda of Understanding (MoUs).
- Participation in government-led committees, where appropriate.

---

# 6. Non-Partisanship

TJF is politically neutral and does not:

- Endorse political parties or candidates.
- Participate in partisan campaigning.
- Use organizational resources for political purposes.

Engagement with government institutions shall be based on policy, development outcomes and the public interest rather than political affiliation.

---

# 7. Representation

Only authorized personnel may represent TJF in official government engagements.

Representatives should:

- Be adequately briefed.
- Act within delegated authority.
- Communicate approved organizational positions.
- Maintain professional conduct.

Significant engagements should be documented.

---

# 8. Regulatory Compliance

TJF shall:

- Maintain required registrations and licenses.
- Comply with reporting obligations.
- Cooperate with lawful regulatory inspections.
- Observe procurement, labour, tax and other applicable legal requirements.

Compliance responsibilities should be clearly assigned and monitored.

---

# 9. Information Sharing

Information shared with government institutions should be:

- Accurate.
- Timely.
- Relevant.
- Lawfully disclosed.
- Consistent with confidentiality obligations.

Sensitive or personal information shall only be disclosed where authorized and in accordance with applicable law and TJF's Data Protection and Privacy Policy.

---

# 10. Advocacy and Policy Dialogue

TJF may:

- Submit policy recommendations.
- Participate in consultations.
- Present research findings.
- Contribute technical expertise.
- Facilitate dialogue among stakeholders.

Advocacy should remain evidence-based, constructive and aligned with TJF's mission.

---

# 11. Ethics and Integrity

All engagement with public officials shall comply with TJF's:

- Code of Conduct.
- Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption Policy.
- Conflict of Interest Policy.
- Gifts and Hospitality Policy.

No employee, volunteer or representative shall offer or accept improper inducements.

---

# 12. Risk Management

Potential risks include:

- Reputational risk.
- Regulatory non-compliance.
- Political misperception.
- Conflicts of interest.
- Confidentiality breaches.

Risk assessments should be conducted for significant government partnerships or engagements.

---

# 13. Documentation

Records should be maintained for:

- Official correspondence.
- Meeting agendas and minutes.
- Signed agreements.
- Consultation submissions.
- Regulatory filings.
- Reports to government institutions.
- Joint activity plans.

Records shall be managed in accordance with the Records Management Policy.

---

# 14. Monitoring and Evaluation

Government engagement may be monitored using indicators such as:

| Indicator | Measurement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Government meetings held | Number |
| Joint initiatives implemented | Number |
| Policy consultations attended | Number |
| Regulatory reports submitted on time | Percentage |
| MoUs with government entities | Number |
| Government stakeholder satisfaction | Survey / Feedback |

Lessons learned should inform future engagement strategies.

---

# 15. Roles and Responsibilities

## Board of Trustees

- Provides oversight for significant strategic government relationships.
- Ensures alignment with TJF's mission and governance standards.

---

## Executive Director

- Leads high-level government engagement.
- Approves major institutional relationships and official representations.

---

## Partnerships & External Relations Department

Responsible for:

- Coordinating government engagement.
- Maintaining stakeholder relationships.
- Tracking agreements and commitments.
- Supporting policy dialogue.

---

## Programme Teams

Responsible for:

- Technical collaboration.
- Programme coordination.
- Reporting on joint activities.
- Escalating issues as appropriate.

---

## Communications Department

Responsible for:

- Coordinating public communications related to government engagement.
- Ensuring consistency with approved messaging.

---

## All Staff

All personnel should:

- Respect public institutions.
- Comply with this Framework.
- Protect confidential information.
- Avoid partisan conduct while representing TJF.

---

# 16. Review

This Framework should be reviewed:

- Every three years.
- Following significant legislative or regulatory changes.
- After major government partnership evaluations.
- When organizational governance arrangements change.

---

# Conclusion

Constructive engagement with government institutions strengthens the Foundation's ability to contribute to sustainable development, improve public policy and expand the reach of its programmes.

By applying this Framework, The Jopetnah Foundation ensures that all government relationships are conducted professionally, transparently and in a manner that safeguards its independence, credibility and public trust.

---

> **Effective government engagement is built on integrity, mutual respect and a shared commitment to improving the lives of the communities we serve.**
