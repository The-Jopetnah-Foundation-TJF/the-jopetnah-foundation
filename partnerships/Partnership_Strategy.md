# Partnership Strategy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes partnerships as a fundamental driver of sustainable development and institutional growth.

This Partnership Strategy establishes the Foundation's approach to identifying, developing, managing and sustaining strategic relationships that advance TJF's mission of creating meaningful and lasting impact.

The Strategy provides a framework for collaboration with governments, development partners, communities, private sector organizations, academic institutions and other stakeholders.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Strategy is to:

- Define TJF's partnership approach.
- Establish partnership priorities.
- Guide partnership development.
- Strengthen stakeholder relationships.
- Improve collaboration effectiveness.
- Promote responsible partnership practices.
- Support sustainable impact.

---

# 2. Partnership Vision

## Vision Statement

To build trusted, innovative and impactful partnerships that accelerate sustainable development and empower communities.

---

# 3. Partnership Mission

TJF seeks to create partnerships that combine resources, expertise, networks and innovation to address complex social, economic and environmental challenges.

---

# 4. Strategic Partnership Objectives

The Foundation's partnership objectives are to:

## 4.1 Expand Impact

Work with complementary organizations to reach more communities and increase programme effectiveness.

---

## 4.2 Strengthen Technical Capacity

Collaborate with organizations that provide:

- Technical expertise.
- Research capacity.
- Innovation.
- Specialized knowledge.

---

## 4.3 Mobilize Resources

Develop relationships that support:

- Programme financing.
- Technical assistance.
- Infrastructure development.
- Community investments.

---

## 4.4 Influence Sustainable Development

Participate in networks and collaborations that contribute to:

- Policy dialogue.
- Sector improvement.
- Knowledge sharing.
- Community empowerment.

---

# 5. Partnership Principles

All TJF partnerships are guided by:

## Shared Values

Partners should demonstrate commitment to:

- Integrity.
- Accountability.
- Inclusion.
- Respect.
- Sustainable development.

---

## Mutual Benefit

Partnerships should create value for:

- Communities.
- TJF.
- Partner organizations.
- Wider stakeholders.

---

## Complementary Strengths

Partnerships should combine different capabilities, including:

- Knowledge.
- Networks.
- Resources.
- Technology.
- Experience.

---

## Transparency

Partners should maintain:

- Clear communication.
- Defined expectations.
- Open reporting.
- Honest collaboration.

---

## Sustainability

Partnerships should aim to create lasting value beyond individual projects.

---

Potential risks include:

- Misalignment of values.
- Reputation risks.
- Financial risks.
- Compliance failures.
- Poor performance.
- Safeguarding concerns.

All significant partnerships should undergo appropriate assessment.

- Sustainability potential.
