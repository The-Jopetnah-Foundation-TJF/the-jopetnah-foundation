## 🎯 Mission

To advance **education, health, economic opportunity, and environmental resilience** in underserved communities through integrated, locally-led programs.

**Vision:** A generation of communities that are educated, healthy, economically self-reliant, and environmentally sustainable.

**Values:** Integrity · Community ownership · Transparency · Sustainability · Inclusion

## 💡 The Problem

In the communities Jopetnah serves, a child out of school, a family without healthcare, and a household without income are rarely separate problems — they're the same problem, seen from different angles. Most charities specialize in one slice of it. **Jopetnah is built to address the whole picture, for the whole family.**

## 🧩 Our Model

Jopetnah runs four integrated program pillars rather than four disconnected charities. This isn't just a delivery choice — it's a funding advantage: a single gift can be shown to cover a child's school fees, a parent's health check-up, **and** seed capital for a small business, all in one family.

```
            ┌───────────────────────────┐
            │   ONE FAMILY, ONE GIFT    │
            └─────────────┬─────────────┘
     ┌───────────┬─────────┴─────────┬───────────┐
     ▼           ▼                   ▼           ▼
 Education     Health           Livelihoods   Environment
```

## 🏛️ Governance & Accountability

*(Structure below reflects current planning intent — final legal form to be confirmed with counsel.)*

- Independent **Board of Trustees** (7–9 members) for fiduciary oversight
- **Executive Director** + Program Directors for each of the four pillars
- **Advisory Council** of community, diaspora, and sector leaders
- Commitment to published annual impact reports and audited financials once operational
