# Funding Priorities (2026–2030)

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) is committed to creating sustainable, inclusive and measurable social impact through strategic investments that empower communities and strengthen institutions.

This document outlines the Foundation's priority funding areas for the period 2026–2030 and serves as a guide for donors, development partners, governments, corporations and philanthropic organizations seeking to align their support with the Foundation's strategic direction.

---

# Strategic Goal

Mobilize sustainable financial, technical and partnership resources to expand the Foundation's capacity to deliver transformative programmes while maintaining high standards of governance, transparency and accountability.

---

# Strategic Investment Principles

Funding partnerships should contribute to one or more of the following principles:

- Sustainable community development
- Long-term institutional capacity
- Innovation and technology
- Inclusion and equity
- Environmental responsibility
- Evidence-based programming
- Accountability and measurable results
- Community ownership

---

# Priority Area 1 – Education and Skills Development

## Objective

Increase access to quality education and lifelong learning opportunities.

### Indicative Areas for Investment

- Scholarships and bursaries
- School improvement initiatives
- Teacher capacity development
- Digital learning infrastructure
- STEM education
- Technical and vocational education
- Youth leadership programmes
- Adult literacy
- Career guidance and mentorship

### Expected Outcomes

- Improved educational access
- Enhanced learning outcomes
- Increased youth employability
- Stronger educational institutions

---

# Priority Area 2 – Health and Wellbeing

## Objective

Promote healthier communities through preventive, accessible and community-centred healthcare initiatives.

### Indicative Areas for Investment

- Community health outreach
- Maternal and child health
- Mental health awareness
- Nutrition programmes
- Water, sanitation and hygiene (WASH)
- Disease prevention campaigns
- Health worker capacity building
- Medical outreach services

### Expected Outcomes

- Improved community health indicators
- Increased health awareness
- Reduced preventable illness
- Stronger local health systems

---

# Priority Area 3 – Sustainable Livelihoods

## Objective

Improve household resilience through economic empowerment and entrepreneurship.

### Indicative Areas for Investment

- Entrepreneurship development
- Vocational training
- Youth employment
- Women's economic empowerment
- Financial literacy
- Village savings and loan associations
- Agribusiness development
- Small enterprise support

### Expected Outcomes

- Increased household income
- Greater financial inclusion
- Improved employment opportunities
- Stronger local enterprises

---

# Priority Area 4 – Environmental Sustainability and Climate Resilience

## Objective

Strengthen community resilience to environmental and climate-related challenges.

### Indicative Areas for Investment

- Tree planting and restoration
- Climate-smart agriculture
- Renewable energy initiatives
- Waste management
- Environmental education
- Biodiversity conservation
- Community resilience planning

### Expected Outcomes

- Improved environmental stewardship
- Increased climate resilience

- Greener communities

## Objective

Build strong systems that improve programme quality, accountability and organizational sustainability.

### Indicative Areas for Investment

- Policy implementation

- Digital transformation
- Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning (MEL)
- Financial management systems
- Cybersecurity
- Risk management

### Expected Outcomes

- Stronger institutional capacity
- Better programme oversight
- Improved compliance
- Enhanced organizational resilience

# Priority Area 6 – Innovation, Research and Digital Transformation

### Indicative Areas for Investment

- Research studies
- Data systems
- Geographic Information Systems (GIS)
- Artificial Intelligence (AI) for development

# Cross-Cutting Priorities

The Foundation integrates the following themes across all programmes:

- Gender equality
- Disability inclusion
- Youth empowerment
- Child safeguarding
- Human rights
- Environmental sustainability
- Good governance
- Community participation

---

# Geographic Focus

The Foundation's initial priority is Uganda, with opportunities to support regional initiatives where they align with the Foundation's mission, partnerships and organizational capacity.

Expansion will be guided by strategic planning, available resources and demonstrated impact.

---

# Partnership Opportunities

The Foundation welcomes support through:

- Programme grants
- Core institutional funding
- Technical assistance
- Capacity building
- Research collaborations
- Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR)
- In-kind contributions
- Volunteer expertise
- Innovation partnerships

---

# Resource Mobilization Priorities

To strengthen long-term sustainability, the Foundation aims to diversify its funding portfolio by increasing support from:

- Individual donors
- Family foundations
- Corporate partners
- Philanthropic trusts
- Bilateral agencies
- Multilateral organizations
- Government partnerships
- Academic institutions
- International NGOs

No single funding source should compromise the Foundation's independence or strategic direction.

---

# Measuring Success

Progress against these priorities will be monitored using indicators such as:

- Funds mobilized
- Number of strategic partnerships
- Communities reached
- Beneficiaries served
- Programme outcomes achieved
- Institutional capacity improvements
- Donor retention
- Compliance with reporting commitments

Annual reviews will inform adjustments to priorities and resource allocation.
