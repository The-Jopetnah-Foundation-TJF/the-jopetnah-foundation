# Donor Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

## Introduction

Thank you for your interest in partnering with **The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF)**.

This document provides answers to common questions from prospective donors, corporate partners, philanthropic organizations, development agencies, governments, and other stakeholders.

If your question is not addressed here, please contact the Foundation using the details provided at the end of this document.

---

# About the Foundation

## What is The Jopetnah Foundation?

The Jopetnah Foundation is an independent, non-profit organization dedicated to improving lives through sustainable, inclusive and community-driven development initiatives.

The Foundation works collaboratively with communities, governments, development partners, businesses and civil society organizations to create measurable social impact.

---

## Where does the Foundation operate?

The Foundation's primary area of operation is Uganda, with opportunities for regional collaboration where they align with our mission, partnerships and organizational capacity.

---

## What sectors does the Foundation work in?

Our strategic focus includes:

- Education and skills development
- Health and wellbeing
- Sustainable livelihoods
- Environmental sustainability
- Institutional strengthening
- Research and innovation
- Community empowerment

---

# Partnerships

## Who can partner with TJF?

We welcome collaboration with:

- Individual philanthropists
- Corporate organizations
- Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) programmes
- Philanthropic foundations
- Governments
- International development agencies
- Civil society organizations
- Academic institutions
- Faith-based organizations
- Community-based organizations

Partnerships are established where there is alignment of mission, values and strategic objectives.

---

## Does the Foundation accept unrestricted funding?

Yes, where appropriate.

Flexible funding enables the Foundation to strengthen organizational systems, improve programme quality and respond to emerging community needs.

Any unrestricted funding will be managed in accordance with the Foundation's governance and financial management policies.

---

## Can donors support a specific programme?

Yes.

Subject to organizational priorities and available opportunities, donors may support:

- A specific project
- A thematic programme
- A geographic area
- Institutional strengthening
- Research and innovation
- Core organizational capacity

---

# Financial Accountability

## How are donated funds managed?

The Foundation is committed to responsible financial stewardship.

Funds are managed through approved budgets, financial controls, internal oversight and transparent reporting processes.

---

## Does the Foundation provide financial reports?

Yes.

Reporting frequency depends on the partnership agreement but may include:

- Financial statements
- Budget performance reports
- Narrative reports
- Activity summaries
- Impact reports

---

## Are funds independently audited?

Where required by law, donor agreements or organizational policy, the Foundation will commission independent financial audits.

Audit findings are reviewed to strengthen governance and financial management.

---

# Monitoring and Impact

## How does TJF measure impact?

The Foundation uses Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning (MEL) systems to assess:

- Outputs delivered
- Outcomes achieved
- Community impact
- Lessons learned
- Programme effectiveness

Results inform decision-making and continuous improvement.

---

## Will donors receive progress updates?

Yes.

Partners receive updates in accordance with agreed reporting schedules.

Depending on the project, updates may include:

- Progress reports
- Financial reports
- Success stories
- Case studies
- Photographs (where appropriate and with consent)
- Lessons learned

---

# Governance

## Who governs the Foundation?

The Foundation is governed by a Board of Trustees responsible for strategic oversight, accountability, financial stewardship and policy approval.

The Executive Director leads the day-to-day operations and implementation of Board-approved strategies.

---

## What policies guide the Foundation?

The Foundation maintains policies covering areas such as:

- Governance
- Financial management
- Conflict of interest
- Anti-corruption
- Child safeguarding
- Data privacy
- Risk management
- Code of conduct

Policies are reviewed periodically to ensure continued relevance and effectiveness.

---

# Volunteering and Non-Financial Support

## Can individuals volunteer?

Yes.

The Foundation welcomes volunteers whose skills and experience align with our programmes and organizational needs.

Volunteer engagement is guided by the Foundation's volunteer management procedures.

---

## Can organizations provide technical assistance instead of funding?

Yes.

We value non-financial contributions including:

- Technical expertise
- Training
- Research
- Technology
- Equipment
- Professional services
- Knowledge sharing

---

# Transparency

## How does TJF ensure transparency?

The Foundation promotes transparency through:

- Responsible governance
- Financial controls
- Monitoring and evaluation
- Regular reporting
- Policy compliance
- Stakeholder engagement

Transparency is fundamental to maintaining trust with beneficiaries, donors and partners.
