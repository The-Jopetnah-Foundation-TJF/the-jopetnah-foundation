# TJF Grants & Funding Calendar

> **The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF)**
> **Building whole communities, one life at a time.**

This document is the Foundation's central, version-controlled **grants, funding-opportunities and donor-prospect calendar**. It is designed to support disciplined resource mobilisation by identifying, screening, prioritising, preparing for, pursuing and tracking funding opportunities relevant to The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF).

The calendar is maintained in-repository so that funding intelligence, decisions, responsibilities and updates remain transparent, auditable and accessible to authorised members of the TJF team.

---

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this calendar is to:

* identify funding opportunities aligned with TJF's mission, programmes and strategic priorities;
* distinguish genuine grant opportunities from scholarships, fellowships, CSR partnerships, sponsorships, in-kind support and sub-partnership opportunities;
* provide an early-warning system for upcoming funding windows;
* support timely preparation of concept notes, proposals, budgets and supporting documentation;
* record eligibility, funding route, strategic fit and organisational readiness;
* assign responsibility and next actions for priority opportunities;
* preserve donor intelligence and institutional knowledge within TJF;
* reduce duplicated research and avoid pursuing opportunities for which TJF is not currently eligible or appropriately positioned; and
* strengthen TJF's transition from opportunistic fundraising to a structured, diversified and sustainable resource-mobilisation pipeline.

> **Important:** Inclusion in this calendar does not constitute an endorsement, guarantee of eligibility, guarantee of funding, or indication that an application should be submitted. Each opportunity must be independently verified against the funder's current official guidance before TJF commits significant proposal-development resources.

# 3. How to Read This Calendar

This is not simply a list of grants. It is a **funding intelligence and pipeline-management tool**.

Each prospect should be assessed against five questions:

1. **Is TJF eligible?**
2. **Does the opportunity fit TJF's strategic priorities?**
3. **Is the funding route realistic for TJF at its current institutional stage?**
4. **Is TJF sufficiently prepared to submit a competitive application?**
5. **What is the next practical action?**

Where the answer to one or more questions is uncertain, the opportunity should remain in the research or watchlist category rather than being treated as an active application.

# 4. Status Definitions

Use the following standard status vocabulary consistently.

| Status                    | Meaning                                                                                               |
| ------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Open**                  | A verified application window is currently open.                                                      |
| **Upcoming**              | A future funding window is expected or formally announced.                                            |
| **Rolling**               | The funder genuinely accepts applications on an ongoing basis.                                        |
| **Monitor**               | The funder/opportunity is relevant but no actionable window is currently open.                        |
| **Researching**           | Eligibility, funding route or programme details are still being verified.                             |
| **Relationship Building** | Funding is primarily relationship-, referral- or invitation-based.                                    |
| **Partner Required**      | TJF should participate through a consortium, prime recipient, fiscal sponsor or other partner.        |
| **Preparation**           | TJF is developing a concept, partnership or proposal in anticipation of an opportunity.               |
| **In Progress**           | Proposal development or formal application work has begun.                                            |
| **Submitted**             | Application has been formally submitted.                                                              |
| **Due Diligence**         | Funder is conducting review, verification or organisational assessment.                               |
| **Awarded**               | Funding has been formally confirmed.                                                                  |
| **Declined**              | TJF has received a formal unsuccessful decision.                                                      |
| **Closed**                | The relevant application window has ended and no immediate action is possible.                        |
| **Not Eligible**          | TJF does not currently meet the applicable eligibility requirements.                                  |
| **Archived**              | The opportunity is retained for historical/intelligence purposes but is no longer an active prospect. |

# 5. Priority Definitions

### P1 — High Priority

Strong strategic and geographic fit, realistic funding route and reasonable organisational readiness.

**Action:** Actively pursue or prepare.

### P2 — Strategic

Good potential fit but requires further preparation, relationship development, a partner or a future funding window.

**Action:** Develop readiness and monitor.

### P3 — Watchlist

Potentially relevant but currently uncertain, low probability or dependent on future circumstances.

**Action:** Monitor without significant proposal expenditure.

| **In-kind Support**             | Goods, equipment, services or other non-cash support.                                  |

# 7. Organisational Readiness

Every active prospect should be assessed against TJF's readiness.

### 🟢 Ready

TJF appears eligible and possesses the principal documentation and partnerships required.

### 🟡 Conditional

Potentially eligible, but one or more requirements need confirmation or completion.

### 🔵 Partner Required

The opportunity is best pursued through a strategic partner, consortium or prime recipient.

### 🟠 Preparation Required

The opportunity is strategically attractive, but TJF needs organisational, technical or programme preparation before applying.

### 🔴 Not Currently Eligible

TJF should not invest proposal-development resources unless its eligibility position changes.

---

# 8. Verification Standard

Funding information changes frequently. Accordingly:

* **Never rely solely on an old deadline.**
* Always consult the funder's current official guidance before applying.
* Record the date on which an opportunity was last verified.
* Record whether the information was verified from the official funder, an implementing partner or a secondary source.
* Treat historical deadlines as historical information, not current opportunities.
* Where a funder has no open application mechanism, classify the opportunity accordingly rather than describing it as "rolling."
* Where a programme requires a partner, do not present it as a direct TJF application opportunity.
* Where a funding amount is uncertain, state **"Varies"** rather than estimating.

**Calendar review frequency:** Monthly
**Full donor-intelligence review:** Quarterly
**Immediate verification:** Whenever TJF intends to pursue an opportunity

## 10.6 U.S. Government Health Opportunities

The former USAID Uganda row has been retained conceptually but converted into a broader **specific-solicitation monitoring category**.

TJF should never treat "USAID," "State Department" or "CDC" as one generic grant opportunity.

Instead:

1. identify the specific solicitation;
2. confirm the applicant category;
3. confirm registration requirements;
4. determine whether direct application is realistic;
5. identify potential prime/consortium partners;
6. assess compliance and reporting requirements; and
7. only then create a proposal-development workstream.

## 10.7 WHO/TDR Impact Grants

The TDR Impact Grants are implementation-research grants rather than general NGO programme grants. TDR describes them as supporting implementation research on infectious diseases of poverty, including work involving NGOs and professional/civil-society organisations, with a strong emphasis on research capacity, evidence and One Health approaches.

Recent regional awards demonstrate that grant sizes can be in the approximate US$10,000–US$15,000 range, with duration depending on the applicable regional call.

For TJF, the strongest route would likely involve:

> **TJF + academic/research institution + health/clinical implementation partner**

rather than TJF attempting to submit a purely service-delivery proposal.

# 14. Recommended Scoring Model

For internal prioritisation, TJF may score opportunities from **1–5** in the following categories:

| Criterion                    |   Weight |
| ---------------------------- | -------: |
| Strategic alignment          |      20% |
| Eligibility / access         |      15% |
| Geographic fit               |      10% |
| Programme fit                |      15% |
| Funding attractiveness       |      10% |
| Organisational readiness     |      10% |
| Partnership potential        |       5% |
| Probability of success       |      10% |
| Long-term relationship value |       5% |
| **Total**                    | **100%** |

### Suggested interpretation

* **80–100%:** P1 — pursue actively
* **65–79%:** P2 — prepare / cultivate
* **50–64%:** P3 — monitor
* **Below 50%:** generally do not pursue unless there is a compelling strategic reason

This score should support—not replace—professional judgement.

# 16. Go / No-Go Decision

TJF should not automatically apply simply because a grant appears attractive.

### GO

Proceed when:

* eligibility is confirmed;
* strategic fit is strong;
* TJF can demonstrate relevant capacity;
* the funding route is realistic;
* required partnerships are available;
* proposal development resources are available; and
* expected value justifies the effort.

### NO-GO

Do not proceed when:

* TJF is clearly ineligible;
* the funder excludes Uganda;
* the funding route requires a partner that cannot realistically be secured;
* the programme is fundamentally outside TJF's mission;
* the compliance burden is disproportionate;
* the deadline is too short to produce a competitive proposal; or
* the opportunity would require material commitments that TJF cannot responsibly make.

A **No-Go decision is a valid fundraising decision**, not a failure.

# 20. Source Integrity

The preferred hierarchy for verifying funding intelligence is:

1. **Official funder website**
2. **Official government / UN / implementing-agency website**
3. **Official programme announcement**
4. **Official application portal**
5. **Trusted partner or consortium announcement**
6. **Reputable secondary funding database**
7. **Social media announcement only as a lead requiring independent verification**

Secondary sources should not be treated as definitive where the funder's own guidance is available.

---

# 21. Important Distinction: Opportunity vs Prospect

A **funding opportunity** is a specific mechanism through which TJF may seek support.

A **funding prospect** is an institution, relationship or programme that may produce future funding but does not necessarily have an open application.

Examples:

* **GEF-SGP 2026 Uganda call:** specific funding opportunity.
* **Segal Family Foundation:** donor prospect / relationship-based funding route.
* **MTN Foundation:** corporate funding prospect with specific programmes.
* **Pandemic Fund:** institutional funding ecosystem where TJF may participate through implementing partners.

This distinction should be preserved throughout TJF's donor-management system.
