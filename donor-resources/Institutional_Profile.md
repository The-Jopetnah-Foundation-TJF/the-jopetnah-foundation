# Institutional Profile

## Executive Summary

The **Jopetnah Foundation (TJF)** is an independent, non-profit and non-partisan charitable organization committed to empowering communities through sustainable development initiatives.

The Foundation works with communities, governments, civil society, development partners, academic institutions and the private sector to design and implement programmes that improve lives and strengthen resilience.

Our work is guided by principles of integrity, accountability, inclusion, innovation and sustainability.

# Approach to Development

The Foundation adopts a community-driven development model.

Our programmes are designed through consultation with communities and implemented in partnership with stakeholders to ensure relevance, ownership and sustainability.

Our approach includes:

- Participatory planning
- Evidence-based programme design
- Inclusive implementation
- Continuous monitoring
- Learning and adaptation
- Long-term sustainability

---

# Governance

The Foundation is governed by a Board of Trustees responsible for:

- Strategic oversight
- Policy approval
- Financial stewardship
- Risk management
- Organizational accountability

The Executive Director is responsible for day-to-day leadership and implementation of Board-approved strategies.

Supporting functions include programme management, finance, partnerships, communications, monitoring and evaluation, and administration.

Partnerships are built on trust, transparency and shared impact.
