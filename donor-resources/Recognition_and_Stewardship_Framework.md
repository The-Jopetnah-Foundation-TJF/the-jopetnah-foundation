# Donor Recognition and Stewardship Framework

## 1. Purpose

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that sustainable development depends upon strong, transparent and mutually beneficial partnerships.

This Framework establishes the principles and practices through which the Foundation acknowledges contributions, maintains meaningful engagement with supporters and demonstrates accountability throughout the partnership lifecycle.

The objective is to strengthen trust, encourage long-term collaboration and ensure that every donor understands the impact of their contribution.

---

# 2. Scope

This Framework applies to all forms of support received by the Foundation, including:

- Individual donations
- Corporate partnerships
- Foundation grants
- Government funding
- International development assistance
- In-kind contributions
- Technical assistance
- Volunteer expertise
- Strategic partnerships

---

# 3. Guiding Principles

The Foundation's stewardship activities are guided by the following principles:

## Respect

Every donor is valued regardless of the size or nature of their contribution.

---

## Transparency

Partners receive timely and accurate information regarding the use of resources and programme progress.

---

## Accountability

The Foundation accepts responsibility for managing contributions in accordance with agreed objectives, applicable policies and donor requirements.

---

## Integrity

Recognition activities shall never compromise the Foundation's independence, mission or ethical standards.

---

## Partnership

Stewardship extends beyond acknowledging donations; it involves building enduring relationships based on shared values and measurable impact.

---

---

# 5. Donor Recognition

Recognition should be appropriate to the donor's preferences, applicable laws and any confidentiality requirements.

Recognition may include:

- Formal thank-you letters
- Certificates of appreciation
- Annual impact reports
- Recognition on the Foundation website (with consent)
- Recognition in annual reports (with consent)
- Invitations to Foundation events
- Programme launch invitations
- Project completion ceremonies
- Partnership announcements (where mutually agreed)

Recognition should never imply endorsement or compromise the Foundation's independence.

---

# 6. Communication Standards

The Foundation commits to maintaining professional and timely communication with all partners.

Communication may include:

- Acknowledgement of contributions
- Progress updates
- Quarterly or annual reports
- Project milestones
- Success stories
- Lessons learned
- Opportunities for collaboration

Where possible, communication should be tailored to the reporting requirements and interests of each partner.

---

# 7. Reporting Commitments

The Foundation seeks to provide clear and accurate reporting that demonstrates accountability and learning.

Depending on the partnership agreement, reports may include:

- Financial reports
- Narrative progress reports
- Monitoring and evaluation summaries
- Output and outcome indicators
- Case studies
- Photographs and media (with appropriate consent)
- Risk updates
- Lessons learned
- Final completion reports

Reporting schedules should be agreed at the beginning of each partnership.

---

# 8. Donor Engagement Opportunities

Partners may be invited to engage with the Foundation through:

- Programme visits
- Community engagement events
- Learning forums
- Stakeholder consultations
- Research dissemination events
- Strategic planning discussions
- Annual partner meetings
- Volunteer initiatives

Participation is voluntary and subject to programme requirements and safeguarding considerations.

---

# 9. Confidentiality and Privacy

The Foundation respects the privacy of all supporters.

Donor information will be handled in accordance with applicable data protection requirements and the Foundation's Data Privacy Policy.

No donor will be publicly recognized without prior consent where such consent is required.

---

# 10. Feedback and Continuous Improvement

The Foundation encourages feedback from donors and partners regarding:

- Communication quality
- Reporting effectiveness
- Programme performance
- Partnership experience
- Opportunities for improvement

Feedback will be reviewed to strengthen stewardship practices and organizational learning.

---

# 11. Roles and Responsibilities

## Board of Trustees

- Provides oversight of stewardship principles.
- Promotes a culture of accountability and transparency.

---

## Executive Director

- Champions strategic partnerships.
- Approves high-level recognition initiatives.
- Ensures organizational compliance with this Framework.

---

## Partnerships & Resource Mobilization

Responsible for:

- Donor relationship management
- Stewardship planning
- Partnership communications
- Recognition activities
- Maintaining donor records

---

## Programme Teams

Responsible for:

- Delivering agreed activities
- Providing programme updates
- Supporting monitoring and reporting

---

## Finance Team

Responsible for:

- Financial reporting
- Budget monitoring
- Compliance with donor financial requirements

---

# 12. Measuring Success

The effectiveness of this Framework may be assessed through indicators such as:

- Donor retention rate
- Repeat funding
- Number of strategic partnerships
- Timeliness of reporting
- Donor satisfaction feedback
- Partnership renewals
- Collaborative initiatives launched

These indicators should inform continuous improvement.

---

# 13. Review

This Framework shall be reviewed at least every two years or sooner if significant organizational, legal or strategic changes occur.
