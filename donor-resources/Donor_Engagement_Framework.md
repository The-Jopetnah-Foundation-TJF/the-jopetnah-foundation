# Donor Engagement Framework

## 1. Purpose

The Donor Engagement Framework establishes the principles, processes and standards used by The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) to build, manage and sustain relationships with donors, grant-makers, corporate partners, philanthropic institutions, governments and development agencies.

The Framework promotes long-term partnerships based on trust, transparency, measurable impact and shared value.

---

# 2. Scope

This Framework applies to all fundraising and partnership activities undertaken by The Jopetnah Foundation, including:

- Individual giving
- Corporate partnerships
- Institutional grants
- Foundations
- Faith-based organizations
- Government partnerships
- International development agencies
- Universities and research institutions
- Philanthropic networks

---

# 3. Objectives

The Foundation seeks to:

- Build strategic, long-term donor relationships.
- Demonstrate accountability and transparency.
- Deliver measurable social impact.
- Improve donor confidence and retention.
- Diversify funding sources.
- Strengthen collaboration across sectors.

---

# 4. Guiding Principles

## Partnership

We view donors as long-term partners in development rather than sources of funding.

---

## Transparency

We communicate openly about objectives, finances, risks and results.

---

## Accountability

Every contribution entrusted to the Foundation is managed responsibly and reported accurately.

---

## Integrity

All fundraising and partnership activities shall be conducted ethically and in accordance with Foundation policies.

---

## Sustainability

Funding decisions should contribute to sustainable community development rather than short-term interventions.

---

# 6. Stage Descriptions

## Stage 1 — Prospect Identification

Potential partners are identified based on:

- Strategic alignment
- Geographic priorities
- Funding interests
- Sector focus
- Shared values
- Partnership potential

---

## Stage 2 — Research & Qualification

The Foundation conducts due diligence to determine:

- Funding priorities
- Eligibility requirements
- Application timelines
- Reporting expectations
- Compliance obligations
- Partnership history

---

## Stage 3 — Initial Engagement

Engagement may include:

- Introductory meetings
- Presentations
- Concept discussions
- Networking events
- Conferences
- Virtual consultations

The objective is to establish mutual interest and identify areas of collaboration.

---

## Stage 4 — Needs Assessment

The Foundation works with prospective partners to understand:

- Community priorities
- Programme objectives
- Expected outcomes
- Available resources
- Risks
- Sustainability considerations

---

## Stage 5 — Proposal Development

Where appropriate, the Foundation prepares:

- Concept notes
- Grant proposals
- Project budgets
- Logical frameworks
- Monitoring plans
- Risk assessments

All proposals shall be evidence-based and aligned with the Foundation's Strategic Plan.

---

## Stage 6 — Due Diligence

Before formalizing a partnership, both parties should complete appropriate due diligence processes.

This may include:

- Governance review
- Financial review
- Legal compliance
- Reputational assessment
- Risk analysis
- Ethical screening

---

## Stage 7 — Partnership Agreement

Where collaboration proceeds, a formal agreement should define:

- Objectives
- Roles and responsibilities
- Budget
- Reporting schedule
- Performance indicators
- Communication protocols
- Duration
- Termination provisions

---

## Stage 8 — Programme Implementation

The Foundation implements activities in accordance with approved work plans, budgets and partnership agreements.

Implementation shall emphasize:

- Community participation
- Efficient resource utilization
- Continuous monitoring
- Adaptive management

---

## Stage 9 — Monitoring and Reporting

The Foundation provides regular reports that may include:

- Financial reports
- Narrative reports
- Output indicators
- Outcome indicators
- Success stories
- Challenges encountered
- Lessons learned
- Risk updates

Reporting frequency shall be determined by partnership agreements.

---

## Stage 10 — Stewardship and Relationship Management

Following implementation, the Foundation continues to engage donors through:

- Progress updates
- Impact reports
- Recognition initiatives
- Invitations to events
- Learning sessions
- Strategic planning discussions

The objective is to develop enduring partnerships based on shared impact.

---

Provides governance oversight and ensures accountability.

---

- Proposal coordination
- Partnership development
- Stewardship

---

- Technical proposal development
- Programme implementation
- Monitoring
- Reporting

---

## Finance Team

Responsible for:

- Budget preparation
- Financial reporting
- Compliance with donor requirements
- Internal controls

---

## Monitoring & Evaluation Team

Responsible for:

- Performance measurement
- Data quality
- Learning
- Impact reporting

---

# 8. Communication Standards

The Foundation commits to:

- Timely responses
- Honest communication
- Respectful engagement
- Professional conduct
- Evidence-based reporting

Communication should strengthen trust throughout the partnership lifecycle.

---

# 9. Ethical Fundraising

The Foundation will not knowingly accept funding that:

- Conflicts with its mission or values.
- Compromises its independence.
- Creates undue influence over programmes or governance.
- Violates applicable laws or sanctions.
- Poses unacceptable reputational or ethical risks.

Potential funding sources may be declined following due diligence where such risks cannot be appropriately managed.

---

# 10. Continuous Improvement

The Foundation periodically reviews this Framework based on:

- Partner feedback
- Lessons learned
- Programme evaluations
- Changes in donor expectations
- Regulatory developments
- Strategic priorities

---

# 11. Success Indicators

Implementation of this Framework should contribute to:

- Increased donor retention
- Stronger strategic partnerships
- Improved proposal quality
- Enhanced transparency
- Diversified funding portfolio
- Greater community impact

---

# 12. Document Review

This Framework shall be reviewed at least once every two years or earlier where significant organizational, legal or strategic changes occur.
