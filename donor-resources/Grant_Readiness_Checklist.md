# Grant Readiness Checklist

## Purpose

This checklist enables The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) to assess its readiness to pursue, secure and manage grants from governments, development agencies, philanthropic foundations, corporate partners and multilateral organizations.

It is intended to support consistent preparation, reduce proposal risks and strengthen institutional credibility during donor due diligence.

---

# Readiness Assessment

Use the following scale:

| Status | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| ✅ | Complete and up to date |
| 🟡 | Partially complete or requires review |
| ❌ | Not yet available |

# Section 8 – Proposal Preparation

Before submitting any proposal, confirm that:

- ☐ Opportunity aligns with the Foundation's mission.
- ☐ Eligibility requirements have been reviewed.
- ☐ Proposal team assigned.
- ☐ Budget reviewed by Finance.
- ☐ Risks identified.
- ☐ Implementation capacity confirmed.
- ☐ Required attachments prepared.
- ☐ Executive approval obtained.

---

# Common Supporting Documents

Typical donor requests include:

- Certificate of Registration
- Governing Documents
- Board List
- Organizational Profile
- Strategic Plan
- Audited Financial Statements (if available)
- Annual Report
- Policies and Procedures
- Safeguarding Policy
- Financial Management Policy
- Bank Details
- References or Partnership Letters

Maintain current versions of these documents in a secure organizational repository.

---

# Continuous Improvement

Following each grant application, the proposal team should document:

- Lessons learned
- Reviewer feedback (if provided)
- Strengths of the submission
- Areas for improvement
- Actions before the next application

These insights should inform future proposal development.

---

# Review

This checklist should be reviewed annually and whenever significant organizational, legal or strategic changes occur.

---

> **Grant readiness is not a one-time milestone—it is an ongoing organizational commitment to strong governance, accountability and continuous improvement.**
