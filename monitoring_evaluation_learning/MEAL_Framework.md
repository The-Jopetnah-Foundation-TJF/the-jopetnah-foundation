# Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability & Learning (MEAL) Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) implements a comprehensive Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability & Learning (MEAL) system to ensure that programmes achieve measurable, sustainable and meaningful impact.

The MEAL Framework provides the institutional approach for planning, collecting, analyzing and using information to improve programme effectiveness.

The Framework ensures that TJF:

- Measures results.
- Demonstrates impact.
- Learns from experience.
- Responds to stakeholder needs.
- Strengthens organizational performance.

---

# 1. Purpose

The MEAL Framework aims to:

- Establish consistent measurement systems.
- Improve programme quality.
- Strengthen accountability.
- Support strategic decision-making.
- Demonstrate value to partners and donors.

---

# 2. MEAL Approach

TJF applies a results-based management approach.

The framework follows:

```
Resources
    │
    ▼
Activities
    │
    ▼
Outputs
    │
    ▼
Outcomes
    │
    ▼
Impact
```

---

# 3. MEAL Standards

## Standard 1: Clear Results

All TJF programmes must define:

- Expected results.
- Indicators.
- Targets.
- Measurement methods.

---

## Standard 2: Reliable Data

Data must be:

- Accurate.
- Relevant.
- Timely.
- Verified.

---

## Standard 3: Stakeholder Participation

Beneficiaries and partners should participate in:

- Programme feedback.
- Assessments.
- Learning activities.

---

## Standard 4: Evidence-Based Decisions

Programme decisions should be informed by:

- Monitoring data.
- Evaluation findings.
- Stakeholder feedback.

Monitoring tracks ongoing programme performance.

- Activities completed.
- Timelines.
- Participation.

---

### Output Monitoring

- Services delivered.

# 6. Evaluation System

Evaluations assess programme performance.

TJF may conduct:

## Baseline Evaluation

Conducted before implementation.

Purpose:

- Establish starting conditions.
- Define measurement points.

---

## Mid-Term Evaluation

Conducted during implementation.

Purpose:

- Assess progress.
- Identify improvements.

---

## End-Term Evaluation

Conducted after completion.

Purpose:

- Measure achievements.
- Capture lessons.

---

## Impact Evaluation

Assesses:

- Long-term change.
- Sustainability.
- Broader effects.

---

# 7. Evaluation Criteria

TJF evaluations consider:

## Relevance

Does the programme address real needs?

---

## Effectiveness

Were objectives achieved?

---

## Efficiency

Were resources used effectively?

---

## Impact

What changes occurred?

---

## Sustainability

Will results continue?
