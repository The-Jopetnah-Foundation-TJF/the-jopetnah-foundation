# Theory of Change

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) believes that sustainable development requires integrated approaches that address immediate needs while strengthening long-term community resilience.

This Theory of Change explains how TJF's investments, programmes and partnerships contribute to measurable improvements in people's lives.

It describes the relationship between:

- Resources invested.
- Actions implemented.
- Results achieved.
- Long-term transformation created.

---

# 1. Purpose of the Theory of Change

The Theory of Change provides a framework for:

- Designing effective programmes.
- Measuring impact.
- Communicating TJF's approach.
- Guiding strategic decisions.
- Aligning stakeholders.

---

# 2. TJF Impact Vision
