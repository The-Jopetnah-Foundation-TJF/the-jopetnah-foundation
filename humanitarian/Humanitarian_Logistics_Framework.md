# Humanitarian Logistics Framework

## Executive Summary

Humanitarian logistics is fundamental to delivering timely and effective emergency assistance.

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) shall maintain a resilient humanitarian logistics system capable of sourcing, transporting, storing and distributing emergency supplies under diverse operating conditions while ensuring transparency, accountability and value for money.

This Framework establishes the governance, systems and operational standards for humanitarian logistics across all TJF emergency operations.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Establish an efficient humanitarian supply chain.
- Improve emergency procurement and delivery.
- Strengthen logistics coordination.
- Ensure accountability for humanitarian assets.
- Reduce delays and operational risks.
- Support effective emergency response.

---

# 2. Scope

This Framework applies to:

- Emergency procurement.
- Warehousing.
- Fleet operations.
- Transport management.
- Inventory management.
- International and domestic logistics.
- Emergency equipment.
- Humanitarian relief supplies.

It applies to:

- Headquarters.
- Country offices.
- Regional operations.
- Logistics personnel.
- Procurement teams.
- Programme staff.
- Approved logistics partners.

---

# 3. Logistics Principles

Humanitarian logistics shall be guided by:

## Timeliness

Deliver assistance rapidly to affected populations.

---

## Accountability

Maintain accurate records throughout the supply chain.

---

## Efficiency

Optimize resources while minimizing waste.

---

## Transparency

Ensure logistics decisions are documented and auditable.

---

## Safety

Protect personnel, assets and beneficiaries.

---

## Flexibility

Adapt logistics systems to changing operational environments.

---

# 5. Emergency Procurement

Emergency procurement shall:

- Follow approved emergency procedures.
- Prioritize speed without compromising integrity.
- Use pre-qualified suppliers where possible.
- Ensure value for money.
- Document all procurement decisions.

Emergency procurement shall remain subject to financial oversight.

---

# 6. Supplier Management

TJF shall maintain a register of approved suppliers based on:

- Legal compliance.
- Financial stability.
- Product quality.
- Delivery capacity.
- Ethical business conduct.
- Previous performance.

Supplier performance shall be reviewed periodically.

---

# 7. Receiving and Inspection

All incoming goods shall be:

- Verified against purchase orders.
- Inspected for quality.
- Checked for quantity.
- Recorded in inventory systems.
- Reported if damaged or incomplete.

Goods failing inspection shall be documented and managed according to procurement procedures.

---

# 8. Warehouse Management

Warehouse operations shall include:

- Controlled access.
- Safe storage.
- Stock identification.
- Environmental protection.
- Stock rotation.
- Security measures.
- Regular inventory counts.

Warehouse layouts should support efficient movement of goods and emergency dispatch.

---

# 9. Inventory Management

Inventory systems shall record:

- Item descriptions.
- Quantities.
- Batch or lot numbers where applicable.
- Expiry dates.
- Storage locations.
- Stock movements.
- Current balances.

Regular reconciliations shall be performed to identify discrepancies.

---

# 10. Fleet and Transport Management

Transport operations shall ensure:

- Vehicle roadworthiness.
- Qualified drivers.
- Route planning.
- Fuel accountability.
- Cargo security.
- Journey monitoring.
- Maintenance scheduling.

Alternative transport arrangements shall be identified for emergency situations.

---

# 11. Cold Chain Management

Where temperature-sensitive items are handled, TJF shall maintain:

- Temperature-controlled storage.
- Continuous monitoring.
- Equipment maintenance.
- Temperature records.
- Contingency procedures for equipment failure.

---

# 12. Customs and Cross-Border Logistics

For international operations, TJF shall:

- Comply with customs regulations.
- Obtain required permits.
- Coordinate with government authorities.
- Maintain shipping documentation.
- Monitor cross-border movements.

---

# 13. Asset Tracking

Emergency assets shall be tracked using:

- Asset identification numbers.
- Movement registers.
- Custodian records.
- Maintenance logs.
- Disposal records.

Assets shall be used only for authorized humanitarian purposes.

---

# 14. Security of Supplies

TJF shall implement measures to:

- Prevent theft.
- Reduce diversion.
- Protect warehouses.
- Secure transportation.
- Control access to logistics facilities.

Security incidents shall be reported immediately.

---

# 15. Reverse Logistics

Reverse logistics procedures shall address:

- Returned supplies.
- Damaged goods.
- Expired items.
- Surplus stock.
- Equipment recovery.
- Environmentally responsible disposal.

---

# 16. Coordination

Logistics operations shall be coordinated with:

- Programme teams.
- Procurement.
- Finance.
- Emergency Operations Centre.
- Government authorities.
- Humanitarian partners.
- Logistics service providers.

---

# 17. Information Systems

TJF shall maintain logistics information systems capable of supporting:

- Inventory management.
- Shipment tracking.
- Warehouse management.
- Fleet monitoring.
- Reporting.
- Data analysis.

Systems should be backed up regularly and protected against unauthorized access.

---

# 18. Capacity Building

TJF shall strengthen logistics capacity through:

- Staff training.
- Warehouse simulations.
- Fleet safety training.
- Procurement workshops.
- Logistics coordination exercises.
- Lessons learned reviews.

---

# 19. Monitoring and Performance Management

Logistics performance shall be monitored through:

- Delivery times.
- Inventory accuracy.
- Fleet utilization.
- Supplier performance.
- Transport costs.
- Distribution efficiency.

Corrective actions shall be implemented where performance gaps are identified.
