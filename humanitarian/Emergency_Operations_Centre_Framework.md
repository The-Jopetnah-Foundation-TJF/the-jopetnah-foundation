# Emergency Operations Centre Framework

## Executive Summary

The Emergency Operations Centre (EOC) serves as The Jopetnah Foundation's central coordination hub for emergency preparedness, response, recovery and information management.

The EOC enables timely decision-making, coordinated resource deployment, situational awareness and effective communication during humanitarian operations. It supports collaboration between TJF departments, government agencies, humanitarian partners, donors and affected communities.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Establish TJF's Emergency Operations Centre.
- Coordinate emergency operations.
- Improve decision-making during crises.
- Strengthen interdepartmental coordination.
- Enhance situational awareness.
- Support efficient resource management.

---

# 2. Scope

This Framework applies to:

- Humanitarian emergencies.
- Natural disasters.
- Disease outbreaks.
- Security incidents.
- Large-scale organizational crises.
- Multi-location emergency responses.

It applies to:

- Headquarters.
- Country offices.
- Regional offices.
- Programme teams.
- Emergency response personnel.
- Volunteers and designated partners.

---

# 3. Guiding Principles

The EOC shall operate according to:

- Unified command.
- Timely coordination.
- Accurate information management.
- Accountability.
- Flexibility.
- Continuous communication.
- Evidence-based decision-making.

---

# 4. Objectives

The EOC seeks to:

- Coordinate emergency operations.
- Maintain a common operating picture.
- Support leadership decisions.
- Facilitate information sharing.
- Manage emergency resources.
- Coordinate partner engagement.

---

# 5. EOC Activation Levels

## Level 1 – Monitoring

Activated when:

- Elevated risks exist.
- Routine monitoring is required.

Activities include:

- Situation monitoring.
- Information gathering.
- Preparedness reviews.

---

## Level 2 – Partial Activation

Activated when:

- A significant incident requires coordination.
- Multiple departments are involved.

Activities include:

- Resource coordination.
- Situation reporting.
- Operational planning.

---

## Level 3 – Full Activation

Activated when:

- Major emergencies occur.
- National or international coordination is required.

Activities include:

- Full staffing.
- Continuous operations.
- Executive-level oversight.

---

# 9. Incident Command Integration

The EOC shall support an Incident Command System (ICS) by:

- Establishing a clear chain of command.
- Assigning operational responsibilities.
- Coordinating field operations.
- Supporting unified decision-making.

---

# 10. Situation Reporting

The EOC shall produce:

- Initial Incident Reports.
- Daily Situation Reports (SITREPs).
- Resource Status Reports.
- Operational Briefings.
- Recovery Updates.

Reports should include:

- Current situation.
- Response activities.
- Challenges.
- Resource requirements.
- Planned actions.

---

# 11. Information Management

The EOC shall maintain:

- Incident databases.
- GIS and mapping information where available.
- Beneficiary data.
- Resource tracking systems.
- Contact directories.
- Decision logs.

Information shall be protected in accordance with TJF's data protection policies.

---

# 12. Resource Coordination

The EOC shall coordinate:

- Personnel deployment.
- Vehicles.
- Relief supplies.
- Equipment.
- Emergency funding.
- Partner contributions.

Priority shall be based on assessed humanitarian need.

---

# 13. Communication Systems

Primary communication methods may include:

- Telephone.
- Mobile messaging.
- Email.
- Video conferencing.
- Two-way radio.
- Satellite communication where available.

Backup communication methods shall be identified for use during system failures.

---

# 14. Operational Facilities

The EOC should include:

- Operations room.
- Meeting and briefing space.
- Communications area.
- Information display boards.
- Secure records storage.
- ICT infrastructure.
- Backup power supply where feasible.

Virtual EOCs may be activated when physical access is restricted.

---

# 15. Coordination with External Stakeholders

The EOC shall coordinate with:

- National disaster management authorities.
- Local governments.
- Emergency services.
- United Nations agencies.
- NGOs.
- Community leaders.
- Donors.
- Private sector partners.

---

# 16. Staff Welfare

The EOC shall promote:

- Safe working conditions.
- Shift scheduling.
- Staff wellbeing.
- Psychosocial support.
- Rest periods during prolonged operations.

---

# 17. Transition to Recovery

The EOC shall coordinate the transition from emergency response to recovery through:

- Demobilization planning.
- Handover to recovery teams.
- Resource reconciliation.
- Final situation reporting.
- Lessons learned documentation.

---

# 18. Deactivation

The Executive Director may authorize EOC deactivation when:

- Immediate threats have been addressed.
- Essential services are stabilized.
- Recovery arrangements are operational.
- Coordination requirements have reduced.

A formal deactivation report shall be prepared.

---
