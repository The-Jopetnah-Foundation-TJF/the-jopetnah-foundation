# Emergency Funding Mobilization Strategy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that timely access to financial resources is essential for effective humanitarian response. Delays in funding can significantly affect the organization's ability to save lives, reduce suffering and support early recovery.

This Emergency Funding Mobilization Strategy establishes the governance, financing mechanisms and partnership approaches required to mobilize emergency resources rapidly while ensuring responsible stewardship, transparency and compliance with donor requirements.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Strategy aims to:

- Mobilize emergency funding rapidly.
- Diversify humanitarian funding sources.
- Strengthen donor confidence.
- Improve financial preparedness.
- Ensure transparent management of emergency resources.
- Support sustainable humanitarian operations.

---

# 2. Scope

This Strategy applies to:

- Natural disasters.
- Public health emergencies.
- Humanitarian crises.
- Conflict-related emergencies.
- Emergency recovery programmes.
- Emergency preparedness initiatives.

It applies to:

- Board of Directors.
- Executive Management.
- Finance Department.
- Resource Mobilization Team.
- Programme Teams.
- Country and Regional Offices.

---

# 3. Guiding Principles

Emergency funding shall be guided by:

## Timeliness

Funding shall be mobilized and deployed as quickly as possible.

---

## Accountability

Emergency funds shall be managed responsibly and transparently.

---

## Equity

Resources shall be allocated according to assessed humanitarian needs.

---

## Sustainability

Funding decisions shall consider both immediate response and long-term recovery.

---

## Compliance

All funding activities shall comply with applicable laws, donor agreements and TJF policies.

---

# 6. Emergency Financing Mechanisms

TJF may utilize:

- Emergency response appeals.
- Rapid response grants.

- Crowdfunding initiatives.
- Matching gift campaigns.
- Corporate emergency partnerships.
- In-kind contributions.

# 7. Emergency Reserve Fund

TJF should seek to establish an Emergency Reserve Fund to:

- Support immediate response before external funding is received.
- Cover urgent operational expenses.
- Enable rapid deployment of emergency teams.

The Reserve Fund shall be governed by Board-approved financial procedures.

---

# 8. Emergency Appeal Process

Emergency appeals shall include:

- Situation overview.
- Humanitarian needs.
- Target population.
- Planned interventions.
- Budget.
- Expected outcomes.
- Accountability commitments.
- Donation instructions.

Appeals shall be approved by the Executive Director or delegated authority.

---

# 9. Proposal Development

Emergency funding proposals should include:

- Evidence-based needs assessments.
- Clear objectives.
- Implementation plans.
- Monitoring and evaluation framework.
- Risk analysis.
- Sustainability considerations.
- Budget justification.

---

# 10. Donor Engagement

TJF shall maintain regular communication with donors through:

- Situation updates.
- Financial reports.
- Programme progress reports.
- Impact stories.
- Lessons learned.
- Final reports.

Relationships shall be built on transparency, responsiveness and mutual trust.

---

# 11. Financial Management

Emergency funds shall be managed through:

- Approved budgets.
- Segregated accounting records where required.
- Internal controls.
- Procurement oversight.
- Regular reconciliations.
- Audit-ready documentation.

---

# 12. Resource Allocation

Funds shall be allocated based on:

- Severity of humanitarian need.
- Operational priorities.
- Available resources.
- Donor restrictions.
- Equity and inclusion considerations.

Allocation decisions shall be documented.

---

# 13. Risk Management

Key financial risks include:

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Delayed donor funding | Maintain reserve funding and diversify funding sources |
| Misuse of funds | Strong internal controls and regular audits |
| Currency fluctuations | Financial planning and monitoring |
| Fraud | Segregation of duties, whistleblowing and compliance reviews |
| Donor non-compliance | Regular compliance monitoring and staff training |

---

# 14. Transparency and Accountability

TJF shall promote accountability through:

- Timely financial reporting.
- Independent audits.
- Donor reporting.
- Public impact reporting where appropriate.
- Beneficiary feedback mechanisms.
