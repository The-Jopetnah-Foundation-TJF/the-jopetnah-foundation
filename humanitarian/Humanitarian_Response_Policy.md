# Humanitarian Response Policy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) is committed to protecting lives, reducing suffering and supporting the recovery of individuals and communities affected by disasters, emergencies and humanitarian crises.

This Humanitarian Response Policy establishes the principles, governance arrangements and operational standards that guide TJF’s humanitarian actions. It ensures that emergency interventions are timely, impartial, accountable and aligned with internationally recognized humanitarian standards.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Policy aims to:

- Establish TJF's humanitarian response framework.
- Guide emergency preparedness and response.
- Protect affected populations.
- Promote coordinated humanitarian action.
- Ensure responsible management of humanitarian resources.
- Strengthen resilience and recovery.

---

# 2. Scope

This Policy applies to:

- Natural disasters.
- Public health emergencies.
- Disease outbreaks.
- Floods.
- Droughts.
- Food insecurity.
- Conflict-related emergencies.
- Population displacement.
- Humanitarian crises requiring rapid intervention.

It applies to:

- Board Members.
- Executive Management.
- Staff.
- Volunteers.
- Consultants.
- Partner organizations.
- Emergency response personnel.

---

# 3. Humanitarian Principles

All TJF humanitarian operations shall be guided by:

## Humanity

Protect human life, dignity and well-being while reducing suffering wherever it is found.

---

## Neutrality

Do not take sides in political, religious or military disputes.

---

## Impartiality

Provide assistance based solely on need without discrimination.

---

## Independence

Maintain operational independence while coordinating with governments and humanitarian partners.

---

## Accountability

Remain accountable to affected communities, donors, partners and stakeholders.

---

## Do No Harm

Ensure interventions do not unintentionally increase risks or vulnerabilities.

---

# 4. Strategic Objectives

TJF seeks to:

- Save lives during emergencies.
- Protect vulnerable populations.
- Restore essential services.
- Support community recovery.
- Strengthen resilience to future crises.
- Coordinate effectively with humanitarian partners.

---

# 5. Humanitarian Response Areas

TJF may respond in the following sectors:

## Emergency Relief

Including:

- Food assistance.
- Safe drinking water.
- Emergency shelter.
- Essential household supplies.

---

## Health and Nutrition

Including:

- Medical outreach.
- Disease prevention.
- Nutrition support.
- Mental health and psychosocial support.

---

## Water, Sanitation and Hygiene (WASH)

Including:

- Safe water access.
- Hygiene promotion.
- Emergency sanitation facilities.

---

## Livelihood Recovery

Including:

- Agricultural recovery.
- Small business support.
- Cash or livelihood assistance, where appropriate.

---

## Protection

Including:

- Child protection.
- Gender-sensitive programming.
- Support for older persons and persons with disabilities.
- Prevention of exploitation, abuse and neglect.

---

TJF follows:


---

# 7. Activation Criteria

Humanitarian response may be activated when:

- A disaster significantly affects communities.
- Government authorities request support.
- TJF identifies urgent unmet humanitarian needs.
- The Executive Director authorizes activation.
- The Board approves large-scale emergency operations where required.

---

# 8. Coordination

TJF shall coordinate with:

- Government authorities.
- United Nations agencies.
- Humanitarian coordination platforms.
- NGOs.
- Community leaders.
- Development partners.

Coordination aims to:

- Avoid duplication.
- Improve efficiency.
- Maximize collective impact.

---

# 9. Community Engagement

Affected communities shall be involved in:

- Needs assessments.
- Programme design.
- Feedback mechanisms.
- Complaint handling.
- Monitoring activities.

---

# 10. Protection of Vulnerable Groups

Special consideration shall be given to:

- Children.
- Women and girls.
- Older persons.
- Persons with disabilities.
- Refugees and displaced persons.
- Other vulnerable populations.

---

# 11. Resource Management

Emergency resources shall be managed through:

- Transparent procurement.
- Financial accountability.
- Inventory control.
- Proper documentation.
- Regular audits.

---

# 12. Communication During Emergencies

Emergency communications should be:

- Accurate.
- Timely.
- Respectful.
- Coordinated.
- Accessible.

Only authorized spokespersons may issue official statements on behalf of TJF.

---

# 13. Safety and Security

TJF shall prioritize:

- Staff safety.
- Volunteer safety.
- Beneficiary protection.
- Secure operations.
- Compliance with security protocols.

---

# 14. Monitoring, Evaluation and Learning

Humanitarian operations shall include:

- Situation monitoring.
- Performance measurement.
- Beneficiary feedback.
- After-action reviews.
- Lessons learned documentation.

---

# 15. Roles and Responsibilities

## Board of Directors

Responsible for:

- Strategic oversight.
- Approval of major emergency operations.
- Governance and accountability.

---

## Executive Director

Responsible for:

- Activating emergency responses.
- Mobilizing organizational resources.
- Representing TJF with national and international partners.

---

## Humanitarian Affairs and Emergency Response Department

Responsible for:

- Operational planning.
- Coordination.
- Emergency deployment.
- Situation reporting.

---

## Programme Teams

Responsible for:

- Needs assessments.
- Programme implementation.
- Community engagement.
- Monitoring and reporting.

---

## Finance and Administration Department

Responsible for:

- Emergency financial management.
- Procurement.
- Logistics support.
- Asset accountability.

# 17. Policy Review

This Policy shall be reviewed:

- Every three years.
- After major humanitarian responses.
- Following significant legislative or humanitarian policy changes.
- Based on lessons learned and operational evaluations.

---

# Conclusion

The Jopetnah Foundation is committed to providing principled, accountable and effective humanitarian assistance that protects lives, restores dignity and strengthens community resilience.

Through preparedness, coordination and continuous learning, TJF seeks to deliver timely and sustainable humanitarian action that supports affected populations while contributing to long-term recovery and development.

---

> **Compassion in action, guided by integrity and preparedness, creates hope when communities need it most.**
