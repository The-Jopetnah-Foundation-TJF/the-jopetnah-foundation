# Relief Distribution Procedures

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) is committed to delivering humanitarian assistance efficiently, transparently and with respect for the dignity of affected communities.

These Relief Distribution Procedures establish standardized processes for planning, receiving, storing, transporting and distributing relief supplies while ensuring accountability, fairness and protection of beneficiaries.

---

# 1. Purpose

These Procedures aim to:

- Ensure fair and transparent distribution of relief assistance.
- Protect beneficiaries during distributions.
- Promote accountability for humanitarian resources.
- Standardize operational procedures.
- Reduce fraud, diversion and duplication.
- Improve monitoring and reporting.

---

# 2. Scope

These Procedures apply to:

- Emergency food assistance.
- Non-food item distributions.
- Shelter materials.
- Water, sanitation and hygiene supplies.
- Medical supplies.
- Cash or voucher assistance where applicable.

Applicable personnel include:

- Staff.
- Volunteers.
- Logistics teams.
- Programme personnel.
- Partner organizations.

---

# 3. Guiding Principles

Relief distribution shall be based on:

- Humanity.
- Impartiality.
- Neutrality.
- Independence.
- Accountability.
- Transparency.
- Respect for human dignity.
- Do No Harm.

---

# 5. Needs Assessment

Before any distribution, TJF shall conduct an assessment to determine:

- Number of affected households.
- Priority needs.
- Vulnerable population groups.
- Accessibility.
- Security considerations.
- Available local resources.

Assessment findings shall guide all distribution decisions.

---

# 6. Beneficiary Registration

Beneficiaries shall be identified using objective and transparent criteria.

Registration information may include:

- Full name.
- Household size.
- Community or location.
- Contact information where available.
- Vulnerability category.
- Unique registration number.

Personal information shall be protected in accordance with TJF's Data Protection Policy.

---

# 7. Beneficiary Selection Criteria

Priority may be given to:

- Households headed by women.
- Children.
- Older persons.
- Persons with disabilities.
- Pregnant and lactating women.
- Refugees and internally displaced persons.
- Chronically ill individuals.
- Extremely vulnerable households.

Selection criteria shall be communicated clearly to communities.

---

# 8. Relief Planning

Distribution plans shall specify:

- Distribution locations.
- Distribution schedules.
- Quantities.
- Staffing.
- Security arrangements.
- Transportation plans.
- Communication strategies.
- Contingency measures.

---

# 9. Warehouse Management

Relief items shall be:

- Properly received.
- Inspected upon delivery.
- Recorded in inventory systems.
- Stored safely.
- Protected from damage.
- Rotated where expiry dates apply.

Stock counts shall be conducted regularly.

---

# 10. Transportation

Transportation arrangements shall ensure:

- Safe loading.
- Secure transport.
- Timely delivery.
- Route planning.
- Vehicle inspections.
- Documentation of dispatched goods.

Drivers shall follow approved transport procedures.

---

# 11. Distribution Site Management

Distribution sites should provide:

- Safe access.
- Clear signage.
- Waiting areas.
- Registration desks.
- Distribution stations.
- Complaint desk.
- First aid support where appropriate.
- Security presence if required.

Special arrangements should be made for persons with reduced mobility.

---

# 12. Crowd Management

Distribution teams shall:

- Organize orderly queues.
- Prevent overcrowding.
- Prioritize vulnerable individuals.
- Coordinate with community leaders.
- Maintain safety throughout the distribution process.

Use of force shall only occur where legally authorized and strictly necessary for safety.

---

# 13. Distribution Procedures

At each distribution:

1. Verify beneficiary identity.
2. Confirm registration details.
3. Record items issued.
4. Obtain beneficiary acknowledgement where appropriate.
5. Provide information on complaints and feedback mechanisms.

Distribution records shall be maintained accurately.

---

# 14. Protection Measures

Distribution activities shall ensure:

- Privacy.
- Dignity.
- Inclusion.
- Child safeguarding.
- Prevention of exploitation and abuse.
- Gender-sensitive approaches.

Staff and volunteers shall adhere to TJF's Code of Conduct.

---

# 15. Complaint and Feedback Mechanism

Beneficiaries shall have access to confidential channels to:

- Ask questions.
- Report concerns.
- Report fraud.
- Report misconduct.
- Appeal eligibility decisions.
- Suggest improvements.

Complaints shall be investigated promptly and documented.

---

# 16. Monitoring During Distribution

Distribution monitoring shall assess:

- Timeliness.
- Coverage.
- Quantity distributed.
- Quality of items.
- Beneficiary satisfaction.
- Operational challenges.

Monitoring teams should remain independent from distribution teams where practicable.

---

# 17. Post-Distribution Monitoring

Following distributions, TJF shall evaluate:

- Whether assistance reached intended beneficiaries.
- Appropriate use of assistance.
- Remaining humanitarian needs.
- Community feedback.
- Lessons learned.

Findings shall inform future responses.

---

# 18. Documentation Requirements

Distribution records shall include:

- Beneficiary lists.
- Distribution plans.
- Inventory records.
- Dispatch notes.
- Attendance records.
- Monitoring reports.
- Complaint logs.
- Final distribution reports.

Records shall be retained in accordance with TJF's records management requirements.

# Conclusion

Well-managed relief distribution ensures that humanitarian assistance reaches those most in need while preserving dignity, promoting accountability and strengthening community trust.

Through standardized procedures, transparent systems and continuous improvement, The Jopetnah Foundation is committed to delivering humanitarian assistance responsibly, efficiently and ethically.

---
