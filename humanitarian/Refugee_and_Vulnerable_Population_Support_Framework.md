# Refugee and Vulnerable Population Support Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) is committed to supporting displaced and vulnerable populations through inclusive, rights-based and community-centered humanitarian and development interventions.

This Framework establishes the principles, governance arrangements and operational standards for assisting refugees, internally displaced persons (IDPs), asylum seekers, returnees, stateless persons and other vulnerable groups while promoting dignity, protection, resilience and long-term self-reliance.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Protect the rights and dignity of displaced and vulnerable populations.
- Deliver equitable humanitarian assistance.
- Promote inclusion and social cohesion.
- Strengthen resilience and self-reliance.
- Support durable solutions through partnerships.
- Ensure accountability to affected populations.

---

# 2. Scope

This Framework applies to programmes supporting:

- Refugees.
- Internally displaced persons (IDPs).
- Asylum seekers.
- Returnees.
- Stateless persons.
- Host communities affected by displacement.
- Other populations facing significant vulnerability.

It applies across all TJF humanitarian and development operations.

---

# 3. Guiding Principles

TJF shall uphold:

## Humanity

Every individual shall be treated with compassion and respect.

---

## Non-Discrimination

Assistance shall be provided without discrimination based on nationality, ethnicity, religion, gender, disability, age, political opinion or legal status.

---

## Protection

The safety, dignity and rights of affected individuals shall remain central to all interventions.

---

## Participation

Affected communities shall participate in decisions affecting their lives.

---

## Accountability

Programmes shall remain accountable to beneficiaries, donors and partners.

---

## Do No Harm

Activities shall minimize unintended negative consequences and strengthen community cohesion.

---

# 4. Strategic Objectives

TJF seeks to:

- Improve access to essential humanitarian services.
- Strengthen protection systems.
- Support education and healthcare access.
- Promote livelihoods and economic resilience.
- Encourage peaceful coexistence between displaced populations and host communities.
- Build long-term resilience.

---

# 5. Programme Areas

TJF may implement programmes in:

## Protection Services

Including:

- Legal information and referrals.
- Protection monitoring.
- Case management.
- Family tracing and reunification referrals where appropriate.

---

## Basic Needs Assistance

Including:

- Food assistance.
- Shelter support.
- Non-food items.
- Water, sanitation and hygiene (WASH).

---

## Health Services

Including:

- Primary healthcare referrals.
- Maternal and child health support.
- Mental health and psychosocial support.
- Health promotion activities.

---

## Education

Supporting:

- Access to formal education.
- Non-formal learning.
- Vocational training.
- Digital learning opportunities.

---

## Livelihoods and Economic Empowerment

Supporting:

- Skills development.
- Entrepreneurship.
- Financial inclusion.
- Employment readiness.
- Small business development.

---

# 6. Registration and Case Management

Where appropriate and in coordination with competent authorities, TJF may support:

- Beneficiary registration.
- Needs assessments.
- Vulnerability screening.
- Individual case management.
- Referral pathways.
- Follow-up and case closure.

Personal data shall be managed in accordance with TJF's Data Protection Policy.

---

# 7. Inclusion of Vulnerable Groups

Special measures shall support:

### Children

Including child safeguarding, education and psychosocial support.

---

### Women and Girls

Including gender-responsive programming, protection and economic empowerment.

---

### Older Persons

Including accessible services and age-sensitive programming.

---

### Persons with Disabilities

Including reasonable accommodation, accessible facilities and inclusive communication.

---

### Survivors of Violence

Including confidential referrals and survivor-centered support.

---

# 8. Community Engagement

TJF shall:

- Engage community leaders.
- Support community committees.
- Promote volunteer participation.
- Encourage community-led solutions.
- Strengthen social cohesion.

---

# 9. Durable Solutions

Where appropriate, TJF shall contribute to durable solutions through:

- Local integration support.
- Livelihood development.
- Education and skills training.
- Community development initiatives.
- Referral to competent authorities for voluntary return or resettlement processes.

TJF does not determine legal refugee status or resettlement decisions.

---

# 10. Coordination

TJF shall coordinate with:

- National governments.
- Local authorities.
- United Nations agencies.
- International organizations.
- NGOs.
- Community-based organizations.
- Faith-based organizations.
- Donors.

---

# 11. Safeguarding and Protection

All staff, volunteers and partners shall comply with TJF safeguarding policies.

Measures include:

- Prevention of sexual exploitation and abuse.
- Child safeguarding.
- Confidential complaint mechanisms.
- Staff vetting and training.
- Survivor-centered response procedures.

---

# 12. Accountability to Affected Populations

Beneficiaries shall have access to:

- Information about available services.
- Feedback mechanisms.
- Complaint channels.
- Participation in programme design.
- Programme evaluation processes.

Complaints shall be addressed promptly and confidentially.

---

# 13. Monitoring and Evaluation

Programme effectiveness shall be monitored through:

- Protection indicators.
- Service coverage.
- Beneficiary satisfaction.
- Outcome evaluations.
- Community feedback.
- Independent assessments where appropriate.

---

- Strategic oversight.

# 16. Review

This Framework shall be reviewed:

- Every three years.
- Following major displacement emergencies.
- After significant programme evaluations.
- Following changes in international protection standards.

---

# Conclusion

The Jopetnah Foundation is committed to ensuring that refugees, displaced persons and vulnerable populations are treated with dignity, protected from harm and supported in rebuilding safe, resilient and productive lives.

Through inclusive programming, strong partnerships and community engagement, TJF contributes to humanitarian protection while promoting long-term recovery, self-reliance and social cohesion.

---

> **Every displaced person deserves safety, dignity, opportunity and hope for a better future.**
