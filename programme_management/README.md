# Programme Management, Community Development & Impact Delivery

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) exists to create sustainable social impact through well-designed, effectively implemented and evidence-based programmes.

This Programme Management Repository establishes the frameworks, policies and operational standards required to ensure that TJF programmes are relevant, inclusive, accountable and capable of delivering measurable improvements in the lives of communities.

The repository provides guidance for programme identification, design, implementation, monitoring, evaluation, learning, community engagement and impact measurement.

---

# Purpose

This framework aims to:

- Strengthen programme quality.
- Standardize programme management practices.
- Improve accountability to communities and partners.
- Promote evidence-based interventions.
- Enhance programme sustainability.
- Support effective resource utilization.

---

# Scope

This framework applies to all TJF programmes, projects and initiatives including:

- Community development programmes.
- Education initiatives.
- Healthcare interventions.
- Livelihood programmes.
- Youth empowerment activities.
- Environmental programmes.
- Humanitarian responses.
- Research and innovation projects.
- Partnership-funded programmes.

---

# Programme Management Principles

TJF programmes shall be guided by:

## Community-Centred Approach

Communities are active participants in identifying needs, designing solutions and evaluating impact.

---

## Results-Based Management

Programmes shall focus on measurable outcomes and sustainable change.

---

## Inclusion and Equity

Programmes shall promote equal opportunities and participation.

---

## Evidence-Based Programming

Decisions should be informed by research, data and learning.

---

## Accountability

TJF shall remain accountable to communities, partners, donors and stakeholders.

---

## Sustainability

Programmes should create lasting benefits beyond project timelines.

# Programme Lifecycle

TJF programmes shall follow a structured lifecycle:

## 1. Needs Identification

Understanding community challenges through:

- Research.
- Consultation.
- Assessments.
- Stakeholder engagement.

---

## 2. Programme Design

Developing:

- Objectives.
- Activities.
- Budgets.
- Implementation plans.
- Monitoring frameworks.

---

## 3. Implementation

Delivering activities through:

- Qualified teams.
- Community partnerships.
- Effective resource management.

---

## 4. Monitoring

Tracking:

- Progress.
- Outputs.
- Outcomes.
- Risks.
- Learning.

---

## 5. Evaluation

Assessing:

- Effectiveness.
- Efficiency.
- Impact.
- Sustainability.

---

## 6. Learning and Improvement

Using evidence to:

- Improve programmes.
- Share knowledge.
- Scale successful approaches.
