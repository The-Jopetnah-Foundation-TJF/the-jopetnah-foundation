# Environmental Sustainability Policy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that environmental sustainability is essential to achieving long-term social and economic development.

Climate change, environmental degradation and unsustainable resource use disproportionately affect vulnerable communities and threaten sustainable progress.

This Environmental Sustainability Policy establishes TJF’s commitment to protecting the environment, promoting climate resilience and integrating sustainable practices across programmes, operations and partnerships.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Policy aims to:

- Promote environmental responsibility.
- Reduce negative environmental impacts.
- Support climate-resilient communities.
- Encourage sustainable programme design.
- Improve responsible resource management.
- Promote environmental awareness.

---

# 2. Scope

This Policy applies to:

- All TJF programmes.
- Field operations.
- Offices and facilities.
- Procurement activities.
- Partnerships.
- Events and campaigns.
- Infrastructure projects.

It applies to:

- Board members.
- Employees.
- Volunteers.
- Consultants.
- Contractors.
- Partners.

---

# 3. Environmental Commitment

TJF commits to:

- Protecting natural resources.
- Reducing environmental harm.
- Supporting sustainable communities.
- Promoting responsible consumption.
- Encouraging climate action.
- Integrating environmental considerations into decision-making.
