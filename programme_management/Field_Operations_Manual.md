# Field Operations Manual

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) delivers programmes through direct community engagement, field activities and partnerships.

Effective field operations require clear procedures, professional conduct, safety practices, accurate documentation and strong accountability systems.

This Field Operations Manual establishes the standards and procedures that guide TJF field teams in implementing programmes effectively while maintaining quality, safety and respect for communities.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Manual aims to:

- Standardize field operations.
- Strengthen programme implementation.
- Guide field staff responsibilities.
- Promote safety and accountability.
- Improve reporting and documentation.
- Support quality service delivery.

---

# 2. Scope

This Manual applies to:

- Programme officers.
- Field coordinators.
- Community facilitators.
- Volunteers.
- Consultants.
- Partner field teams.

It covers:

- Field planning.
- Community engagement.
- Activity implementation.
- Monitoring.
- Reporting.
- Safety management.

---

# 3. Field Operations Principles

TJF field operations shall follow:

---

## Professionalism

Field teams must demonstrate:

- Integrity.
- Respect.
- Responsibility.
- Accountability.

---

## Community Respect

Field teams must recognize:

- Local knowledge.
- Cultural practices.
- Community priorities.

---

## Safety First

All activities must prioritize:

- Staff safety.
- Community safety.
- Protection of assets.

---

## Accountability

Field teams must maintain:

- Accurate records.
- Transparent communication.
- Responsible resource use.

---

# 4. Field Team Structure

## Programme Manager

Responsibilities:

- Provides operational leadership.
- Oversees programme implementation.
- Reviews progress.

---

## Field Coordinator

Responsibilities:

- Coordinates field activities.
- Supervises field teams.
- Manages schedules.

---

## Field Officers

Responsibilities:

- Conduct community activities.
- Collect information.
- Support beneficiaries.
- Prepare reports.

---

## Community Volunteers

Responsibilities:

- Support local engagement.
- Assist programme activities.
- Promote community participation.

---

# 5. Field Planning Procedures

Before field activities, teams should prepare:

- Activity plan.
- Objectives.
- Target participants.
- Location details.
- Required resources.
- Risk considerations.

---

# 6. Community Entry Procedures

Before starting activities:

Field teams should:

- Introduce TJF.
- Engage local leaders.
- Explain programme objectives.
- Confirm community expectations.
- Establish communication channels.

---

# 7. Field Activity Implementation

Activities should follow approved:

- Work plans.
- Budgets.
- Programme designs.
- Safeguarding requirements.

Field teams must document:

- Date.
- Location.
- Participants.
- Activities completed.
- Outcomes achieved.

---

# 8. Beneficiary Interaction Standards

Field teams must:

- Treat beneficiaries with dignity.
- Avoid discrimination.
- Maintain confidentiality.
- Provide accurate information.
- Encourage feedback.

---

# 9. Field Data Collection

Data collection should ensure:

- Accuracy.
- Consent.
- Confidentiality.
- Secure storage.

Methods may include:

- Interviews.
- Surveys.
- Registration forms.
- Observation.

---

# 10. Field Reporting Requirements

Field reports should include:

## Activity Information

- Date.
- Location.
- Participants.
- Activities conducted.

---

## Progress Information

- Achievements.
- Challenges.
- Recommendations.

---

## Evidence

Where appropriate:

- Photos.
- Attendance records.
- Supporting documents.

All evidence must respect privacy requirements.

---

# 11. Field Logistics Management

Field teams may coordinate:

- Transport.
- Equipment.
- Supplies.
- Accommodation.
- Communication resources.

All resources must be used responsibly.

---

# 12. Field Safety and Security

Before deployment, teams should consider:

- Location risks.
- Weather conditions.
- Travel safety.
- Community tensions.
- Emergency contacts.

---

# 13. Incident Reporting

Field incidents should be reported including:

- Accidents.
- Security concerns.
- Safeguarding issues.
- Loss of equipment.
- Community complaints.

---

# 14. Monitoring Field Performance

Supervision should assess:

- Activity progress.
- Quality of delivery.
- Community satisfaction.
- Compliance with procedures.

---

# 15. Field Visits and Supervision

Supervisors should conduct:

- Routine field visits.
- Quality checks.
- Staff support sessions.
- Programme reviews.

---

# 16. Equipment and Asset Management

Field teams are responsible for:

- Proper use of equipment.
- Security of assets.
- Reporting damage or loss.

---

# 17. Communication Standards

Field communication should be:

- Accurate.
- Respectful.
- Professional.

Public communication must follow TJF communication guidelines.

---

# 18. Gender and Inclusion in Field Operations

Field teams must ensure:

- Equal participation.
- Accessible activities.
- Respectful engagement.
- Protection of vulnerable groups.

---

# 19. Environmental Responsibility

Field operations should:

- Minimize waste.
- Protect local environments.
- Use resources responsibly.

---
