# Safeguarding Policy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) is committed to creating safe environments where all individuals are treated with dignity, respect and protection from harm.

As an organization working with communities, vulnerable groups, children, families and partners, TJF recognizes its responsibility to prevent abuse, exploitation, neglect, harassment and any form of misconduct.

This Safeguarding Policy establishes the principles, standards and procedures required to protect beneficiaries, employees, volunteers, partners and stakeholders involved in TJF activities.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Policy aims to:

- Prevent harm and abuse.
- Protect vulnerable individuals.
- Promote safe programme delivery.
- Establish reporting mechanisms.
- Define safeguarding responsibilities.
- Ensure accountability for misconduct.

---

# 2. Scope

This Policy applies to:

- Board members.
- Employees.
- Volunteers.
- Interns.
- Consultants.
- Contractors.
- Partners.
- Visitors.
- Community representatives.

It applies to all TJF activities including:

- Programme implementation.
- Field operations.
- Training activities.
- Research.
- Community engagement.
- Partnerships.

---

# 3. Safeguarding Commitment

TJF commits to:

- Doing no harm.
- Respecting human dignity.
- Preventing exploitation and abuse.
- Protecting vulnerable persons.
- Responding appropriately to concerns.
- Maintaining confidentiality.
- Promoting safe organizational culture.

---

# 4. Safeguarding Principles

## Respect and Dignity

Every person shall be treated with:

- Respect.
- Fairness.
- Compassion.
- Professionalism.

---

## Zero Tolerance for Abuse

TJF does not tolerate:

- Abuse.
- Exploitation.
- Harassment.
- Discrimination.
- Violence.
- Neglect.

---

## Survivor-Centred Approach

Individuals reporting harm shall receive:

- Respect.
- Confidentiality.
- Support.
- Protection from retaliation.

---

## Accountability

Everyone associated with TJF has a responsibility to prevent and report safeguarding concerns.

---

# 5. Types of Safeguarding Risks

Safeguarding concerns may include:

## Physical Abuse

Intentional actions causing physical harm.

---

## Emotional Abuse

Actions causing psychological harm, intimidation or humiliation.

---

## Sexual Exploitation and Abuse

Any misuse of power for sexual purposes.

---

## Neglect

Failure to provide appropriate care or protection.

---

## Exploitation

Taking unfair advantage of vulnerable individuals.

---

## Harassment

Unwanted behaviour causing fear, distress or harm.

---

## Discrimination

Unfair treatment based on personal characteristics or status.

---

# 6. Code of Conduct Requirements

All TJF representatives must:

- Behave professionally.
- Respect community members.
- Avoid conflicts of interest.
- Maintain appropriate boundaries.
- Protect confidential information.
- Report safeguarding concerns.

---

# 7. Prohibited Behaviours

TJF representatives must never:

- Abuse their position of authority.
- Exploit beneficiaries.
- Engage in sexual relationships with beneficiaries where power imbalance exists.
- Request inappropriate favours.
- Use degrading language.
- Ignore safeguarding concerns.

---

# 8. Safeguarding in Programme Design

All programmes should consider:

- Potential risks.
- Vulnerable groups.
- Safe participation methods.
- Accessibility requirements.
- Protection measures.

Safeguarding risks should be included in programme risk assessments.

---

# 9. Safe Recruitment Practices

TJF shall promote safeguarding through:

- Reference checks.
- Appropriate screening.
- Safeguarding commitments.
- Staff orientation.
- Code of Conduct acceptance.

---

# 10. Training and Awareness

TJF shall provide training on:

- Safeguarding principles.
- Reporting procedures.
- Prevention of abuse.
- Professional conduct.
- Community protection.

---

# 11. Reporting Safeguarding Concerns

Concerns may be reported through:

- Supervisors.
- Safeguarding focal persons.
- Management channels.
- Confidential reporting systems.

Reports should include:

- Nature of concern.
- Date and location.
- Individuals involved.
- Available evidence.

---

# 12. Confidentiality and Information Protection

Safeguarding information must be:

- Stored securely.
- Accessed only by authorized persons.
- Shared only when necessary.

Confidentiality must be balanced with legal and protection responsibilities.

---

# 13. Response Procedures

When a concern is reported, TJF shall:

## Step 1: Receive

Listen respectfully and document information.

---

## Step 2: Assess

Determine urgency and required action.

---

## Step 3: Protect

Ensure immediate safety where required.

---

## Step 4: Refer

Connect affected individuals with appropriate support services.

---

## Step 5: Investigate

Conduct fair and confidential review processes.

---

## Step 6: Respond

Apply appropriate corrective measures.

---

# 14. Safeguarding Focal Person

TJF shall designate safeguarding focal persons responsible for:

- Receiving concerns.
- Providing guidance.
- Supporting awareness activities.
- Coordinating responses.

---

# 15. Partner Safeguarding Requirements

Partners must:

- Meet TJF safeguarding standards.
- Train relevant personnel.
- Report safeguarding concerns.
- Maintain safe practices.

---

# 16. Safeguarding Monitoring

Indicators may include:

| Indicator | Measurement |
|---|---|
| Staff trained | Percentage |
| Safeguarding reports received | Number |
| Cases addressed | Percentage |
| Programme risk assessments completed | Percentage |
| Community awareness activities | Number |

---

# 17. Disciplinary Measures

Violations may result in:

- Warning.
- Training requirements.
- Suspension.
- Termination.
- Referral to authorities where appropriate.

---

# 18. Review

This Policy shall be reviewed:

- Every three years.
- Following safeguarding incidents.
- Following changes in legal or international standards.

---

# Conclusion

Safeguarding is a fundamental responsibility of The Jopetnah Foundation.

By promoting safe environments, ethical behaviour and accountability, TJF protects the dignity, rights and wellbeing of everyone connected to its mission.

---

> **Every person deserves to participate in a safe environment where dignity, respect and protection are guaranteed.**
