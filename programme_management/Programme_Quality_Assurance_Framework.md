# Programme Quality Assurance Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) is committed to delivering programmes that create meaningful, measurable and sustainable impact.

Quality assurance ensures that programmes are designed effectively, implemented responsibly, monitored consistently and improved continuously.

This Programme Quality Assurance Framework establishes TJF’s standards, processes and responsibilities for maintaining excellence across all programme activities.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Establish programme quality standards.
- Strengthen implementation effectiveness.
- Promote continuous improvement.
- Improve accountability.
- Support evidence-based decision-making.
- Enhance community impact.

---

# 2. Scope

This Framework applies to:

- All TJF programmes.
- Projects.
- Community interventions.
- Emergency responses.
- Partnerships.
- Grant-funded initiatives.

It applies to:

- Programme teams.
- MEAL personnel.
- Management.
- Partners.

---

# 3. Quality Assurance Principles

TJF quality assurance is guided by:

---

## Excellence

Striving for high standards in programme delivery.

---

## Effectiveness

Ensuring programmes achieve intended outcomes.

---

## Efficiency

Maximizing responsible use of resources.

---

## Accountability

Maintaining transparency with communities, partners and donors.

---

## Learning

Using evidence to improve future programmes.

# 5. Quality Standards

TJF programmes should demonstrate:

---

## Relevance

Programmes respond to real community needs.

Assessment includes:

- Needs analysis.
- Stakeholder feedback.
- Community priorities.

---

## Effectiveness

Programmes achieve planned objectives.

Measured through:

- Outcomes.
- Indicators.
- Beneficiary results.

---

## Efficiency

Resources are managed responsibly.

Consider:

- Budget utilization.
- Operational performance.
- Cost effectiveness.

---

## Sustainability

Benefits continue beyond programme support.

Consider:

- Local ownership.
- Capacity building.
- Long-term solutions.

---

## Impact

Programmes contribute to meaningful change.

Measured through:

- Social outcomes.
- Economic improvements.
- Community transformation.

---

# 6. Quality Assurance Process

## Stage 1: Programme Design Review

Before approval, TJF reviews:

- Needs assessment.
- Objectives.
- Theory of Change.
- Budget.
- Risks.
- Monitoring framework.

---

## Stage 2: Implementation Quality Monitoring

During implementation, TJF monitors:

- Activity progress.
- Resource utilization.
- Community participation.
- Compliance.

---

## Stage 3: Programme Review

Reviews may assess:

- Performance.
- Challenges.
- Lessons learned.
- Required improvements.

---

## Stage 4: Programme Evaluation

Evaluations may examine:

- Relevance.
- Effectiveness.
- Efficiency.
- Sustainability.
- Impact.

---

# 8. Monitoring and Evaluation Integration

Quality assurance works closely with MEAL systems.

Key areas include:

- Indicator tracking.
- Data verification.
- Outcome measurement.
- Learning documentation.

---

# 9. Programme Compliance Reviews

Reviews should assess compliance with:

- TJF policies.
- Donor requirements.
- Legal requirements.
- Safeguarding standards.
- Financial procedures.

---

# 10. Programme Risk Management

Quality assurance includes identifying:

- Operational risks.
- Financial risks.
- Safeguarding risks.
- Environmental risks.
- Partnership risks.

Risk mitigation plans should be maintained.

---

# 11. Community Accountability

Quality programmes require community involvement through:

- Feedback systems.
- Satisfaction surveys.
- Community consultations.
- Complaint mechanisms.

---

# 12. Partner Quality Assurance

Partners implementing TJF programmes must demonstrate:

- Technical capacity.
- Ethical standards.
- Reporting capability.
- Compliance with TJF policies.

---

# 13. Programme Improvement Process

When gaps are identified, TJF shall:

1. Identify the issue.
2. Analyse the cause.
3. Develop improvement actions.
4. Assign responsibility.
5. Monitor progress.
