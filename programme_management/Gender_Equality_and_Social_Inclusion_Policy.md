# Gender Equality and Social Inclusion Policy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) believes that sustainable development is only possible when all people have equal opportunities to participate, contribute and benefit from social and economic progress.

Gender inequality, discrimination, disability barriers and social exclusion can prevent individuals and communities from achieving their full potential.

This Gender Equality and Social Inclusion (GESI) Policy establishes TJF’s commitment to promoting equality, diversity, dignity, accessibility and inclusive participation across all programmes, operations and partnerships.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Policy aims to:

- Promote gender equality.
- Strengthen social inclusion.
- Prevent discrimination.
- Increase participation of marginalized groups.
- Improve accessibility of programmes.
- Support equitable development outcomes.

---

# 2. Scope

This Policy applies to:

- Board members.
- Employees.
- Volunteers.
- Consultants.
- Partners.
- Programme participants.
- Communities engaged by TJF.

It applies across:

- Programme design.
- Implementation.
- Monitoring.
- Workplace practices.
- Partnerships.
- Communications.

---

# 3. Policy Commitment

TJF commits to:

- Respecting human dignity.
- Promoting equal opportunity.
- Challenging discrimination.
- Removing barriers to participation.
- Supporting vulnerable and marginalized groups.
- Integrating inclusion into decision-making.

# 4. Key Definitions

## Gender Equality

Equal rights, responsibilities and opportunities for all genders.

---

## Social Inclusion

Ensuring that individuals and groups have meaningful participation in society and access to opportunities.

---

## Marginalized Groups

Individuals or communities who experience barriers due to social, economic, physical or cultural factors.
