Design note · 24 October 2026

Designing Jopetnah Scholars: support that follows the learner

Jopetnah Scholars is our proposed education pathway for learners who face documented financial barriers to secondary or tertiary education. In design, we are working to three principles:

  • Fund the whole journey. Support is sized to what we can fund through a learner’s full school cycle, so no one is left half-way.
  • Pay schools directly. Costs go to the school, not through households, to keep the support accountable.
  • Pair money with mentoring. Monthly mentoring, study skills and career guidance, with follow-up for the learner and their caregiver.

This is a design note, not a result. The pathway has not launched, and no learners have been selected or supported.
