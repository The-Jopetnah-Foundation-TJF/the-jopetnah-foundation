# ICT Asset Management Policy

## Executive Summary

Information and Communication Technology (ICT) assets are essential resources that enable The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) to deliver programmes, communicate with stakeholders and operate efficiently.

This ICT Asset Management Policy establishes the procedures and responsibilities for acquiring, recording, using, maintaining, protecting, transferring and disposing of ICT assets throughout their lifecycle.

The Policy promotes accountability, cost efficiency, security and responsible stewardship of organizational resources.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Policy aims to:

- Establish effective ICT asset management practices.
- Maintain accurate asset records.
- Protect technology investments.
- Improve accountability.
- Support cybersecurity controls.
- Optimize asset utilization.
- Ensure responsible disposal of obsolete equipment.

---

# 2. Scope

This Policy applies to all TJF ICT assets including:

## Hardware

- Desktop computers.
- Laptops.
- Servers.
- Mobile devices.
- Tablets.
- Printers.
- Networking equipment.
- Storage devices.
- Security equipment.

---

## Software

- Operating systems.
- Applications.
- Enterprise software.
- Licences.
- Cloud subscriptions.

---

## Digital Resources

- Cloud platforms.
- Domains.
- Digital accounts.
- Online services.
- Technology subscriptions.

---

# 3. ICT Asset Management Principles

TJF ICT asset management shall follow:

## Accountability

Every ICT asset must have a responsible custodian.

---

## Security

Assets must be protected from unauthorized access, damage or misuse.

---

## Lifecycle Management

Assets must be managed from acquisition through disposal.

---

## Cost Effectiveness

Technology investments should provide organizational value.

---

## Transparency

Asset records must be accurate and auditable.

---

# 5. Asset Acquisition

ICT assets shall be acquired through approved procurement processes.

Before acquisition, TJF should assess:

- Business need.
- Technical requirements.
- Security considerations.
- Budget availability.
- Compatibility.
- Long-term sustainability.

# 7. Asset Ownership and Custody

All ICT assets remain the property of TJF unless formally transferred.

Users assigned assets are responsible for:

- Proper use.
- Security protection.
- Reporting damage.
- Preventing loss.

---

# 8. Asset Security Requirements

Users must:

- Protect devices from theft.
- Use passwords.
- Apply security updates.
- Avoid unauthorized modifications.
- Store equipment securely.

---

# 9. Software Asset Management

Software management shall include:

- Licence tracking.
- Approved software lists.
- Renewal monitoring.
- Security updates.
- Removal of unauthorized software.

TJF shall not use illegal or unlicensed software.

---

# 10. Mobile Device Management

Mobile devices accessing TJF information should have:

- Security controls.
- Screen protection.
- Approved applications.
- Remote management where appropriate.

Lost devices must be reported immediately.

---

# 11. Maintenance and Support

ICT assets should receive:

- Preventive maintenance.
- Security updates.
- Repairs.
- Performance monitoring.

Maintenance activities should be documented.

---

# 12. Asset Inventory Review

The ICT Department shall conduct periodic asset reviews to verify:

- Asset existence.
- User assignment.
- Condition.
- Location.
- Compliance status.

---

# 13. Asset Transfer

When assets are transferred:

Documentation should include:

- Previous custodian.
- New custodian.
- Transfer date.
- Condition assessment.

---

# 14. Asset Loss or Damage

Users must immediately report:

- Lost devices.
- Theft.
- Accidental damage.
- Unauthorized access.

The IT Department shall assess risks and required actions.

---

# 15. Disposal of ICT Assets

Before disposal:

- Data must be securely erased.
- Storage devices must be sanitized.
- Disposal must follow environmental requirements.

Disposal methods may include:

- Recycling.
- Certified destruction.
- Donation where appropriate.

---

# 16. Cloud and Subscription Management

Cloud services should be managed through:

- Approved accounts.
- Licence tracking.
- Renewal monitoring.
- Access reviews.
- Security assessments.

---

- Access restrictions.
- Recovery of assets.
- Disciplinary action.
- Contractual consequences.
