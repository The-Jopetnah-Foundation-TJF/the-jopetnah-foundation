# Information Technology, Digital Transformation & Knowledge Management

## Executive Summary

Technology is a strategic enabler of The Jopetnah Foundation's mission. Reliable digital systems improve programme delivery, organizational efficiency, accountability, collaboration and evidence-based decision-making.

This repository establishes the governance framework for managing information technology, cybersecurity, digital transformation and organizational knowledge in a secure, responsible and sustainable manner.

---

# Objectives

This framework aims to:

- Strengthen IT governance and oversight.
- Protect information assets.
- Improve cybersecurity resilience.
- Support digital innovation.
- Enable effective knowledge sharing.
- Ensure business continuity.
- Promote responsible adoption of emerging technologies, including Artificial Intelligence (AI).
- Improve operational efficiency through digital transformation.

---

# Scope

This framework applies to:

- Board of Trustees.
- Executive leadership.
- Employees.
- Volunteers.
- Consultants.
- Contractors.
- Interns.
- Third-party technology service providers with access to TJF systems or information.

It covers:

- Hardware and software.
- Networks and cloud services.
- Email and collaboration platforms.
- Digital records.
- Data protection.
- Cybersecurity.
- Artificial intelligence.
- Knowledge management.
- ICT procurement and asset management.
- Business continuity for technology services.

---

# Guiding Principles

Technology within TJF should be:

- Secure.
- Reliable.
- Accessible.
- Ethical.
- Innovative.
- Cost-effective.
- User-centred.
- Compliant with applicable laws and organizational policies.

# Governance

The Board of Trustees provides strategic oversight for technology governance.

The Executive Director is accountable for organizational implementation.

The Information Technology Department is responsible for day-to-day management of digital infrastructure, cybersecurity, technology projects and user support.

All staff share responsibility for protecting information assets and complying with technology policies.

---

# Continuous Improvement

The technology governance framework should be reviewed regularly to address:

- Emerging cyber threats.
- Changes in technology.
- Regulatory developments.
- Lessons learned from incidents.
- Organizational growth and changing operational needs.

---

# Conclusion

Modern, secure and well-governed technology is essential to achieving TJF's mission.

By implementing this framework, The Jopetnah Foundation strengthens operational resilience, safeguards information, enables innovation and ensures that technology continues to support sustainable development and organizational excellence.

---

> **Technology delivers its greatest value when it is secure, trusted and used to empower people, strengthen organizations and improve lives.**
