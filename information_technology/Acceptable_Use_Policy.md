# Acceptable Use Policy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) provides technology resources to support organizational operations, programme delivery, communication and collaboration.

This Acceptable Use Policy establishes the rules and responsibilities governing the appropriate use of TJF technology resources. It ensures that digital systems are used securely, ethically and effectively while protecting organizational information and reputation.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Policy aims to:

- Promote responsible technology use.
- Protect TJF information systems.
- Reduce cybersecurity risks.
- Prevent misuse of digital resources.
- Define user responsibilities.
- Support compliance with organizational policies.

---

# 2. Scope

This Policy applies to:

- Board members.
- Employees.
- Volunteers.
- Interns.
- Consultants.
- Contractors.
- Partner personnel with authorized access.

It applies to:

- Computers.
- Laptops.
- Mobile devices.
- Email accounts.
- Internet access.
- Cloud platforms.
- Applications.
- Networks.
- Digital communication tools.

---

# 3. Acceptable Use Principles

Users must ensure that technology resources are:

- Used for legitimate organizational purposes.
- Protected from unauthorized access.
- Used in compliance with applicable laws.
- Managed responsibly.
- Used in a manner consistent with TJF values.

---

# 4. User Responsibilities

All users must:

- Protect TJF devices and accounts.
- Maintain confidentiality.
- Follow security requirements.
- Report incidents promptly.
- Use approved systems.
- Respect other users.

---

# 5. Account Security

Users must:

- Keep passwords confidential.
- Never share login credentials.
- Use strong passwords.
- Enable multi-factor authentication where required.
- Lock devices when unattended.
- Report suspected account compromise.

---

# 6. Computer and Device Use

Users must:

- Use approved TJF devices appropriately.
- Install only authorized software.
- Apply required security updates.
- Protect devices from damage or theft.
- Report lost or stolen equipment immediately.

Users must not:

- Disable security controls.
- Modify system configurations without authorization.
- Install unauthorized applications.

---

# 7. Email Use

TJF email systems should be used professionally.

Users must:

- Verify recipients before sending messages.
- Protect confidential information.
- Avoid suspicious links and attachments.
- Use professional communication standards.

Users must not:

- Send harmful content.
- Conduct fraudulent activities.
- Impersonate others.
- Distribute unauthorized bulk messages.

---

# 8. Internet Usage

Internet access is provided for organizational purposes.

Users should:

- Use online resources responsibly.
- Avoid unsafe websites.
- Protect confidential information.

Users must not use TJF systems for:

- Illegal activities.
- Cybersecurity attacks.
- Harassment.
- Hate speech.
- Fraud.
- Unauthorized commercial activities.

---

# 9. Social Media Use

Users representing TJF online must:

- Follow approved communication guidelines.
- Protect organizational reputation.
- Avoid sharing confidential information.
- Clearly distinguish personal opinions from official TJF positions.

Only authorized personnel may publish official TJF statements.

---

# 10. Cloud Services

Users accessing cloud systems must:

- Use approved platforms.
- Protect shared files.
- Follow access permissions.
- Avoid unauthorized sharing.

Confidential documents must only be stored in approved locations.

---

# 11. Data Protection Responsibilities

Users must:

- Collect only necessary information.
- Protect personal data.
- Avoid unauthorized disclosure.
- Secure sensitive documents.

Users must follow TJF's Data Protection and Privacy Policy.

---

# 12. Remote Work Requirements

Remote users must:

- Secure their workspace.
- Protect TJF equipment.
- Avoid public exposure of confidential information.
- Use approved communication systems.
- Report security concerns.

---

# 13. Prohibited Activities

Users must not:

## Security Violations

- Access systems without authorization.
- Attempt to bypass security controls.
- Introduce malware.
- Conduct unauthorized testing.

---

## Information Misuse

- Copy confidential information without approval.
- Share restricted documents improperly.
- Remove organizational data without authorization.

---

## Unacceptable Behaviour

- Harassment.
- Discrimination.
- Threatening communication.
- Misuse of organizational platforms.

---

## Illegal Activities

- Fraud.
- Copyright infringement.
- Unauthorized financial activities.
- Any activity prohibited by law.

---

# 14. Personal Use

Limited personal use may be permitted where it:

- Does not interfere with work.
- Does not create security risks.
- Does not consume excessive resources.
- Does not violate this Policy.

---

# 15. Monitoring and Privacy

TJF may monitor technology usage to:

- Protect systems.
- Investigate security incidents.
- Ensure compliance.
- Improve services.

Monitoring shall be conducted responsibly and in accordance with applicable privacy requirements.

---

# 16. Reporting Violations

Users should report:

- Security concerns.
- Suspected misuse.
- Lost devices.
- Data exposure.
- Policy violations.

Reports should be made to:

- Information Technology Department.
- Supervisor.
- Appropriate management channel.

---

# 17. Enforcement

Failure to comply with this Policy may result in:

- Access restrictions.
- Additional training.
- Disciplinary action.
- Termination of access privileges.
- Contractual consequences.

Serious violations may result in further legal action where appropriate.

---

# 18. Third-Party Access

External users accessing TJF systems must:

- Receive authorization.
- Follow security requirements.
- Protect TJF information.
- Remove access when no longer required.

---

# 19. Training and Awareness

TJF shall provide awareness activities covering:

- Cybersecurity.
- Safe technology practices.
- Data protection.
- Responsible communication.

---

# 20. Review

This Policy shall be reviewed:

- Every two years.
- Following major technology changes.
- After significant security incidents.
- When organizational requirements change.

---

# Conclusion

Responsible use of technology protects TJF's mission, operations and stakeholders.

By following this Acceptable Use Policy, every user contributes to a secure, productive and trustworthy digital environment.

---

> **Technology is a shared responsibility. Every secure action protects the mission we serve.**
