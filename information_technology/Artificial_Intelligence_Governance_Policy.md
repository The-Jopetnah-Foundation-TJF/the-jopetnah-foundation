# Artificial Intelligence Governance Policy

## Executive Summary

Artificial Intelligence (AI) presents significant opportunities for The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) to improve efficiency, strengthen decision-making, enhance research, expand communication and increase programme impact.

However, AI must be adopted responsibly to protect human rights, privacy, organizational integrity and stakeholder trust.

This Artificial Intelligence Governance Policy establishes the principles, safeguards and responsibilities required for the ethical, secure and effective use of AI technologies across TJF.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Policy aims to:

- Promote responsible AI adoption.
- Ensure human oversight of AI-supported decisions.
- Protect sensitive information.
- Reduce AI-related risks.
- Encourage innovation.
- Establish accountability for AI use.
- Align AI applications with TJF values and mission.

---

# 2. Scope

This Policy applies to:

- Board members.
- Employees.
- Volunteers.
- Consultants.
- Interns.
- Contractors.
- Third-party providers using AI solutions for TJF activities.

It covers:

- Generative AI tools.
- Automated systems.
- Machine learning applications.
- AI-powered analytics.
- AI-assisted content creation.
- AI-enabled decision support systems.

---

# 3. Definition of Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence refers to technologies that enable computer systems to perform tasks normally requiring human intelligence, including:

- Learning from data.
- Generating content.
- Recognizing patterns.
- Making predictions.
- Supporting decisions.

---

# 4. AI Governance Principles

TJF's AI use shall follow these principles:

---

## Human Oversight

AI must support human judgement, not replace responsible decision-making.

Critical decisions affecting people must include appropriate human review.

---

## Transparency

Users should understand when AI is being used and its limitations.

---

## Accountability

Individuals and departments remain responsible for outcomes produced with AI assistance.

---

## Privacy Protection

Personal and confidential information must be protected when using AI systems.

---

## Fairness and Inclusion

AI systems should avoid discrimination, bias or unfair outcomes.

---

## Security

AI tools must be used securely to protect organizational information.

---

## Purpose Alignment

AI applications must support TJF's mission and strategic objectives.

---

# 5. Approved AI Uses

AI may support activities such as:

## Administrative Support

Examples:

- Drafting documents.
- Summarizing information.
- Improving workflows.
- Supporting scheduling.

---

## Research and Analysis

Examples:

- Data analysis support.
- Literature review assistance.
- Trend identification.

---

## Communication

Examples:

- Content development.
- Translation assistance.
- Communication planning.

---

## Programme Support

Examples:

- Improving service design.
- Analysing programme information.
- Supporting reporting processes.

---

## Learning and Training

Examples:

- Educational materials.
- Staff development resources.
- Knowledge organization.

---

# 6. Restricted AI Uses

AI must not be used to:

- Make fully automated decisions affecting individuals without human review.
- Process sensitive information without authorization.
- Replace professional judgement in critical matters.
- Generate misleading information intentionally.
- Conduct unauthorized surveillance.
- Circumvent organizational controls.

---

# 7. Data Protection Requirements

Before using AI tools, users must consider:

- What information is being entered.
- Where data is processed.
- Who can access information.
- Whether confidential information is protected.

Users must not upload:

- Beneficiary personal data.
- Confidential employee information.
- Sensitive partner information.
- Restricted financial information.

unless explicitly approved.

---

# 8. AI Risk Assessment

Before implementing significant AI solutions, TJF should assess:

- Purpose.
- Benefits.
- Risks.
- Data requirements.
- Security implications.
- Ethical considerations.
- Human oversight requirements.

---

# 9. AI Generated Content

AI-generated content must be:

- Reviewed by responsible personnel.
- Fact-checked.
- Edited where necessary.
- Consistent with TJF values.

AI should not be used to create false information or misleading representations.

---

# 10. Intellectual Property

Users must consider:

- Ownership rights.
- Copyright requirements.
- Licensing restrictions.
- Attribution obligations.

AI-generated materials should be reviewed before external publication.

---

# 11. AI in Research and Data Analysis

AI-supported research must maintain:

- Methodological quality.
- Data protection.
- Research ethics.
- Transparency regarding AI assistance.

AI outputs should not replace professional analysis.

---

# 12. Vendor and Third-Party AI Tools

Before using external AI services, TJF should assess:

- Vendor reputation.
- Data security practices.
- Privacy commitments.
- Contractual obligations.
- Compliance requirements.

---

# 13. Roles and Responsibilities

## Executive Director

Responsible for:

- Strategic AI oversight.
- Approving significant AI initiatives.
- Ensuring responsible adoption.

---

## Information Technology Department

Responsible for:

- AI technology assessment.
- Security review.
- Approved tool management.
- User guidance.

---

## Department Heads

Responsible for:

- Ensuring appropriate AI use.
- Managing AI-related risks.
- Supporting staff compliance.

---

## All Users

Responsible for:

- Using AI responsibly.
- Protecting confidential information.
- Reviewing AI outputs.
- Reporting concerns.

---

# 14. AI Training and Awareness

TJF shall provide guidance on:

- Responsible AI use.
- Data protection.
- AI limitations.
- Verification of AI outputs.
- Ethical considerations.

---

# 15. Monitoring and Evaluation

AI adoption may be measured through:

| Indicator | Measurement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Approved AI tools | Number |
| Staff trained | Percentage |
| AI projects implemented | Number |
| Efficiency improvements | Assessment |
| AI risks identified | Number |

---

# 16. AI Incident Management

AI-related incidents may include:

- Unauthorized disclosure.
- Incorrect outputs.
- Bias concerns.
- Security failures.
- Misuse of AI systems.

Incidents should be reported through established security channels.

---

# 17. Review

This Policy shall be reviewed:

- Every two years.
- Following major AI developments.
- After significant AI-related incidents.
- When regulatory requirements change.

---

# Conclusion

Artificial Intelligence can significantly enhance TJF's ability to innovate, learn and deliver greater impact.

By applying responsible governance, human oversight and ethical safeguards, The Jopetnah Foundation ensures that AI serves people, strengthens trust and advances sustainable development.

---

> **Responsible Artificial Intelligence combines innovation with wisdom, ensuring technology remains a force for positive human impact.**
