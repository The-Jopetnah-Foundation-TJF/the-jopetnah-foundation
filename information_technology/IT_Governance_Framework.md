# IT Governance Framework

## Executive Summary

Information Technology (IT) is a critical enabler of The Jopetnah Foundation's (TJF) mission, operational effectiveness and long-term sustainability.

This IT Governance Framework establishes the structures, principles and processes required to ensure that technology resources are managed strategically, securely and responsibly.

The Framework ensures that ICT investments support TJF's strategic objectives while maintaining appropriate controls over cybersecurity, information management, compliance, service delivery and digital innovation.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Establish effective IT governance structures.
- Align technology investments with TJF strategy.
- Strengthen accountability for ICT decisions.
- Improve cybersecurity and risk management.
- Ensure efficient use of technology resources.
- Promote digital transformation.
- Support reliable technology services.

---

# 2. Scope

This Framework applies to:

- Board of Trustees.
- Executive Management.
- Information Technology Department.
- All employees.
- Volunteers.
- Consultants.
- Contractors.
- Third-party technology providers.

It covers:

- ICT strategy.
- Hardware and software.
- Cloud services.
- Networks.
- Information systems.
- Data management.
- Cybersecurity.
- Digital projects.
- Technology procurement.
- ICT risk management.

---

# 3. IT Governance Principles

TJF technology management shall follow these principles:

## Strategic Alignment

Technology decisions must support TJF's mission, programmes and strategic priorities.

---

## Value Delivery

ICT investments should provide measurable organizational value.

---

## Risk Management

Technology risks must be identified, assessed and managed proactively.

---

## Resource Optimization

Technology resources must be used efficiently and responsibly.

---

## Transparency

ICT decisions should be documented, accountable and reviewable.

---

## Security by Design

Security considerations must be integrated into technology planning and implementation.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Technology services should continuously evolve based on organizational needs and emerging opportunities.

---

# 4. IT Governance Structure

## Board of Trustees

Responsibilities:

- Provides strategic oversight of major technology investments.
- Reviews significant ICT risks.
- Ensures technology supports organizational sustainability.

---

# Executive Director

Responsibilities:

- Provides executive sponsorship for technology initiatives.
- Approves major ICT investments.
- Ensures alignment between technology and organizational strategy.

---

# IT Governance Committee

Where established, the committee shall provide oversight of:

- ICT strategy.
- Digital transformation initiatives.
- Cybersecurity risks.
- Technology investments.
- Major system implementations.

Membership may include:

- Executive Director.
- IT Manager.
- Finance Representative.
- Operations Representative.
- Programme Representative.
- Data Protection Officer (where applicable).

---

# Information Technology Department

Responsible for:

- ICT strategy implementation.
- Technology operations.
- Cybersecurity management.
- User support.
- System administration.
- Technology standards.
- ICT project delivery.

---

# Department Heads

Responsible for:

- Identifying technology needs.
- Ensuring proper use of ICT resources.
- Supporting digital adoption within their departments.

---

# All Users

Responsible for:

- Protecting TJF information assets.
- Following ICT policies.
- Reporting security incidents.
- Using technology responsibly.

---

# 5. IT Strategic Planning

TJF shall maintain an ICT strategy aligned with organizational priorities.

The strategy should address:

- Current technology environment.
- Future technology needs.
- Digital transformation opportunities.
- Cybersecurity improvements.
- Budget requirements.
- Implementation roadmap.

---

# 6. ICT Investment Management

Technology investments should be evaluated based on:

- Strategic value.
- Cost effectiveness.
- Security requirements.
- Sustainability.
- User needs.
- Risk impact.

Major ICT investments should include:

- Business justification.
- Budget approval.
- Implementation plan.
- Risk assessment.
- Post-implementation review.

---

# 7. Technology Procurement

ICT procurement shall follow TJF procurement policies.

Procurement decisions should consider:

- Technical suitability.
- Security requirements.
- Vendor reputation.
- Total cost of ownership.
- Support availability.
- Scalability.

---

# 8. ICT Risk Management

Technology risks should be identified and managed through:

- Risk assessments.
- Security reviews.
- Vulnerability management.
- Incident monitoring.
- Business continuity planning.

Key ICT risks include:

- Cyberattacks.
- Data loss.
- System failures.
- Unauthorized access.
- Vendor dependency.
- Technology obsolescence.

---

# 9. Information Security Governance

TJF shall maintain appropriate controls to protect:

- Confidentiality.
- Integrity.
- Availability.

Security governance includes:

- Access management.
- Password controls.
- Data protection.
- Cybersecurity awareness.
- Incident response.
- Monitoring.

---

# 10. Digital Transformation Governance

Digital transformation initiatives should:

- Support organizational strategy.
- Improve efficiency.
- Enhance stakeholder experience.
- Increase programme impact.
- Promote innovation.

Projects should have:

- Clear objectives.
- Defined ownership.
- Budget approval.
- Implementation timelines.
- Success indicators.

---

# 11. IT Service Management

The IT Department should maintain reliable technology services through:

- User support procedures.
- Service request management.
- System monitoring.
- Maintenance schedules.
- Issue escalation processes.

---

# 12. Third-Party Technology Management

Technology suppliers should be managed through:

- Vendor assessment.
- Service agreements.
- Security requirements.
- Performance monitoring.
- Contract reviews.

Third parties accessing TJF systems must comply with applicable ICT policies.

---

# 13. Compliance Requirements

Technology operations should comply with:

- Applicable laws and regulations.
- Data protection requirements.
- Contractual obligations.
- Donor requirements where applicable.
- TJF governance policies.

---

# 14. Performance Measurement

IT performance may be measured through:

| Indicator | Measurement |
|-----------|-------------|
| System availability | Percentage uptime |
| User satisfaction | Survey results |
| Security incidents | Number |
| Incident resolution time | Average duration |
| Technology projects completed | Number |
| ICT budget utilization | Percentage |

---

# 15. Review and Continuous Improvement

This Framework should be reviewed:

- Every two years.
- Following major technology changes.
- After significant cybersecurity incidents.
- When organizational strategy changes.

---

# Conclusion

Effective IT governance enables TJF to use technology strategically, securely and responsibly.

By implementing this Framework, The Jopetnah Foundation strengthens operational efficiency, protects critical information assets and ensures that digital capabilities contribute directly to sustainable organizational impact.

---

> **Technology governance transforms ICT from a support function into a strategic driver of innovation, accountability and growth.**
