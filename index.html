The problem

It never arrives in one piece.

In the communities Jopetnah serves, a child out of school, a family without healthcare, and a household without income are rarely separate problems — they're the same problem, seen from different angles.

Most organizations specialize in one slice of it. Jopetnah is built to address the whole picture, for the whole family.

Our model

Four pillars.
One point of entry.

Because our programs work together, a single Jopetnah gift or partnership can be shown to reach every part of one family's life — not just one slice of it.

Education & Youth Development

Scholarships, mentorship, and digital-skills labs.

Health & Wellbeing

Community health champions, malaria prevention, and mobile wellness clinics.

Livelihoods

Micro-grants, vocational-to-job pipelines, savings circles.

Environment

Clean water, sanitation access, and climate-smart farming.

Programs

Flagship work in every pillar

A closer look at what Jopetnah funds and runs on the ground.

Education & Youth Development

  • Jopetnah Scholars — need-based scholarships + mentorship
  • Future-Ready Labs — digital & vocational skills hubs
  • Read to Lead — community libraries & literacy

Health & Wellbeing

  • Community Health Champions — trained local health educators
  • Mobile Wellness Clinics & Malaria Response — maternal health, screenings, vaccines, bed nets & rapid testing referrals
  • Mind & Body Program — mental health & nutrition education

Poverty Alleviation & Livelihoods

  • Jopetnah Micro-Grants — seed capital + training
  • Skills-to-Income Pipeline — training tied to jobs
  • Cooperative Savings Circles — savings + financial literacy

Environment & Community Development

  • Green Neighborhoods — tree planting & clean-ups
  • Water & Sanitation Access — boreholes & hygiene education
  • Climate-Smart Livelihoods — eco-friendly agribusiness
