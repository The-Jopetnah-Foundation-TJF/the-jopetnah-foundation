# Reporting Standards Manual

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes reporting as a critical accountability and management function.

Effective reporting enables TJF to communicate achievements, demonstrate impact, identify challenges, support decision-making and maintain trust with communities, partners, donors and stakeholders.

This Reporting Standards Manual establishes TJF’s requirements for preparing, reviewing, approving and sharing programme and organizational reports.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Manual aims to:

- Standardize TJF reporting practices.
- Improve reporting quality.
- Strengthen transparency and accountability.
- Support evidence-based decision-making.
- Enhance stakeholder communication.

---

# 2. Scope

This Manual applies to:

- Programme reports.
- Project reports.
- Monitoring reports.
- Evaluation reports.
- Donor reports.
- Partnership reports.
- Annual organizational reports.

It applies to:

- Staff.
- Consultants.
- Partners.
- Programme implementers.

---

# 3. Reporting Principles

TJF reporting shall follow:

---

## Accuracy

Reports must reflect verified information.

---

## Completeness

Reports should provide sufficient information for understanding performance.

---

## Timeliness

Reports must be submitted according to approved schedules.

---

## Transparency

Information should be presented honestly and clearly.

---

## Evidence-Based Reporting

Claims should be supported by:

- Data.
- Records.
- Evidence.
- Analysis.

---

# 4. Reporting Framework

TJF reporting follows:

```
DATA COLLECTION
        ↓
DATA ANALYSIS
        ↓
REPORT PREPARATION
        ↓
QUALITY REVIEW
        ↓
APPROVAL
        ↓
DISTRIBUTION
        ↓
LEARNING AND IMPROVEMENT
```

---

# 5. Types of TJF Reports

## 5.1 Activity Reports

Purpose:

To document completed activities.

Contents:

- Activity details.
- Date and location.
- Participants.
- Results achieved.
- Challenges.

Frequency:

As required.

---

## 5.2 Monthly Programme Reports

Purpose:

To track implementation progress.

Contents:

- Activities completed.
- Indicator progress.
- Resource utilization.
- Challenges.
- Next steps.

---

## 5.3 Quarterly Performance Reports

Purpose:

To provide management oversight.

Contents:

- Programme achievements.
- Performance trends.
- Risks.
- Financial progress.
- Recommendations.

---

## 5.4 Annual Impact Report

Purpose:

To communicate TJF’s yearly achievements.

Contents:

- Organizational highlights.
- Programme results.
- Impact stories.
- Partnerships.
- Financial overview.
- Future priorities.

---

## 5.5 Donor Reports

Purpose:

To meet funding accountability requirements.

Contents:

- Grant objectives.
- Activities implemented.
- Results achieved.
- Financial information.
- Challenges.
- Sustainability plans.

---

## 5.6 Evaluation Reports

Purpose:

To communicate assessment findings.

Contents:

- Methodology.
- Findings.
- Conclusions.
- Recommendations.

---

# 6. Standard Report Structure

All major reports should include:

## Cover Page

Including:

- TJF branding.
- Report title.
- Reporting period.
- Date.

---

## Executive Summary

Brief overview of:

- Purpose.
- Key achievements.
- Major findings.

---

## Background

Description of:

- Programme context.
- Objectives.
- Target groups.

---

## Implementation Progress

Including:

- Activities completed.
- Results achieved.
- Performance against targets.

---

## Monitoring and Evaluation Findings

Including:

- Indicator results.
- Data analysis.
- Lessons learned.

---

## Challenges and Risks

Including:

- Identified barriers.
- Mitigation actions.

---

## Recommendations and Next Steps

Including:

- Improvement actions.
- Future priorities.

---

# 7. Reporting Quality Review

Before approval, reports should be checked for:

- Accuracy.
- Completeness.
- Consistency.
- Evidence support.
- Language quality.
- Compliance.

---

# 8. Data Presentation Standards

Reports should use:

- Tables.
- Charts.
- Graphs.
- Case studies.
- Photographic evidence where appropriate.

All visuals must respect:

- Consent requirements.
- Privacy standards.
- Ethical communication.

---

# 9. Reporting Timeline Standards

| Report Type | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Activity Report | After activity completion |
| Monthly Report | Monthly |
| Quarterly Report | Every three months |
| Annual Report | Yearly |
| Evaluation Report | Based on evaluation schedule |

---

# 10. Report Approval Process

Reports shall follow:

```
Prepared by Programme Team
          ↓
Reviewed by MEAL Department
          ↓
Approved by Programme Manager
          ↓
Final Approval by Management (where required)
```
