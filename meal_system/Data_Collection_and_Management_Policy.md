# Data Collection and Management Policy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that reliable data is essential for effective programme planning, implementation, monitoring, evaluation and decision-making.

Responsible data management ensures that information collected from communities, beneficiaries, partners and operations is accurate, secure, ethical and used appropriately.

This Data Collection and Management Policy establishes TJF’s standards for collecting, processing, storing, protecting and utilizing data across all organizational activities.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Policy aims to:

- Establish standards for data collection and management.
- Ensure data accuracy and reliability.
- Protect personal and sensitive information.
- Promote ethical data use.
- Strengthen evidence-based decision-making.

---

# 2. Scope

This Policy applies to:

- Programme data.
- Beneficiary information.
- Monitoring data.
- Evaluation data.
- Research data.
- Operational information.
- Partner-generated information.

It applies to:

- Employees.
- Volunteers.
- Consultants.
- Researchers.
- Partners.

---

# 3. Data Management Principles

TJF manages data according to the following principles:

---

## Accuracy

Information must be correct, complete and reliable.

---

## Relevance

Only necessary information should be collected.

---

## Transparency

Individuals should understand how their information is used.

---

## Confidentiality

Personal information must be protected.

---

## Security

Data must be protected from unauthorized access, loss or misuse.

---

## Accountability

TJF remains responsible for responsible data practices.

---

# 4. Types of Data Collected

TJF may collect:

## Programme Data

Including:

- Activities implemented.
- Outputs achieved.
- Outcomes measured.

---

## Beneficiary Data

Including:

- Registration information.
- Participation records.
- Service access information.

---

## Monitoring Data

Including:

- Indicators.
- Targets.
- Progress reports.

---

## Evaluation Data

Including:

- Surveys.
- Interviews.
- Assessment findings.

---

## Operational Data

Including:

- Finance information.
- Logistics records.
- Administrative information.

---

# 5. Data Collection Standards

Data collection must ensure:

- Clear purpose.
- Appropriate methods.
- Informed participation.
- Accurate recording.
- Ethical handling.

---

# 6. Data Collection Methods

TJF may use:

## Surveys

For collecting structured information.

---

## Interviews

For detailed understanding.

---

## Focus Group Discussions

For community perspectives.

---

## Observation

For verifying conditions and activities.

---

## Administrative Records

For operational information.

---

## Digital Data Collection Tools

Including:

- Mobile data systems.
- Online forms.
- Databases.

---

# 7. Consent and Ethical Data Collection

Before collecting personal information, TJF should:

- Explain the purpose.
- Obtain appropriate consent.
- Respect confidentiality.
- Allow questions and clarification.

Special protection shall apply to:

- Children.
- Vulnerable groups.
- Sensitive cases.

---

# 8. Data Quality Management

TJF shall maintain data quality through:

## Completeness

Ensuring required information is captured.

---

## Accuracy

Ensuring information reflects reality.

---

## Consistency

Applying standardized approaches.

---

## Timeliness

Ensuring information is available when required.

---

## Verification

Checking information through reviews and validation.

---

# 9. Data Storage and Organization

Data shall be stored through approved systems including:

- Secure databases.
- Authorized digital platforms.
- Protected physical records.

Records should include:

- Data source.
- Collection date.
- Responsible person.
- Version information.

---

# 10. Data Security Measures

TJF shall implement:

- Access controls.
- Password protection.
- Secure backups.
- User permissions.
- Encryption where appropriate.

---

# 11. Data Access Management

Access shall be based on:

- Job responsibilities.
- Programme requirements.
- Authorization levels.

Unauthorized access, sharing or modification is prohibited.

---

# 12. Data Protection Requirements

TJF shall protect individuals by:

- Minimizing unnecessary data collection.
- Limiting access.
- Protecting identities.
- Safeguarding sensitive information.

---

# 13. Data Analysis and Use

Data should support:

- Programme improvement.
- Strategic planning.
- Reporting.
- Research.
- Decision-making.

Analysis should be:

- Objective.
- Evidence-based.
- Documented.

---

# 14. Data Sharing

Data may be shared with:

- Donors.
- Government authorities.
- Partners.
- Researchers.

Sharing must ensure:

- Appropriate authorization.
- Privacy protection.
- Responsible use.

---

# 15. Data Retention and Disposal

TJF shall maintain records according to:

- Legal requirements.
- Donor obligations.
- Organizational needs.

When data is no longer required, disposal should be secure.

---

# 16. Digital Data Management

TJF may use technology solutions including:

- Management information systems.
- Dashboards.
- Digital monitoring platforms.
- Cloud-based systems.

Digital systems must maintain:

- Reliability.
- Security.
- Accessibility.

---

# 17. Data Breach Management

A suspected breach must be:

1. Reported immediately.
2. Investigated.
3. Contained.
4. Documented.
5. Addressed through corrective action.
