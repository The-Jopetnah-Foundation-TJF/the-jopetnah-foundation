# Learning and Knowledge Management Policy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that organizational knowledge is a valuable resource for achieving sustainable impact.

Learning enables TJF to understand what works, improve programme approaches, strengthen decision-making and share successful practices with partners and communities.

This Learning and Knowledge Management Policy establishes TJF’s systems for capturing, organizing, sharing and applying knowledge generated through programmes, research, partnerships and organizational experience.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Policy aims to:

- Establish a culture of continuous learning.
- Capture institutional knowledge.
- Promote evidence-based improvements.
- Share lessons and best practices.
- Support innovation and organizational growth.

---

# 2. Scope

This Policy applies to:

- All TJF programmes.
- Projects.
- Research activities.
- Partnerships.
- Operations.
- Organizational processes.

It applies to:

- Board members.
- Employees.
- Volunteers.
- Consultants.
- Partners.

---

# 3. Definition of Knowledge Management

Knowledge Management refers to the systematic process of:

- Creating knowledge.
- Capturing information.
- Organizing resources.
- Sharing learning.
- Applying knowledge for improvement.

---

# 4. Learning Principles

TJF learning is guided by:

---

## Continuous Improvement

Every experience provides opportunities for improvement.

---

## Evidence-Based Learning

Decisions should be informed by:

- Data.
- Research.
- Experience.
- Feedback.

---

## Knowledge Sharing

Useful information should benefit teams, partners and communities.

---

## Innovation

TJF encourages new ideas and improved solutions.

---

## Reflection

Teams should regularly review performance and lessons.

---

# 5. Knowledge Management Cycle

TJF follows:

```
CREATE KNOWLEDGE
        ↓
CAPTURE EXPERIENCE
        ↓
ORGANIZE INFORMATION
        ↓
SHARE LEARNING
        ↓
APPLY IMPROVEMENTS
        ↓
GENERATE NEW KNOWLEDGE
```
