# Evaluation Policy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes evaluation as a critical process for understanding programme performance, measuring impact and improving future interventions.

Evaluation enables TJF to determine whether programmes are relevant, effective, efficient, sustainable and responsive to community needs.

This Evaluation Policy establishes the principles, standards and procedures for conducting evaluations across TJF programmes and initiatives.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Policy aims to:

- Establish TJF’s evaluation standards.
- Assess programme performance and impact.
- Promote evidence-based decision-making.
- Strengthen accountability to stakeholders.
- Capture lessons for improvement.
- Support organizational learning.

---

# 2. Scope

This Policy applies to:

- All TJF programmes.
- Projects.
- Community interventions.
- Emergency responses.
- Partnerships.
- Research activities.

It applies to:

- Management.
- Programme teams.
- MEAL Department.
- External evaluators.
- Partners.

---

# 3. Definition of Evaluation

Evaluation is a systematic and objective assessment of an ongoing or completed programme to determine:

- Relevance.
- Effectiveness.
- Efficiency.
- Sustainability.
- Impact.

---

# 4. Evaluation Principles

TJF evaluations shall follow:

---

## Independence

Evaluations should maintain objectivity and avoid conflicts of interest.

---

## Credibility

Findings should be supported by reliable evidence.

---

## Participation

Relevant stakeholders should contribute to evaluation processes.

---

## Transparency

Methods and findings should be clearly documented.

---

## Learning Orientation

Evaluations should improve future programmes.

---

# 5. Evaluation Criteria

TJF applies internationally recognized evaluation criteria:

---

## Relevance

Assessment of whether the programme addresses:

- Community needs.
- Strategic priorities.
- Stakeholder expectations.

---

## Effectiveness

Assessment of whether objectives were achieved.

Questions include:

- What results were achieved?
- Were targets reached?

---

## Efficiency

Assessment of whether resources were used effectively.

Considerations include:

- Budget management.
- Time utilization.
- Operational performance.

---

## Sustainability

Assessment of whether benefits continue after programme completion.

---

## Impact

Assessment of long-term changes created by the programme.

---

# 6. Types of Evaluations

## Baseline Evaluation

Conducted before implementation to establish:

- Existing conditions.
- Community needs.
- Starting indicators.

---

## Formative Evaluation

Conducted during programme design to improve approaches.

---

## Mid-Term Evaluation

Conducted during implementation to assess:

- Progress.
- Challenges.
- Required adjustments.

---

## Final Evaluation

Conducted at programme completion to assess achievements.

---

## Impact Evaluation

Measures long-term changes resulting from interventions.

---

## Real-Time Evaluation

Used during emergencies or rapidly changing situations.

---

# 7. Evaluation Planning

Each programme should identify:

- Evaluation purpose.
- Evaluation questions.
- Methodology.
- Timeline.
- Budget.
- Responsible persons.

---

# 8. Evaluation Methodologies

TJF may use:

## Quantitative Methods

Including:

- Surveys.
- Statistical analysis.
- Indicator measurement.

---

## Qualitative Methods

Including:

- Interviews.
- Focus groups.
- Community discussions.

---

## Mixed Methods

Combining quantitative and qualitative approaches.

---

# 9. Stakeholder Participation

Evaluation stakeholders may include:

- Beneficiaries.
- Community leaders.
- Programme staff.
- Partners.
- Donors.
- Government representatives.

---

# 10. Evaluation Process

## Stage 1: Planning

Define:

- Purpose.
- Scope.
- Methodology.

---

## Stage 2: Data Collection

Gather evidence through approved methods.

---

## Stage 3: Analysis

Interpret findings and identify patterns.

---

## Stage 4: Reporting

Prepare evaluation report including:

- Findings.
- Conclusions.
- Recommendations.

---

## Stage 5: Learning and Action

Develop improvement plans based on recommendations.

---

# 11. Evaluation Reports

Reports should include:

## Executive Summary

Overview of findings.

---

## Background

Programme context and objectives.

---

## Methodology

Approaches used.

---

## Findings

Evidence and observations.

---

## Conclusions

Overall assessment.

---

## Recommendations

Actions for improvement.

---

# 12. Management Response

Following evaluations, TJF shall develop a management response identifying:

- Accepted recommendations.
- Responsible persons.
- Implementation timelines.
- Follow-up mechanisms.

---

# 13. Independent Evaluations

TJF may engage external evaluators to provide:

- Objectivity.
- Technical expertise.
- Independent assessment.

Independent evaluations may be required for:

- Large programmes.
- Donor-funded projects.
- Strategic initiatives.

---

# 14. Evaluation Quality Assurance

Evaluation quality shall be assessed through:

- Appropriate methodology.
- Reliable evidence.
- Ethical processes.
- Clear reporting.
- Practical recommendations.

---

# 15. Roles and Responsibilities

## Executive Director

Responsible for:

- Approving major evaluations.
- Ensuring findings inform strategy.

---

## MEAL Department

Responsible for:

- Evaluation planning.
- Quality assurance.
- Coordination.
- Learning dissemination.

---

## Programme Teams

Responsible for:

- Providing information.
- Supporting evaluation activities.
- Implementing recommendations.

---

## External Evaluators

Responsible for:

- Independent assessment.
- Professional standards.

---

# 17. Knowledge Sharing

Evaluation findings may be shared through:

- Reports.
- Learning sessions.
- Programme reviews.
- Strategic planning meetings.

---

# 18. Review

This Policy shall be reviewed:

- Every three years.
- Following major evaluation lessons.
- Based on changes in evaluation standards.

---

# Conclusion

Evaluation enables TJF to understand the difference it creates and continuously improve its contribution to communities.

Through systematic evaluation, The Jopetnah Foundation strengthens accountability, learning and sustainable development impact.

---

> **Evaluation turns experience into knowledge and knowledge into lasting improvement.**
