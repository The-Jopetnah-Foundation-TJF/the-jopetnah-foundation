# Research and Innovation Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes research and innovation as essential drivers of sustainable development, evidence-based programming and transformational change.

Through research, TJF generates knowledge about community challenges, evaluates solutions and contributes to improved development practices.

Through innovation, TJF encourages creative approaches, technology adoption and new models that address complex social, economic and environmental challenges.

This Research and Innovation Framework establishes TJF’s approach to evidence generation, experimentation, knowledge creation and scaling of successful solutions.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Establish TJF’s research approach.
- Promote innovation-driven solutions.
- Generate evidence for decision-making.
- Strengthen programme effectiveness.
- Encourage collaboration with research institutions.
- Support scaling of successful interventions.

---

It guides:

- Leadership.

- Researchers.
- Partners.

# 3. Research and Innovation Principles

TJF research and innovation are guided by:

## Practical Impact

Research should contribute to real-world improvements.
