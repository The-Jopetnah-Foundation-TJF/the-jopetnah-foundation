# Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability & Learning (MEAL) Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) is committed to delivering programmes that generate measurable, sustainable and meaningful impact.

The Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability and Learning (MEAL) Framework provides the foundation for collecting evidence, measuring performance, engaging stakeholders, improving programmes and demonstrating accountability.

Through a strong MEAL system, TJF ensures that decisions are guided by reliable information, community voices are considered and resources are used effectively.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Establish TJF’s MEAL approach.
- Strengthen evidence-based programme management.
- Measure programme performance and impact.
- Promote accountability to communities and partners.
- Support organizational learning.
- Improve decision-making.

---

# 2. Scope

This Framework applies to:

- All TJF programmes.
- Projects.
- Community initiatives.
- Emergency responses.
- Partnerships.
- Research activities.

It guides:

- Programme teams.
- MEAL officers.
- Management.
- Partners.
- Field teams.

---

# 3. MEAL Definition

## Monitoring

The continuous process of tracking programme activities, outputs, progress and performance.

---

## Evaluation

The systematic assessment of programme relevance, effectiveness, efficiency, sustainability and impact.

---

## Accountability

The responsibility to communicate, listen and respond to stakeholders, especially communities served.

---

## Learning

The process of using evidence and experience to improve programmes and organizational performance.

---

# 4. TJF MEAL Principles

The MEAL system is guided by:

---

## Evidence-Based Decision Making

Decisions should be informed by reliable data and analysis.

---

## Participation

Communities and stakeholders should contribute to programme learning.

---

## Transparency

Information should be shared responsibly with relevant stakeholders.

---

## Quality Data

Data collection should be:

- Accurate.
- Timely.
- Relevant.
- Secure.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Lessons should strengthen future programmes.

---

# 5. MEAL Framework Model

TJF follows the cycle:

```
PROGRAMME DESIGN
        ↓
INDICATOR DEVELOPMENT
        ↓
DATA COLLECTION
        ↓
ANALYSIS
        ↓
REPORTING
        ↓
LEARNING
        ↓
PROGRAMME IMPROVEMENT
```

## Routine Monitoring

Tracking daily and monthly implementation progress.

---

## Field Monitoring

Conducting field visits and verification.

---

## Indicator Tracking

Measuring progress against targets.

---

## Financial Monitoring

Reviewing resource utilization.

To establish starting conditions.
