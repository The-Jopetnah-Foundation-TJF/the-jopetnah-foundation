# Knowledge Management Policy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes knowledge as one of its most valuable institutional assets.

Effective knowledge management enables TJF to capture experience, preserve institutional memory, improve decision-making, strengthen innovation and maximize development impact.

This Knowledge Management Policy establishes the principles, systems and responsibilities for creating, collecting, organizing, sharing and applying knowledge across TJF operations.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Policy aims to:

- Establish a structured knowledge management system.
- Preserve institutional knowledge.
- Improve organizational learning.
- Promote knowledge sharing.
- Strengthen evidence-based decisions.
- Support innovation and continuous improvement.

---

# 2. Scope

This Policy applies to:

- Research knowledge.
- Programme data.
- Operational experience.
- Technical expertise.
- Lessons learned.
- Policies and procedures.
- Reports and publications.
- Digital resources.
- Institutional records.

It applies to:

- Board Members.
- Management.
- Employees.
- Volunteers.
- Consultants.
- Partners where applicable.

---

# 3. Knowledge Management Vision

To establish TJF as a learning organization where knowledge is continuously created, shared and applied to improve communities and organizational performance.

---

# 4. Knowledge Management Mission

To transform information and experience into actionable knowledge that supports innovation, accountability and sustainable development.

---

# 5. Knowledge Management Principles

TJF knowledge management shall be guided by:

## Accessibility

Relevant knowledge should be available to those who need it.

---

## Accuracy

Knowledge resources shall be reliable, verified and quality-controlled.

---

## Collaboration

Knowledge creation shall encourage teamwork and shared learning.

---

## Protection

Sensitive information shall be safeguarded appropriately.

---

## Learning Culture

TJF shall promote reflection, adaptation and improvement.

---

## Continuous Improvement

Knowledge systems shall evolve based on organizational needs.

---

# 6. Knowledge Management Objectives

TJF seeks to:

- Create reliable knowledge systems.
- Improve information flow across departments.
- Capture lessons from programmes.
- Reduce loss of institutional memory.
- Strengthen collaboration.
- Support innovation and research.

---

# 7. Types of Organizational Knowledge

## Explicit Knowledge

Knowledge that can be documented, including:

- Policies.
- Manuals.
- Reports.
- Research papers.
- Training materials.
- Databases.

---

## Tacit Knowledge

Experience-based knowledge including:

- Staff expertise.
- Field experiences.
- Community insights.
- Technical skills.
- Institutional practices.

---

# 8. Knowledge Management Cycle

TJF shall manage knowledge through:

```
Knowledge Creation
        ↓
Knowledge Collection
        ↓
Knowledge Organization
        ↓
Knowledge Storage
        ↓
Knowledge Sharing
        ↓
Knowledge Application
        ↓
Learning and Improvement
```

# 10. Knowledge Capture

TJF shall document:

- Lessons learned.
- Success stories.

# 13. Knowledge Sharing Mechanisms

- Internal learning sessions.
- Workshops.
- Webinars.
- Communities of practice.
- Newsletters.

- Knowledge forums.

TJF may establish communities of practice focused on areas such as:

- Humanitarian response.
- Healthcare.

# 15. Lessons Learned Management

Following major projects, programmes or emergencies, TJF shall conduct:

- Reflection sessions.
- After-action reviews.
- Learning workshops.
- Improvement planning.

Lessons shall be documented and incorporated into future activities.

---

# 16. Knowledge Quality Assurance

Knowledge products shall undergo:

- Technical review.
- Accuracy verification.
- Approval processes.
- Version control.
- Periodic updates.

---

# 17. Information Security and Confidentiality

TJF shall protect knowledge assets through:

- Access controls.
- Data protection measures.
- Secure storage.
- Backup systems.
- Confidentiality agreements.

Sensitive information shall only be accessed by authorized persons.
