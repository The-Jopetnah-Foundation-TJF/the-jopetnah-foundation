# Data Intelligence Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes data as a strategic asset that enables evidence-based decision-making, improved programme delivery, operational efficiency and measurable social impact.

This Data Intelligence Framework establishes TJF’s approach to collecting, managing, analyzing, protecting and applying data to generate meaningful insights that support communities, programmes and institutional growth.

Through responsible use of technology, analytics and emerging intelligence systems, TJF aims to become a data-driven development institution capable of anticipating challenges, measuring impact and designing better solutions.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Establish TJF’s data intelligence ecosystem.
- Strengthen evidence-based decision-making.
- Improve programme monitoring and evaluation.
- Support predictive analysis and planning.
- Promote responsible data use.
- Enhance institutional learning.

---

# 2. Scope

This Framework applies to:

- Programme data.
- Research data.
- Monitoring and evaluation data.
- Financial data.
- Operational data.
- Human resource data.
- Partnership data.
- Community feedback data.
- Geospatial information.
- Digital platform data.

It applies to:

- Headquarters.
- Regional offices.
- Country programmes.
- Staff.
- Researchers.
- Consultants.
- Partners.

---

# 3. Data Intelligence Vision

To establish TJF as a globally recognized data-driven foundation that transforms information into intelligence, insights and impactful decisions.

---

# 4. Data Intelligence Mission

To collect, protect, analyze and apply quality data to improve programmes, strengthen accountability and accelerate sustainable development outcomes.

---

# 5. Data Intelligence Principles

TJF data systems shall follow:

## Accuracy

Data must be reliable, complete and verified.

---

## Accessibility

Authorized users should have timely access to relevant information.

---

## Security

Data shall be protected against unauthorized access, loss or misuse.

---

## Ethics

Data shall be collected and used responsibly.

---

## Transparency

Data processes shall be documented and understandable.

---

## Impact Orientation

Data should support meaningful decisions and improved outcomes.

---

TJF seeks to:

- Build a strong institutional data culture.
- Improve data collection systems.
- Develop analytical capabilities.
- Strengthen programme intelligence.

TJF shall develop capabilities in:

- What happened?
- Current programme status.
- Historical trends.

---

## Diagnostic Analytics

Understanding:

- Why something happened.
- Root causes.
- Performance gaps.

---

## Predictive Analytics

Understanding:

- What may happen in the future.
- Emerging risks.
- Potential opportunities.

---

## Prescriptive Analytics

Supporting:

- What actions should be taken.
- Strategic decisions.

TJF shall protect data through:

- Access controls.
- Encryption.

- Appropriate authorization.

# 18. Data Quality Management

Data quality shall be assessed through:

| Quality Area | Standard |
|---|---|
| Accuracy | Data reflects reality |
| Completeness | Required information available |
| Consistency | Information matches across systems |
| Timeliness | Data available when needed |
| Reliability | Sources are trustworthy |
