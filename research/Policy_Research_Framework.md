# Policy Research Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that sustainable development requires not only effective programmes but also informed policies, strong institutions and evidence-based decision-making.

This Policy Research Framework establishes TJF’s approach to generating, analyzing and communicating evidence that supports policy development, advocacy and improved public decision-making.

Through rigorous research, stakeholder engagement and knowledge dissemination, TJF seeks to contribute constructive solutions to complex social, economic, humanitarian and environmental challenges.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Establish TJF’s policy research approach.
- Generate evidence for policy improvement.
- Strengthen dialogue between research and decision-makers.
- Support advocacy through credible evidence.
- Promote inclusive and sustainable policies.
- Increase TJF’s contribution to development discussions.

---

# 2. Scope

This Framework applies to policy research related to:

- Economic development.
- Poverty reduction.
- Healthcare systems.
- Education.
- Agriculture and food security.
- Climate change.
- Humanitarian affairs.
- Governance.
- Social protection.
- Technology and innovation.
- Sustainable development.

---

# 3. Policy Research Vision

To become a trusted source of evidence and ideas that influence policies, strengthen institutions and improve lives.

---

# 4. Policy Research Mission

To produce credible, relevant and accessible research that connects evidence, policy decisions and practical solutions.

---

# 5. Policy Research Principles

TJF policy research shall follow:

## Evidence-Based Analysis

Research shall be based on reliable data and sound methodologies.

---

## Independence

Research findings shall remain objective and free from inappropriate influence.

---

## Relevance

Research shall address important development challenges.

---

## Inclusiveness

Research shall consider diverse perspectives, especially affected communities.

---

## Transparency

Methods, limitations and assumptions shall be clearly communicated.

---

## Practical Application

Research should contribute to real-world improvements.

---
