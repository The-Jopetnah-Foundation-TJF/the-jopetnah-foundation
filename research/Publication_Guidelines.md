# Publication Guidelines

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes publications as powerful tools for sharing knowledge, influencing policy, demonstrating impact and contributing to global development conversations.

These Publication Guidelines establish standards for producing credible, ethical, consistent and professionally presented knowledge products that represent TJF’s commitment to research excellence, transparency and public value.

The Guidelines ensure that all TJF publications meet appropriate standards of quality, accuracy, accessibility and institutional integrity.

---

# 1. Purpose

These Guidelines aim to:

- Establish TJF publication standards.
- Ensure quality and consistency of knowledge products.
- Promote ethical publishing practices.
- Strengthen TJF’s institutional reputation.
- Improve knowledge dissemination.
- Support evidence-based decision-making.

---

# 2. Scope

These Guidelines apply to all TJF publications, including:

- Research reports.
- Policy briefs.
- White papers.
- Discussion papers.
- Case studies.
- Impact reports.
- Technical manuals.
- Training materials.
- Journals.
- Articles.
- Digital knowledge products.
- Public information documents.

---

# 3. Publication Principles

All TJF publications shall follow:

## Accuracy

Information must be factual, verified and supported by evidence.

---

## Integrity

Publications shall maintain honesty, independence and ethical standards.

---

## Clarity

Content should be understandable and accessible to intended audiences.

---

## Professionalism

Publications shall reflect TJF’s institutional standards and identity.

---

## Inclusiveness

Publications should consider diverse perspectives and affected communities.

---

## Accessibility

Knowledge products should reach relevant stakeholders through appropriate channels.

---

## Research Publications

## Policy Publications

# 5. Publication Development Process

TJF shall follow:

```
Publication Concept
        ↓
Content Development
        ↓
Technical Review
        ↓
Editorial Review
        ↓
Approval
        ↓
Design and Formatting
        ↓
Publication Release
        ↓
Distribution and Promotion
        ↓
Impact Monitoring
```

---

# 6. Publication Proposal Requirements

Before development, proposed publications should define:

- Purpose.
- Target audience.
- Key messages.
- Expected impact.
- Responsible department.
- Required resources.
- Timeline.

---

# 7. Writing Standards

TJF publications shall:

- Use clear and professional language.
- Present evidence objectively.
- Avoid unsupported claims.
- Include appropriate references.
- Explain technical concepts clearly.
- Maintain consistent terminology.

---

# 8. Research Publication Standards

Research publications should include:

## Title

Clear and descriptive.

---

## Executive Summary

Highlighting:

- Purpose.
- Key findings.
- Recommendations.

---

## Introduction

Providing:

- Background.
- Research context.
- Objectives.

---

## Methodology

Explaining:

- Research design.
- Data collection.
- Analysis methods.

---

## Findings

Presenting:

- Evidence.
- Analysis.
- Results.

---

## Recommendations

Providing:

- Practical actions.
- Policy implications.
- Programme improvements.

---

## References

Listing all sources used.

---

# 9. Policy Brief Standards

Policy briefs should:

- Address a specific policy issue.
- Present evidence clearly.
- Identify challenges.
- Provide practical recommendations.
- Be concise and accessible.

Recommended structure:

```
Policy Issue
      ↓
Evidence
      ↓
Analysis
      ↓
Policy Options
      ↓
Recommendations
```

# 10. Editorial Review Process

Publications shall undergo review for:

- Accuracy.
- Quality.
- Compliance.
- Language.
- Branding.
- Ethical considerations.

Reviewers may include:

- Technical experts.
- Researchers.
- Department leaders.
- External specialists where appropriate.

---

# 11. Peer Review

Major research publications may undergo peer review involving:

- Internal experts.
- Academic partners.
- Independent reviewers.

Peer review shall improve:

- Research credibility.
- Methodological quality.
- Technical accuracy.

---

# 12. Publication Approval Authority

## Executive Director

Responsible for:

- Final approval of major publications.
- Institutional representation.

---

## Research Department

Responsible for:

- Publication coordination.
- Quality assurance.
- Editorial management.

---

## Technical Departments

Responsible for:

- Content accuracy.
- Subject expertise.

---

# 13. Branding and Design Standards

All publications shall comply with TJF branding requirements, including:

- Logo usage.
- Official colours.
- Typography.
- Document formatting.
- Institutional messaging.

---

# 14. Referencing and Citation Standards

Publications shall:

- Properly acknowledge sources.
- Avoid plagiarism.
- Maintain consistent citation methods.
- Respect intellectual property rights.

---

# 15. Digital Publication

Digital publications may be distributed through:

- TJF website.
- Digital libraries.
- Social media platforms.
- Email networks.
- Online knowledge platforms.

Digital versions should consider:

- Accessibility.
- Mobile compatibility.
- Data security.

---

# 16. Translation and Localization

Where appropriate, TJF may translate publications to:

- Increase accessibility.
- Support local communities.
- Reach international audiences.

Translations should maintain accuracy and original meaning.

---

# 17. Publication Ethics

TJF prohibits:

- Plagiarism.
- Data manipulation.
- False representation.
- Unauthorized publication.
- Misuse of confidential information.

---

# 18. Copyright and Intellectual Property

Unless otherwise specified:

- TJF retains ownership of institutional publications.
- Contributors shall receive appropriate recognition.
- External materials shall respect copyright requirements.

---

# 19. Publication Distribution Strategy

Distribution may include:

- Stakeholder mailing lists.
- Policy forums.
- Conferences.
- Academic networks.
- Partner platforms.
- Media engagement.

# 21. Review

These Guidelines shall be reviewed:

- Every three years.
- Following major publishing initiatives.
- Based on feedback from readers and stakeholders.
- Considering changes in publishing practices.

---

# Conclusion

Professional publications strengthen institutional credibility, preserve knowledge and extend the impact of research beyond programme boundaries.

Through these Publication Guidelines, The Jopetnah Foundation commits to producing reliable, accessible and influential knowledge products that contribute to better decisions, stronger policies and sustainable development.
