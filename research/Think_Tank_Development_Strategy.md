# Think Tank Development Strategy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) seeks to establish a globally respected Think Tank and Policy Institute dedicated to generating knowledge, advancing research, influencing policy and developing innovative solutions to complex development challenges.

The TJF Think Tank will serve as an intellectual platform connecting researchers, policymakers, communities, businesses, civil society organizations and development partners to promote evidence-based solutions for sustainable transformation.

This Strategy outlines the vision, governance structure, research priorities, partnership model and growth pathway for establishing TJF as a leading knowledge institution.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Strategy aims to:

- Establish the foundation for TJF’s Think Tank development.
- Define the institutional identity and positioning.
- Strengthen policy research capacity.
- Build global knowledge partnerships.
- Promote evidence-based decision-making.
- Increase TJF’s contribution to development discourse.

---
