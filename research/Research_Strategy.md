# Research Strategy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes research as a critical driver of evidence-based decision-making, sustainable development and social transformation.

This Research Strategy establishes TJF's institutional approach to generating, managing and applying knowledge to improve programmes, influence policies and develop innovative solutions for communities worldwide.

Through research, partnerships and knowledge sharing, TJF seeks to contribute meaningful evidence addressing complex social, economic, humanitarian and environmental challenges.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Strategy aims to:

- Establish TJF's research agenda.
- Promote evidence-based programming.
- Generate knowledge for social impact.
- Support policy development and advocacy.
- Strengthen institutional learning.
- Encourage innovation and collaboration.

---

# 2. Scope

This Strategy applies to research activities related to:

- Human development.
- Poverty reduction.
- Education.
- Healthcare.
- Agriculture.
- Climate resilience.
- Economic empowerment.
- Technology and innovation.
- Humanitarian response.
- Social protection.
- Community development.

It applies to:

- TJF departments.
- Researchers.
- Programme teams.
- Academic partners.
- Consultants.
- Research collaborators.
