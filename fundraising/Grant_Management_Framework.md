# Grant Management Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that effective grant management is essential for maintaining donor confidence, achieving programme objectives and ensuring sustainable impact.

This Grant Management Framework establishes the processes and controls required to manage grants throughout their lifecycle, from award acceptance to final closure.

The Framework promotes:

- Accountability.
- Transparency.
- Compliance.
- Efficient implementation.
- Evidence-based reporting.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Framework is to:

- Establish standardized grant management procedures.
- Ensure compliance with donor requirements.
- Strengthen financial accountability.
- Improve programme delivery.
- Protect TJF's reputation.

---

# 2. Scope

This Framework applies to all grants received by TJF from:

- International donors.
- Government institutions.
- Foundations.
- Corporate partners.
- Development agencies.
- Other funding partners.

---

# 3. Grant Management Principles

## Accountability

TJF accepts responsibility for:

- Delivering agreed results.
- Managing resources properly.
- Reporting accurately.

---

## Compliance

All grants must comply with:

- Grant agreements.
- Donor conditions.
- Applicable laws.
- TJF policies.

---

## Transparency

TJF maintains accurate records of:

- Activities.
- Expenditure.
- Results.
- Challenges.

---

## Value for Money

Resources should be used efficiently to maximize impact.

---

# 5. Grant Award Review

Before accepting funding, TJF shall review:

## Strategic Alignment

Confirm:

- Programme relevance.
- Organizational priorities.
- Long-term value.

---

## Financial Requirements

Review:

- Grant amount.
- Budget restrictions.
- Cost eligibility.
- Payment schedule.

---

## Compliance Requirements

Review:

- Reporting obligations.
- Audit requirements.
- Procurement rules.
- Safeguarding requirements.

---

# 6. Grant Agreement Management

Each grant shall have a documented agreement containing:

- Grant purpose.
- Funding amount.
- Implementation period.
- Responsibilities.
- Reporting requirements.
- Compliance conditions.

Grant agreements must be approved before implementation begins.

---

# 7. Grant Implementation Planning

Each grant should have:

## Implementation Plan

Including:

- Activities.
- Timelines.
- Responsible persons.
- Expected outputs.

---

## Budget Plan

Including:

- Approved costs.
- Spending timelines.
- Financial controls.

---

## Monitoring Plan

Including:

- Indicators.
- Data sources.
- Reporting schedules.
