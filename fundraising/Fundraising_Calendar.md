# Fundraising Calendar

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) uses an annual Fundraising Calendar to coordinate fundraising activities, donor engagement and resource mobilization efforts.

The calendar ensures that fundraising activities are:

- Planned.
- Coordinated.
- Measurable.
- Aligned with organizational priorities.

---

# 1. Purpose

The Fundraising Calendar aims to:

- Organize annual fundraising activities.
- Track funding opportunities.
- Improve proposal readiness.
- Coordinate donor engagement.
- Support resource planning.

---

# 2. Annual Fundraising Cycle

TJF follows a continuous fundraising cycle:

```
Planning

   ▼

   ▼
```

# 3. Annual Fundraising Activity Calendar

## January — Planning & Pipeline Review

Activities:

- Review previous year's fundraising performance.
- Approve annual fundraising targets.
- Update donor database.
- Review strategic priorities.
- Identify priority funding opportunities.

Outputs:

- Annual Fundraising Plan.
- Updated donor pipeline.

---

## February — Donor Research & Opportunity Mapping

Activities:

- Research new donors.
- Review grant opportunities.
- Update donor profiles.
- Identify partnership opportunities.

Outputs:

- Donor Mapping Report.
- Opportunity Tracker.

---

## March — Proposal Development Period

Activities:

- Develop concept notes.
- Prepare grant proposals.
- Conduct internal reviews.
- Finalize budgets.

Outputs:

- Donor-ready proposals.

---

## April — Donor Engagement

Activities:

- Conduct donor meetings.
- Share organizational updates.
- Present programme opportunities.

Outputs:

- Relationship progress reports.

---

## May — Funding Applications

Activities:

- Submit priority applications.
- Track deadlines.
- Respond to donor requests.

Outputs:

- Submitted proposals.

---

## June — Mid-Year Review

Activities:

- Review fundraising performance.
- Analyze funding pipeline.
- Adjust strategy.

Outputs:

- Mid-year fundraising report.

---

## July — Partnership Development

Activities:

- Engage corporate partners.
- Explore sponsorship opportunities.
- Strengthen institutional relationships.

Outputs:

- Partnership opportunities.

---

## August — Campaign Development

Activities:

- Prepare awareness campaigns.
- Develop fundraising materials.
- Mobilize supporters.

Outputs:

- Campaign plans.

---

## September — Major Funding Drive

Activities:

- Launch fundraising initiatives.
- Engage strategic donors.
- Promote impact stories.

Outputs:

- Increased donor engagement.

---

## October — Donor Stewardship

Activities:

- Share impact updates.
- Recognize supporters.
- Conduct donor appreciation activities.

Outputs:

- Donor relationship reports.

---

## November — Grant Renewal & Planning

Activities:

- Review existing grants.
- Prepare renewal applications.
- Plan next funding cycle.

Outputs:

- Renewal pipeline.

---

## December — Annual Review

Activities:

- Evaluate fundraising performance.
- Document lessons learned.
- Prepare next year's calendar.

Outputs:

- Annual Fundraising Report.

# Conclusion

A structured fundraising calendar enables TJF to approach resource mobilization proactively rather than reactively.

Through disciplined planning, relationship management and coordinated execution, The Jopetnah Foundation strengthens its ability to secure sustainable resources for long-term impact.
