# Individual Giving Strategy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that individuals play a significant role in advancing sustainable community development.

The Individual Giving Strategy establishes TJF's approach for building a community of supporters who contribute financial resources, time, expertise and advocacy toward the Foundation's mission.

This Strategy promotes meaningful relationships between TJF and individual supporters through trust, transparency and shared commitment.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Strategy is to:

- Establish an individual donor programme.
- Increase community participation.
- Build recurring giving opportunities.
- Strengthen donor loyalty.
- Expand TJF's supporter network.

---

# 2. Individual Giving Philosophy

TJF believes that:

- Everyone can contribute to positive change.
- Small contributions can create collective impact.
- Donors are partners in transformation.
- Transparency builds trust.
- Long-term relationships create sustainability.

# 7. Monthly Giving Programme

A recurring giving programme allows supporters to provide predictable support.

Benefits:

- Stable funding.
- Stronger donor relationships.
- Long-term programme planning.

Programme elements:

- Easy enrollment.
- Automated contributions.
- Regular impact updates.
