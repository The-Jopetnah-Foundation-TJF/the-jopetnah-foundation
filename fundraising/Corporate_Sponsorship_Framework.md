# Corporate Sponsorship Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes the private sector as an important partner in advancing sustainable development.

Corporate sponsorship provides opportunities for businesses to contribute financial, technical and strategic resources while creating meaningful social impact.

This Framework establishes TJF's approach for identifying, engaging and managing corporate sponsors.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Framework is to:

- Establish a structured corporate partnership approach.
- Attract responsible corporate supporters.
- Create mutually beneficial partnerships.
- Strengthen programme sustainability.
- Promote responsible corporate citizenship.

---

# 2. Corporate Partnership Philosophy

TJF believes that businesses can contribute to positive social transformation through:

- Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR).
- Social investment.
- Employee engagement.
- Innovation partnerships.
- Community development initiatives.

---

# 3. Sponsorship Principles

## Shared Value

Corporate partnerships should create value for:

- Communities.
- TJF.
- Corporate partners.

---

## Alignment

Sponsors should align with:

- TJF mission.
- Ethical standards.
- Community priorities.

---

## Transparency

All sponsorship arrangements should have:

- Clear agreements.
- Defined expectations.
- Proper accountability.

---

## Sustainability

Partnerships should seek long-term impact beyond one-time contributions.

---

# 4. Corporate Partnership Categories

## 4.1 Programme Sponsorship

Companies support specific TJF programmes.

Examples:

- Education initiatives.
- Health programmes.
- Community development projects.

---

## 4.2 Event Sponsorship

Companies support:

- Fundraising events.
- Awareness campaigns.
- Community activities.

---

## 4.3 In-Kind Sponsorship

Support through:

- Equipment.
- Services.
- Technical expertise.
- Facilities.

---

## 4.4 Strategic Corporate Partnerships

Long-term collaboration involving:

- Joint programmes.
- Innovation.
- Shared impact goals.

---

# 5. Corporate Sponsor Categories

## Platinum Partner

Characteristics:

- High-value contribution.
- Strategic partnership.
- Long-term commitment.

Benefits may include:

- Strategic recognition.
- Impact reporting.
- Partnership visibility.

---

## Gold Partner

Characteristics:

- Significant programme support.

Benefits may include:

- Recognition opportunities.
- Engagement opportunities.

---

## Silver Partner

Characteristics:

- Specific activity support.

Benefits may include:

- Acknowledgement.
- Event visibility.

---

## Supporting Partner

Characteristics:

- Smaller contributions.
- Community support.

---

# 6. Corporate Partner Selection Criteria

Potential sponsors should be assessed based on:

## Reputation

Does the company maintain responsible business practices?

---

## Alignment

Does the company support TJF priorities?

---

## Sustainability

Can the partnership create long-term value?

---

## Compliance

Does the partnership meet TJF ethical requirements?

# 8. Corporate Sponsorship Proposal Structure

A sponsorship proposal should include:

## Introduction

About TJF and partnership opportunity.

---

## Partnership Opportunity

Explain:

- Programme.
- Need.
- Expected impact.

---

## Corporate Value Proposition

Explain benefits such as:

- Social impact.
- Brand association.
- Employee engagement.
- Community visibility.

---

## Sponsorship Options

Include:

- Contribution levels.
- Partnership benefits.
- Recognition opportunities.

---

## Impact Measurement

Explain:

- Expected outcomes.
- Reporting approach.
- Success indicators.

---

# 9. Corporate Partner Benefits

Depending on partnership level, TJF may provide:

- Recognition.
- Impact reports.
- Partnership certificates.
- Event visibility.
- Community engagement opportunities.
- Storytelling opportunities.

---

# 10. Sponsorship Agreement Requirements

Agreements should define:

- Partnership purpose.
- Contribution details.
- Duration.
- Responsibilities.
- Communication rights.
- Reporting requirements.
- Termination conditions.

---

# 11. Corporate Relationship Management

TJF should maintain:

- Corporate partner profiles.
- Meeting records.
- Agreements.
- Contribution history.
- Impact reports.

---

# 12. Corporate Engagement Activities

Activities may include:

- Corporate visits.
- Joint events.
- Impact presentations.
- Volunteer programmes.
- Community initiatives.

---

# 13. Corporate Sponsorship Risks

Potential risks:

## Reputation Risk

Mitigation:

- Sponsor due diligence.

---

## Misalignment Risk

Mitigation:

- Partnership assessment.

---

## Dependency Risk

Mitigation:

- Maintain diverse funding sources.

---

# 14. Measuring Corporate Partnership Success

Indicators include:

| Indicator | Measurement |
|---|---|
| Corporate partners acquired | Number |
| Sponsorship value | Amount |
| Partnership renewal rate | Percentage |
| Programme impact | Results achieved |
| Partner satisfaction | Feedback |

---

# 15. Corporate Partnership Review

Partnerships should be reviewed based on:

- Impact achieved.
- Relationship quality.
- Compliance.
- Future opportunities.

---

# Conclusion

Corporate sponsorship enables TJF to create stronger bridges between private sector resources and community development needs.

Through ethical partnerships, shared value creation and transparent engagement, TJF can develop sustainable relationships that accelerate positive impact.

> **Businesses create greater value when success is connected to social progress.**
