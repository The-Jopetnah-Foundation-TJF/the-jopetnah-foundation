# Fundraising Strategy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) is committed to building a sustainable resource base that enables the organization to expand its programmes, strengthen communities and create measurable long-term impact.

This Fundraising Strategy provides a structured approach for identifying, securing, managing and diversifying financial and non-financial resources.

The Strategy recognizes fundraising as a strategic organizational function requiring:

- Strong relationships.
- Clear communication.
- Evidence-based proposals.
- Responsible stewardship.
- Long-term partnerships.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Strategy is to:

- Establish TJF's fundraising direction.
- Define funding priorities.
- Strengthen donor engagement.
- Diversify funding sources.
- Improve fundraising effectiveness.
- Support organizational sustainability.

---

# 2. Fundraising Vision

## Vision Statement

To establish TJF as a trusted and sustainable development organization supported by a diverse network of strategic partners, donors and impact investors.

# 10. Fundraising Ethics

TJF fundraising shall:

- Represent programmes accurately.
- Respect beneficiary dignity.
- Avoid misleading claims.
- Protect donor trust.
- Maintain transparency.

TJF shall not accept resources that:

- Conflict with organizational values.
- Create unacceptable risks.
- Compromise independence.
