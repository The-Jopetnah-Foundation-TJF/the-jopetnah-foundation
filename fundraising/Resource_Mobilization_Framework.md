# Resource Mobilization Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that effective resource mobilization requires a coordinated institutional approach that goes beyond fundraising alone.

Resource mobilization includes the identification, acquisition, management and sustainability of financial, technical, knowledge and strategic resources required to achieve TJF's mission.

This Framework provides the operational structure for mobilizing resources through ethical, strategic and sustainable approaches.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Framework is to:

- Establish a structured resource mobilization system.
- Coordinate fundraising activities.
- Improve funding sustainability.
- Strengthen donor engagement.
- Guide resource planning.
- Support organizational growth.

---

# 2. Definition of Resource Mobilization

Resource mobilization refers to the process of identifying, attracting, securing and managing resources required to support TJF programmes and operations.

Resources may include:

- Financial resources.
- Technical expertise.
- Knowledge.
- Equipment.
- Networks.
- Strategic partnerships.
- Volunteer contributions.

---

# 3. Resource Mobilization Principles

## Strategic Alignment

Resources must support TJF's:

- Vision.
- Mission.
- Strategic priorities.
- Programme objectives.

---

## Sustainability

Mobilization efforts should create long-term organizational resilience.

---

## Partnership Orientation

Funders and supporters should be treated as strategic collaborators.

---

## Transparency

Resources must be managed responsibly and reported accurately.

---

## Ethical Practice

TJF will pursue resources consistent with:

- Organizational values.
- Legal requirements.
- Ethical standards.

# 8. Funding Pipeline Management

TJF shall maintain a resource mobilization pipeline containing:

| Stage | Description |
|---|---|
| Prospect Identified | Potential funder discovered |
| Qualification | Alignment assessed |
| Engagement | Relationship initiated |
| Proposal Development | Application prepared |
| Submitted | Awaiting decision |
| Awarded | Funding confirmed |
| Managed | Implementation phase |
| Completed | Reporting and closure |
