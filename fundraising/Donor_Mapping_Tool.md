# Donor Mapping Tool

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that effective fundraising requires strategic understanding of potential funding partners.

This Donor Mapping Tool provides a structured approach for identifying, researching, prioritizing and managing donor relationships.

The tool enables TJF to maintain a proactive funding pipeline rather than relying only on available opportunities.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this tool is to:

- Identify potential donors.
- Assess donor alignment.
- Prioritize engagement efforts.
- Track donor relationships.
- Improve fundraising planning.

---

# 2. Donor Mapping Principles

## Research-Based Engagement

Donor engagement should be based on understanding:

- Funding priorities.
- Geographic focus.
- Previous investments.
- Strategic interests.

---

## Strategic Alignment

TJF should prioritize donors whose objectives align with:

- TJF programmes.
- Target communities.
- Development priorities.

---

## Relationship Building

Successful fundraising requires long-term relationship development.

# 7. Donor Priority Ranking

## Priority A — Strategic Donors

Characteristics:

- Strong alignment.
- Significant funding potential.
- Long-term relationship opportunity.

Action:

High engagement priority.

---

## Priority B — Potential Partners

Characteristics:

- Moderate alignment.
- Developing relationship.

Action:

Relationship cultivation.

---

## Priority C — Opportunity-Based Donors

Characteristics:

- Limited engagement history.
- Specific funding opportunities.

Action:

Monitor opportunities.
