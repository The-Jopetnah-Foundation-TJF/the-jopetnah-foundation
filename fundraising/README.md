# Fundraising & Resource Mobilization Framework

## Introduction

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes that sustainable impact requires strong and diversified resource mobilization systems.

This Fundraising & Resource Mobilization Framework establishes the Foundation's approach to generating financial and non-financial resources that support programmes, operations and long-term organizational sustainability.

The framework guides TJF's engagement with:

- Institutional donors.
- Development agencies.
- Foundations.
- Corporate partners.
- Government programmes.
- Philanthropic individuals.
- Strategic investors.
- Community supporters.

---

# Purpose

The Fundraising & Resource Mobilization Framework exists to:

- Strengthen TJF's financial sustainability.
- Develop diverse funding streams.
- Improve donor engagement.
- Establish ethical fundraising practices.
- Support effective grant management.
- Increase institutional credibility.

---

# Resource Mobilization Philosophy

TJF believes resource mobilization is based on:

## Impact

Funding should support programmes that create measurable positive change.

---

## Partnership

Funders are viewed as strategic partners rather than only financial contributors.

---

## Accountability

All resources must be managed responsibly and transparently.

---

## Sustainability

Funding approaches should support long-term organizational resilience.

---

## Innovation

TJF encourages creative approaches to mobilizing resources.

# Fundraising Principles

## Alignment

Funding opportunities must support TJF's:

- Vision.
- Mission.
- Strategic priorities.

---

## Integrity

TJF will not accept funding that compromises:

- Values.
- Independence.
- Ethical standards.

---

## Transparency

TJF communicates honestly regarding:

- Programme results.
- Financial performance.
- Challenges.

---

## Compliance

All fundraising activities shall comply with:

- Applicable laws.
- Donor requirements.
- TJF policies.
