# Fundraising Compliance Guide

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) is committed to conducting fundraising activities with integrity, transparency and accountability.

This Fundraising Compliance Guide establishes the standards required to ensure that all fundraising activities are ethical, lawful and aligned with donor expectations.

The Guide protects:

- TJF's reputation.
- Donor confidence.
- Beneficiary dignity.
- Organizational integrity.

---

# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this Guide is to:

- Establish fundraising compliance standards.
- Prevent unethical fundraising practices.
- Strengthen accountability.
- Ensure responsible donor engagement.
- Support regulatory compliance.

---

# 2. Scope

This Guide applies to:

- Board members.
- Management.
- Employees.
- Volunteers.
- Fundraising representatives.
- Partners acting on behalf of TJF.

---

# 3. Fundraising Compliance Principles

## Integrity

TJF shall conduct fundraising honestly and responsibly.

---

## Transparency

All fundraising information must be:

- Accurate.
- Clear.
- Verifiable.

---

## Accountability

TJF accepts responsibility for:

- Funds received.
- Resources managed.
- Results delivered.

---

## Respect

Fundraising activities shall respect:

- Beneficiary dignity.
- Donor rights.
- Community values.

---

# 4. Ethical Fundraising Standards

TJF shall:

## Provide Accurate Information

Fundraising materials must accurately represent:

- Programmes.
- Beneficiaries.
- Expected outcomes.
- Financial needs.

---

## Avoid Misrepresentation

TJF shall not:

- Exaggerate impact.
- Provide false information.
- Manipulate emotional responses.

---

## Protect Beneficiary Dignity

Images and stories must:

- Respect privacy.
- Obtain consent where required.
- Avoid exploitation.

---

# 5. Donor Acceptance Standards

Before accepting funding, TJF should consider:

## Mission Compatibility

Does the funding support TJF objectives?

---

## Reputation Considerations

Could accepting funds create reputational risks?

---

## Legal Compliance

Does the funding comply with:

- Laws.
- Regulations.
- Donor requirements?

---

## Independence

Will funding compromise TJF's ability to operate independently?

---

# 6. Restricted Funding Compliance

Some funding may have restrictions.

TJF must maintain:

- Clear documentation.
- Separate tracking.
- Approved use of funds.
- Donor reporting.

Restricted funds must only be used for approved purposes.

---

# 7. Anti-Fraud Requirements

TJF prohibits:

- False reporting.
- Misuse of funds.
- Unauthorized expenditure.
- Financial manipulation.

Staff must report suspected fraud through approved reporting channels.

---

# 8. Financial Compliance

Fundraising resources must follow:

- Approved budgets.
- Financial procedures.
- Procurement requirements.
- Audit requirements.

Records must include:

- Receipts.
- Agreements.
- Reports.
- Supporting documentation.

---

# 9. Donor Communication Standards

TJF communications should provide:

- Accurate updates.
- Honest reporting.
- Appropriate recognition.

TJF shall communicate:

- Achievements.
- Challenges.
- Lessons learned.

---

# 10. Fundraising Approval Controls

Fundraising materials should receive approval before publication.

Approval may involve:

## Programme Team

Verifies:

- Technical accuracy.

---

## Finance Team

Verifies:

- Financial information.

---

## Communications Team

Verifies:

- Public messaging.

---

## Executive Director

Provides final authorization.

---

# 11. Conflict of Interest Management

Individuals involved in fundraising must disclose:

- Personal interests.
- Relationships affecting decisions.
- Potential conflicts.

Conflicts must be managed according to TJF policies.

---

# 12. Data Protection

Donor information must be:

- Collected responsibly.
- Stored securely.
- Accessed only by authorized persons.

TJF shall respect:

- Privacy.
- Confidentiality.
- Data protection requirements.

---

# 13. Third-Party Fundraising

External fundraisers or representatives must:

- Receive authorization.
- Follow TJF standards.
- Represent TJF accurately.

Unauthorized fundraising activities are prohibited.

---

# 14. Compliance Monitoring

Compliance reviews should assess:

- Fundraising activities.
- Donor agreements.
- Financial records.
- Communication materials.

---

# 15. Reporting Concerns

Concerns may include:

- Misuse of funds.
- False fundraising claims.
- Ethical violations.
- Donor misconduct.

Reports should be handled confidentially.

---

# 16. Training Requirements

Relevant personnel should receive training on:

- Ethical fundraising.
- Donor management.
- Compliance obligations.
- Financial accountability.

---

# 17. Compliance Checklist

Before fundraising activity:

☐ Purpose approved  
☐ Funding requirements reviewed  
☐ Messages verified  
☐ Budget confirmed  
☐ Compliance requirements checked  

During fundraising:

☐ Records maintained  
☐ Donor communication managed  
☐ Funds tracked  

After fundraising:

☐ Results reported  
☐ Lessons documented  
☐ Records archived  

---

# 18. Review and Improvement

This Guide shall be reviewed periodically to ensure continued alignment with:

- Legal requirements.
- Donor expectations.
- Best practices.

---

# Conclusion

Responsible fundraising is built on trust.

By maintaining strong compliance systems, ethical standards and transparent practices, The Jopetnah Foundation strengthens confidence among donors, partners and communities.

---

> **Trust is the foundation of sustainable fundraising; compliance protects that trust.**
