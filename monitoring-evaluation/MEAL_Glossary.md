# Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability and Learning (MEAL) Glossary

## Introduction

This Glossary provides standardized definitions for key Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability and Learning (MEAL) terminology used throughout The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF).

Its purpose is to promote consistent understanding among staff, partners, consultants, donors and stakeholders.

---

# A

## Accountability

The responsibility of an organization to explain, justify and take responsibility for its actions, decisions and results while remaining responsive to stakeholders.

---

## Activity

A specific action or task carried out using available resources to produce planned outputs.

Example:

Conducting community training sessions.

---

## Adaptive Management

A management approach where decisions and implementation strategies are adjusted based on evidence, learning and changing circumstances.

---

# B

## Baseline

The initial situation or condition measured before a programme intervention begins.

A baseline provides a reference point for measuring change over time.

---

## Beneficiary

An individual, household, community or group that receives or benefits from a programme, service or intervention.

The Foundation recognizes beneficiaries as active partners in development.

---

# C

## Contribution

The role an organization, programme or partnership plays in creating observed changes, recognizing that multiple factors may influence outcomes.

---

## Corrective Action

A step taken to address an identified problem, weakness or risk and prevent recurrence.

---

# D

## Data Quality Assessment (DQA)

A systematic review process used to determine whether data meets required quality standards, including accuracy, completeness, consistency, timeliness and reliability.

---

## Disaggregation

The process of separating data into categories to better understand differences among groups.

Examples:

- Gender
- Age
- Disability
- Location

---

# E

## Effectiveness

The extent to which a programme achieves its intended objectives and outcomes.

---

## Efficiency

The relationship between resources used and results achieved.

Efficiency considers whether resources are used responsibly and economically.

---

## Evaluation

A systematic and objective assessment of a programme, project or policy to determine relevance, effectiveness, efficiency, impact and sustainability.

---

## Evidence

Information that supports conclusions, decisions or assessments.

Evidence may come from:

- Data.
- Research.
- Evaluations.
- Surveys.
- Observations.

---

# I

## Impact

The long-term positive or negative changes resulting from an intervention, including social, economic, environmental or institutional changes.

---

## Impact Measurement

The process of assessing whether programmes contribute to meaningful and sustainable changes.

---

## Indicator

A measurable value used to track progress, performance or change.

---

## Input

Resources invested into a programme.

Examples:

- Funding.
- Staff.
- Equipment.
- Technology.

---

# L

## Learning

The process of gaining knowledge from experience, evidence and reflection in order to improve future decisions and actions.

---

## Lessons Learned

Knowledge gained from implementation experience that can improve future programmes or organizational practices.

---

## Logical Framework (Logframe)

A structured planning tool that connects programme objectives, indicators, verification methods and assumptions.

---

# M

## Monitoring

The continuous collection, analysis and use of information to track programme implementation and progress.

---

## MEAL

An integrated approach combining:

- Monitoring.
- Evaluation.
- Accountability.
- Learning.

It ensures programmes are measured, improved and responsive.

---

# O

## Outcome

The changes in behaviour, capacity, systems or conditions that occur as a result of programme outputs.

---

## Output

The immediate products, services or deliverables produced by programme activities.

Example:

Number of people trained.

---

# P

## Participatory Approach

An approach that actively involves stakeholders, especially communities, in planning, implementation, monitoring and evaluation.

---

## Programme

A group of related projects or activities designed to achieve strategic objectives.

---

## Project

A planned set of activities implemented within a defined period and budget to achieve specific results.

---

# R

## Result

A measurable change produced by an intervention.

Results may include:

- Outputs.
- Outcomes.
- Impacts.

---

## Results Framework

A structured system linking organizational goals, outcomes, outputs, indicators and assumptions.

---

## Risk

A potential event or condition that may negatively affect programme achievement.

---

# S

## Safeguarding

Actions taken to protect individuals, especially vulnerable groups, from harm, abuse, exploitation or neglect.

---

## Sustainability

The likelihood that positive programme results continue after external support ends.

---

## Stakeholder

Any individual, group, institution or organization that influences or is affected by the Foundation's activities.

---

# T

## Target

A specific level of achievement expected within a defined timeframe.

Example:

Training 1,000 youth by December.

---

## Theory of Change

A description of how and why a desired change is expected to occur through a series of linked actions and results.

---

# V

## Verification

The process of confirming that reported information is accurate and supported by evidence.

---

# Additional MEAL Concepts

## After-Action Review

A structured reflection process conducted after an activity to identify successes, challenges and improvements.

---

## Assumption

A condition believed necessary for programme success but outside the direct control of the organization.

---

## Case Study

A detailed examination of a particular programme experience, individual, community or situation to generate learning.

---

## Feedback Mechanism

A system that allows stakeholders to provide opinions, concerns, suggestions or complaints.

---

## Key Performance Indicator (KPI)

A measurable indicator used to assess progress toward strategic or operational objectives.

---

## Management Response

A documented response explaining how an organization will address recommendations from evaluations, audits or reviews.

---

## Monitoring Plan

A document describing what information will be collected, how it will be collected, when it will be collected and who is responsible.

---

## Reflection Session

A structured discussion where teams analyse experiences, evidence and lessons to improve future performance.

---

# Glossary Review

This Glossary should be updated periodically to reflect:

- New organizational practices.
- Donor requirements.
- Emerging development terminology.
- Changes in programme approaches.

---

> **A shared language creates shared understanding. Through consistent MEAL terminology, The Jopetnah Foundation strengthens collaboration, accountability and evidence-based decision-making.**
