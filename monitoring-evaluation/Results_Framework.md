# Results Framework

## Executive Summary

The Results Framework translates The Jopetnah Foundation's Theory of Change into a structured performance management system.

It provides a logical hierarchy linking strategic goals, outcomes, outputs, indicators and assumptions, enabling the Foundation to measure progress, demonstrate accountability and support evidence-based decision-making.

This Framework serves as the institutional reference for programme design, annual planning, donor reporting, monitoring, evaluation and organizational learning.

---

# Purpose

The Results Framework is intended to:

- Align programmes with the Foundation's Strategic Plan.
- Establish measurable performance expectations.
- Standardize organizational indicators.
- Support monitoring and evaluation.
- Improve donor reporting.
- Guide programme design.
- Strengthen institutional accountability.

---

# Results Hierarchy

The Foundation applies the following hierarchy:

```text
Vision
   │
   ▼
Strategic Goal
   │
   ▼
Strategic Outcomes
   │
   ▼
Programme Outputs
   │
   ▼
Activities
   │
   ▼
Inputs
```

Monitoring focuses primarily on **outputs**, while evaluation focuses on **outcomes** and **long-term impact**.

---

# Vision

Empowered communities that are resilient, inclusive and capable of achieving sustainable development.

---

# Strategic Goal

To improve the wellbeing, resilience and opportunities of individuals and communities through integrated, community-driven and sustainable development programmes.

---

# Strategic Outcome 1 — Improved Access to Quality Education

### Outcome Statement

Children, youth and adults have increased access to inclusive, relevant and quality education and skills development opportunities.

### Illustrative Outputs

- Schools supported.
- Teachers trained.
- Scholarships awarded.
- Learners enrolled in skills programmes.
- Digital learning initiatives established.

### Sample Indicators

- Number of learners supported.
- Percentage of programme participants completing training.
- Number of educational institutions strengthened.
- Percentage increase in school attendance among supported learners.

---

# Strategic Outcome 2 — Improved Health and Wellbeing

### Outcome Statement

Communities experience improved health knowledge, preventive practices and access to essential health services.

### Illustrative Outputs

- Community health outreaches conducted.
- Health education sessions delivered.
- Health workers trained.
- WASH initiatives implemented.

### Sample Indicators

- Number of individuals reached through health programmes.
- Percentage of participants adopting recommended health practices.
- Number of community health initiatives completed.

---

# Strategic Outcome 3 — Strengthened Sustainable Livelihoods

### Outcome Statement

Households improve their economic resilience through increased income opportunities and financial inclusion.

### Illustrative Outputs

- Entrepreneurs trained.
- Village savings groups established.
- Vocational training programmes completed.
- Small enterprises supported.

### Sample Indicators

- Number of entrepreneurs supported.
- Percentage of participants reporting increased income.
- Number of jobs created or sustained.
- Number of functional savings groups.

---

# Strategic Outcome 4 — Increased Environmental Sustainability

### Outcome Statement

Communities adopt sustainable environmental practices that improve resilience to climate and environmental challenges.

### Illustrative Outputs

- Trees planted.
- Environmental awareness campaigns conducted.
- Climate-smart agriculture demonstrations established.
- Waste management initiatives implemented.

### Sample Indicators

- Number of trees planted and surviving.
- Number of households adopting climate-smart practices.
- Area restored or protected.
- Number of environmental awareness activities completed.

---

# Strategic Outcome 5 — Stronger Institutional Capacity

### Outcome Statement

The Foundation and its partners strengthen governance, systems and organizational effectiveness.

### Illustrative Outputs

- Policies developed or updated.
- Staff trained.
- Digital systems implemented.
- Governance meetings conducted.
- Monitoring systems strengthened.

### Sample Indicators

- Percentage of planned policies approved.
- Number of staff completing capacity-building activities.
- Timeliness of donor reporting.
- Compliance rate with internal policies.

# Cross-Cutting Outcomes

The Foundation integrates the following across all strategic outcomes:

- Gender equality
- Youth empowerment
- Disability inclusion
- Child safeguarding
- Climate resilience
- Human rights
- Community participation
- Digital inclusion

Cross-cutting indicators should be incorporated into programme-specific results frameworks where appropriate.

---

# Indicator Management

Each institutional indicator should have:

- Unique indicator code.
- Indicator definition.
- Unit of measurement.
- Baseline.
- Annual target.
- Data source.
- Collection frequency.
- Responsible officer.
- Verification method.

Detailed specifications are maintained in the **Indicator Reference Guide**.

---

# Data Sources

Common data sources include:

- Programme registers
- Attendance records
- Surveys
- Interviews
- Focus group discussions
- Administrative records
- Financial reports
- Observation
- GIS and digital systems
- Evaluation reports

Data should be collected in accordance with the Data Quality Assurance Framework.

---

# Performance Review

Performance should be reviewed through:

- Monthly operational reviews.
- Quarterly programme reviews.
- Mid-year performance assessments.
- Annual organizational performance reviews.
- Board reporting.
- Donor reporting.
- Independent evaluations where applicable.

---

# Risk Considerations

Achievement of results may be affected by:

- Funding availability.
- Community participation.
- Political and regulatory changes.
- Environmental events.
- Public health emergencies.
- Security conditions.
- Institutional capacity.
- Partner performance.

Programme teams should monitor these risks and implement mitigation measures where feasible.

---

# Continuous Learning

Performance information should be used to:

- Improve programme design.
- Adjust implementation approaches.
- Inform strategic decisions.
- Strengthen partnerships.
- Enhance accountability.
- Share lessons across programmes.

Results should support organizational learning rather than compliance alone.

---

# Review

This Results Framework shall be reviewed every two years, or sooner if significant strategic, operational or contextual changes occur.

---

# Conclusion

The Results Framework provides a common structure for planning, monitoring and evaluating the Foundation's work.

By linking strategic intent to measurable outcomes and outputs, it enables The Jopetnah Foundation to demonstrate accountability, improve programme effectiveness and deliver sustainable impact for the communities it serves.

---

> *"Results are more than numbers—they represent meaningful changes in the lives of people and communities. Our commitment is to measure those changes with integrity, learn from them, and use that knowledge to create even greater impact."*
