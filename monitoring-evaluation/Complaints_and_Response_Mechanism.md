# Complaints and Response Mechanism

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) is committed to maintaining a culture of accountability, transparency, respect and ethical conduct.

This Complaints and Response Mechanism establishes a structured process for receiving, assessing, investigating and responding to complaints from beneficiaries, staff, partners, donors and other stakeholders.

The mechanism ensures that concerns are handled fairly, confidentially and in a timely manner while supporting continuous organizational improvement.

---

# Purpose

The purpose of this Mechanism is to:

- Provide accessible channels for raising concerns.
- Promote accountability and transparency.
- Protect stakeholders from harm.
- Support timely resolution of complaints.
- Identify systemic issues.
- Strengthen organizational learning.
- Maintain trust with communities and partners.

---

# Scope

This Mechanism applies to complaints relating to:

- Programme activities.
- Staff conduct.
- Partner behaviour.
- Service quality.
- Misuse of resources.
- Ethical concerns.
- Fraud or corruption concerns.
- Safeguarding issues.
- Discrimination or harassment.
- Breaches of organizational policies.

---

# Guiding Principles

## Accessibility

All stakeholders should have reasonable access to complaint channels.

---

## Confidentiality

Information relating to complaints should be protected and shared only with authorized persons.

---

## Fairness

Complaints should be reviewed objectively and without bias.

---

## Respect

All complainants and respondents should be treated with dignity.

---

## Timeliness

Complaints should be acknowledged and addressed within reasonable timelines.

---

## Non-Retaliation

No individual should face retaliation for raising a genuine concern.

---

# Who Can Submit a Complaint?

Complaints may be submitted by:

- Programme participants.
- Community members.
- Employees.
- Volunteers.
- Partners.
- Suppliers.
- Donors.
- Other stakeholders.

---

# Types of Complaints

## Programme Complaints

Examples:

- Poor service delivery.
- Exclusion from programmes.
- Failure to meet commitments.

---

## Staff Conduct Complaints

Examples:

- Unprofessional behaviour.
- Misconduct.
- Abuse of authority.

---

## Financial and Integrity Complaints

Examples:

- Fraud concerns.
- Corruption allegations.
- Misuse of organizational resources.

---

## Safeguarding Complaints

Examples:

- Exploitation.
- Abuse.
- Harm to children or vulnerable persons.
- Sexual misconduct.

Safeguarding complaints require immediate escalation according to safeguarding procedures.

---

# Complaint Channels

The Foundation may receive complaints through:

- Written submissions.
- Email channels.
- Telephone reporting.
- Website forms.
- Community meetings.
- Direct reporting to designated officers.
- Partner reporting mechanisms.

The Foundation should provide channels appropriate to local contexts.

---

# Complaint Handling Process

The standard process is:

```text
Complaint Received
        │
        ▼
Registration
        │
        ▼
Acknowledgement
        │
        ▼
Classification
        │
        ▼
Assessment
        │
        ▼
Investigation (where required)
        │
        ▼
Decision & Response
        │
        ▼
Corrective Action
        │
        ▼
Closure & Learning
```

---

# Complaint Registration

Each complaint should receive:

- Unique reference number.
- Date received.
- Complaint category.
- Description.
- Location/programme area.
- Assigned responsible officer.
- Status.

Sensitive information should be handled securely.

---

# Complaint Classification

Complaints should be classified according to:

- Severity.
- Urgency.
- Risk level.
- Required response.

Examples:

| Category | Response Priority |
|---|---|
| General concern | Standard |
| Programme issue | Priority |
| Financial misconduct | High |
| Safeguarding concern | Immediate |

---

# Investigation Standards

Where investigation is required:

- Investigators should be appropriately assigned.
- Conflicts of interest must be avoided.
- Evidence should be documented.
- Confidentiality should be maintained.
- Findings should be objective.

---

# Response Standards

Responses should:

- Acknowledge the complaint.
- Explain actions taken.
- Provide outcomes where appropriate.
- Maintain confidentiality.
- Communicate respectfully.

Where details cannot be disclosed, the complainant should receive an appropriate explanation.

---

# Escalation Procedures

Complaints should be escalated when they involve:

- Serious misconduct.
- Safeguarding concerns.
- Legal issues.
- Significant financial risks.
- Senior leadership involvement.
- Repeated unresolved issues.

---

# Roles and Responsibilities

## Board of Trustees

Responsible for:

- Oversight of serious complaints.
- Ensuring organizational accountability.

---

## Executive Director

Responsible for:

- Ensuring effective implementation.
- Reviewing significant complaints.
- Supporting corrective action.

---

## MEAL Team

Responsible for:

- Maintaining complaint records.
- Monitoring trends.
- Reporting findings.
- Supporting accountability learning.

---

## Safeguarding Officer

Responsible for:

- Managing safeguarding-related complaints.
- Ensuring survivor-centred approaches.
- Coordinating appropriate referrals.

---

## Programme Teams

Responsible for:

- Promoting awareness of complaint channels.
- Supporting resolution processes.
- Implementing corrective actions.

---

# Complaint Tracking

The Foundation should maintain a complaint register containing:

- Complaint reference.
- Date received.
- Category.
- Status.
- Responsible officer.
- Resolution date.
- Corrective actions.

---

# Learning from Complaints

Complaint analysis should identify:

- Recurring issues.
- Programme weaknesses.
- Operational risks.
- Training needs.
- Policy improvements.

Complaints should be viewed as opportunities for strengthening organizational performance.

---

# Protection Against Retaliation

The Foundation prohibits retaliation against individuals who:

- Submit complaints.
- Report concerns.
- Participate in investigations.
- Provide information honestly.

Any retaliation may result in disciplinary action.

---

# Confidentiality and Data Protection

Complaint information shall:

- Be securely stored.
- Be accessible only to authorized personnel.
- Protect personal identities where required.
- Follow applicable data protection requirements.

---

# Monitoring the Mechanism

The Foundation may track:

- Number of complaints received.
- Complaint categories.
- Resolution rates.
- Average response time.
- Repeat issues.
- Corrective actions completed.

---

# Review

This Mechanism shall be reviewed every two years or sooner where lessons learned, organizational changes or regulatory requirements require updates.

---

> **A trusted organization is one that listens, responds and improves. The Jopetnah Foundation views complaints not as threats, but as opportunities to strengthen accountability, protect stakeholders and improve impact.**
