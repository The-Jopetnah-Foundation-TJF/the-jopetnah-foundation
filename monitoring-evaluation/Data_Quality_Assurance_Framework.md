# Data Quality Assurance (DQA) Framework

## Executive Summary

The Data Quality Assurance (DQA) Framework establishes the standards, processes and responsibilities that The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) applies to ensure that programme and organizational data are accurate, complete, timely, reliable and secure.

Reliable data is fundamental to sound decision-making, accountability, donor confidence and continuous organizational learning.

This Framework applies to all programme, operational, financial and organizational data used for planning, monitoring, evaluation and reporting.

---

# Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Ensure high-quality programme data.
- Strengthen evidence-based decision-making.
- Improve donor confidence.
- Support monitoring and evaluation.
- Standardize data management practices.
- Reduce reporting errors.
- Promote organizational accountability.

---

# Scope

This Framework applies to:

- Programme monitoring data.
- Evaluation data.
- Beneficiary information.
- Financial performance data.
- Human resources data used for reporting.
- Partner-submitted programme data.
- Digital and paper-based records.

---

# Data Quality Standards

The Foundation adopts six core dimensions of data quality.

| Dimension | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| Accuracy | Data correctly represents reality. |
| Completeness | Required data is fully recorded. |
| Consistency | Data is reported uniformly across systems and periods. |
| Timeliness | Data is available when needed. |
| Reliability | Results can be reproduced using the same methods. |
| Integrity | Data is protected against unauthorized alteration or loss. |

# Data Collection Standards

Data collection tools should:

- Be aligned with approved indicators.
- Use standardized definitions.
- Minimize duplication.
- Be practical and user-friendly.
- Include clear instructions.
- Respect ethical and safeguarding requirements.

Where feasible, digital data collection systems should be used to improve efficiency and reduce transcription errors.

---

# Verification Procedures

Programme teams should verify data before submission by:

- Checking completeness.
- Confirming calculations.
- Reviewing supporting documentation.
- Validating unusual values.
- Comparing with previous reporting periods where appropriate.

Any discrepancies should be documented and resolved before reporting.

---

# Data Quality Assessments (DQAs)

The Foundation should conduct periodic DQAs to assess:

- Indicator definitions.
- Data collection methods.
- Source documentation.
- Data storage.
- Reporting accuracy.
- Compliance with organizational standards.

Findings should be documented together with corrective actions.

---

# Roles and Responsibilities

## Board of Trustees

- Provides oversight of organizational accountability.
- Reviews significant findings from evaluations and audits.

---

## Executive Director

- Promotes a culture of evidence-based management.
- Ensures adequate resources for data quality systems.

---

## MEAL Team

Responsible for:

- Developing data collection tools.
- Maintaining indicator definitions.
- Conducting DQAs.
- Training programme staff.
- Supporting data analysis.
- Producing quality assurance reports.

---

## Programme Teams

Responsible for:

- Collecting accurate data.
- Maintaining source documents.
- Conducting first-level verification.
- Reporting data on schedule.
- Correcting identified errors.

---

## Finance Team

Responsible for:

- Verifying financial information.
- Ensuring consistency between financial and programme reports where applicable.

---

# Data Storage and Security

Programme data should be:

- Stored securely.
- Backed up regularly.
- Protected from unauthorized access.
- Retained in accordance with the Foundation's records management requirements.

Sensitive information should only be accessible to authorized personnel.

---

# Confidentiality

The Foundation shall:

- Protect personal information.
- Limit access to confidential data.
- Obtain informed consent where required.
- Comply with applicable data protection laws and organizational policies.

Beneficiary dignity and privacy should be respected throughout the data lifecycle.

---

# Data Quality Improvement

Where weaknesses are identified, corrective actions may include:

- Staff refresher training.
- Revision of data collection tools.
- Improved supervision.
- System enhancements.
- Additional verification exercises.
- Updates to indicator definitions.

Corrective actions should be tracked until completed.

---

# Reporting

Data quality should be reviewed through:

- Monthly programme reviews.
- Quarterly performance meetings.
- Annual organizational reviews.
- Internal audits.
- External evaluations where appropriate.

Reports should identify trends, risks and opportunities for improvement.

---

# Key Performance Indicators for Data Quality

The Foundation may monitor indicators such as:

- Percentage of reports submitted on time.
- Percentage of reports requiring correction.
- Number of completed DQAs.
- Percentage of staff trained in data quality.
- Percentage of indicators with complete supporting documentation.

These indicators should be reviewed periodically to strengthen organizational performance.

---

# Continuous Improvement

The Foundation is committed to continuously strengthening its data quality systems by:

- Adopting appropriate digital technologies.
- Investing in staff capacity.
- Learning from audits and evaluations.
- Updating standards in line with emerging good practice.
- Encouraging a culture of accountability and evidence.

---

# Review

This Framework shall be reviewed every two years or earlier where organizational needs, technology or regulatory requirements change.

---
