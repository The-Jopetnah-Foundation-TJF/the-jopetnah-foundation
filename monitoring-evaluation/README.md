# Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability and Learning (MEAL)

## Introduction

The Monitoring, Evaluation, Accountability and Learning (MEAL) framework enables The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) to demonstrate measurable impact, strengthen accountability, improve programme quality and promote evidence-based decision-making.

This repository contains the Foundation's institutional guidance for planning, monitoring, evaluating, learning from and improving programmes and partnerships.

Our MEAL approach supports transparency, continuous improvement and responsible stewardship of resources entrusted to the Foundation.

---

# Purpose

The MEAL documentation aims to:

- Improve programme quality.
- Measure results consistently.
- Strengthen organizational accountability.
- Support learning and innovation.
- Improve evidence-based decision making.
- Meet donor reporting requirements.
- Promote transparency.
- Strengthen institutional effectiveness.

---

# Guiding Principles

The Foundation's MEAL system is guided by the following principles:

## Accountability

We remain accountable to communities, donors, partners, staff and governing bodies.

---

## Evidence

Programme decisions are informed by reliable data and objective analysis.

---

## Learning

Every programme provides opportunities for continuous learning and organizational improvement.

---

## Participation

Communities and stakeholders actively contribute to programme monitoring and evaluation.

---

## Transparency

Programme performance and lessons learned are communicated openly and responsibly.

---

## Quality

Data should be accurate, timely, complete and fit for purpose.

---

# Objectives

The Foundation seeks to:

- Measure programme performance.
- Assess outcomes and long-term impact.
- Improve programme effectiveness.
- Strengthen accountability.
- Enhance donor confidence.
- Support adaptive management.
- Generate organizational knowledge.
- Improve strategic decision-making.

# Roles and Responsibilities

Provides governance oversight and ensures that organizational performance is reviewed against strategic objectives.

---

---

## MEAL Function

- Monitoring systems
- Evaluation planning
- Data quality
- Reporting
- Learning processes
- Indicator management
- Evidence generation

## Programme Teams

- Data collection
- Activity monitoring
- Beneficiary engagement
- Reporting
- Continuous improvement
