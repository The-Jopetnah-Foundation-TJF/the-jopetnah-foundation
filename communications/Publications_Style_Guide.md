# Publications Style Guide

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) is committed to producing clear, accurate and professional publications that strengthen stakeholder confidence and communicate organizational impact.

This Guide establishes editorial and design standards for all official TJF publications to ensure consistency in language, branding, formatting and accessibility.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Guide aims to:

- Promote consistent writing standards.
- Strengthen TJF's professional identity.
- Improve readability.
- Ensure publication quality.
- Support accessibility and inclusivity.
- Protect the Foundation's reputation.

---

# 2. Scope

This Guide applies to all official TJF publications, including:

- Annual Reports
- Strategic Plans
- Policies and Procedures
- Research Reports
- Grant Proposals
- Programme Reports
- Brochures
- Newsletters
- Fact Sheets
- Case Studies
- Presentations
- Website Articles
- Blog Posts
- Press Releases

---

# 3. Writing Principles

All publications should be:

- Clear.
- Accurate.
- Concise.
- Respectful.
- Inclusive.
- Evidence-based.
- Audience-focused.

Avoid unnecessary jargon and explain technical terms where needed.

---

# 4. Tone of Voice

TJF's writing should be:

- Professional
- Trustworthy
- Hopeful
- Respectful
- Practical
- Objective
- Solution-oriented

Avoid exaggerated claims or language that undermines the dignity of beneficiaries.

---

# 5. Language Standards

Preferred spelling:

- Use one English standard consistently throughout a document (British English is recommended unless a donor or partner specifies otherwise).

Grammar:

- Write in active voice where appropriate.
- Use complete sentences.
- Keep paragraphs concise.
- Avoid excessive capitalization.

---

# 6. Inclusive Language

Publications should:

- Respect all people and communities.
- Avoid discriminatory language.
- Use person-first language where appropriate (e.g., "persons with disabilities").
- Reflect diversity in examples and imagery.

---

# 7. Document Structure

Recommended order:

1. Cover Page
2. Table of Contents (for longer documents)
3. Executive Summary
4. Introduction
5. Main Content
6. Conclusions
7. Recommendations (where applicable)
8. Annexes
9. Version Control

---

# 8. Headings

Use a clear heading hierarchy.

Example:

```
# Heading 1

## Heading 2

### Heading 3

#### Heading 4
```

Avoid skipping heading levels.

---

# 9. Tables

Tables should:

- Have clear titles.
- Include consistent column headings.
- Be easy to read.
- Use units of measurement where applicable.

Example:

| Indicator | Target | Result |
|-----------|--------|-------:|
| Households reached | 500 | 542 |

---

# 10. Figures and Images

Images should:

- Support the content.
- Be high quality.
- Include captions where appropriate.
- Respect privacy and consent requirements.

Charts should:

- Clearly label axes.
- Identify data sources.
- Use accessible colour combinations.

---

# 11. Citations and References

Where external information is used:

- Cite reliable sources.
- Use a consistent citation style throughout the document.
- Include a references section for research publications.

---

# 12. Numbers and Dates

Recommended formats:

- Date: **24 July 2026**
- Currency: **UGX 1,250,000**
- Percentages: **45%**
- Large numbers: **1.5 million**

Use commas as thousands separators where appropriate.

---

# 13. Branding

All publications should use:

- Approved TJF logo.
- Approved colour palette.
- Official typography.
- Standard cover design.
- Approved templates where available.

Refer to the **Brand Identity Guidelines**.

---

# 14. Accessibility

Publications should:

- Use readable font sizes.
- Provide sufficient colour contrast.
- Include alternative text for digital images where practical.
- Avoid relying solely on colour to convey meaning.

---

# 15. Quality Assurance

Before publication:

- Verify factual accuracy.
- Check grammar and spelling.
- Confirm branding compliance.
- Review formatting.
- Obtain required approvals.

A final proofread should be completed before release.

---

# 16. Approval Process

Depending on the publication type, approvals may be required from:

- Programme Manager
- Communications Department
- Executive Director
- Board of Trustees (for strategic publications)

No publication should be released without the appropriate approval.

---

# 17. Version Control

Major publications should include a version history.

Example:

| Version | Date | Description | Approved By |
|---------|------|-------------|-------------|
| 1.0 | TBD | Initial Release | Executive Director |

---

# 18. Review

This Guide should be reviewed:

- Every three years.
- After significant branding updates.
- When new publication formats are adopted.
- Following major organizational changes.

---

# Conclusion

Consistent publications strengthen credibility, improve stakeholder understanding and reinforce The Jopetnah Foundation's commitment to professionalism and accountability.

By following this Style Guide, TJF ensures that every publication reflects the quality, integrity and impact of the Foundation's work.

---

> **Every publication represents the voice, values and credibility of The Jopetnah Foundation.**
