# Advocacy and Public Affairs Framework

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) recognizes advocacy as an essential component of sustainable development. While direct programme implementation improves lives, advocacy helps create the policies, institutions and systems that enable long-term change.

This Framework provides guidance for ethical, evidence-based and non-partisan advocacy that advances TJF's mission while strengthening relationships with government, development partners, civil society, communities and the private sector.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Framework aims to:

- Promote evidence-informed public policy.
- Strengthen stakeholder engagement.
- Amplify community voices.
- Support sustainable development.
- Protect TJF's independence and credibility.
- Ensure advocacy activities remain aligned with TJF's mission.

---

# 2. Scope

This Framework applies to:

- Board of Trustees.
- Executive leadership.
- Employees.
- Volunteers.
- Consultants.
- Contractors.
- Authorized representatives acting on behalf of TJF.

It covers:

- Policy dialogue.
- Public awareness campaigns.
- Research dissemination.
- Government engagement.
- Coalition participation.
- Public consultations.
- Advocacy campaigns.

---

# 3. Guiding Principles

Advocacy should be:

- Non-partisan.
- Evidence-based.
- Respectful.
- Inclusive.
- Transparent.
- Lawful.
- Community-centered.
- Collaborative.

---

# 4. Advocacy Objectives

TJF's advocacy seeks to:

- Improve policies affecting communities.
- Increase awareness of development challenges.
- Encourage accountability.
- Promote equitable access to opportunities and services.
- Strengthen partnerships across sectors.

---

# 5. Advocacy Approaches

TJF may engage through:

- Policy briefs.
- Research reports.
- Public consultations.
- Community dialogues.
- Roundtable discussions.
- Conferences and forums.
- Media engagement.
- Digital campaigns.
- Coalition building.
- Technical advisory support.

Advocacy methods should always be appropriate to the issue and context.

---

# 6. Public Affairs

Public affairs activities may include engagement with:

- National government institutions.
- Local governments.
- Development partners.
- Regulatory authorities.
- Academic institutions.
- Professional associations.
- Civil society organizations.
- Corporate partners.

Relationships should be based on mutual respect, transparency and shared development objectives.

---

# 7. Non-Partisanship

TJF does not endorse political parties or political candidates.

The Foundation may:

- Engage constructively with public institutions.
- Participate in policy discussions.
- Provide technical expertise.
- Share research findings.
- Advocate for issues consistent with its mission.

All engagements should maintain organizational neutrality and independence.

---

# 8. Evidence-Based Advocacy

Advocacy positions should be informed by:

- Programme experience.
- Monitoring and evaluation findings.
- Research.
- Community feedback.
- National development priorities.
- International good practices.

Claims should be supported by credible evidence.

---

# 9. Community Participation

Communities should be meaningfully involved in advocacy by:

- Sharing lived experiences.
- Participating in consultations.
- Identifying priorities.
- Contributing to proposed solutions.

Participation should be voluntary, respectful and inclusive.

---

# 10. Partnerships and Coalitions

TJF may join networks or coalitions where they:

- Align with TJF's mission.
- Operate lawfully.
- Respect organizational independence.
- Promote constructive dialogue.

Partnerships should be documented through appropriate agreements where required.

---

# 11. Communications

Public advocacy messages should:

- Be factually accurate.
- Use respectful language.
- Reflect approved organizational positions.
- Be coordinated through the Communications Department where appropriate.

Media engagement should follow the Media Relations Policy.

---

# 12. Risk Management

Potential advocacy risks include:

- Reputational risk.
- Legal risk.
- Security risk.
- Stakeholder misunderstanding.
- Misinformation.

Advocacy plans should identify and manage these risks before implementation.

---

# 13. Monitoring and Evaluation

Advocacy effectiveness may be assessed through indicators such as:

| Indicator | Measurement |
|-----------|-------------|
| Policy submissions made | Number |
| Public consultations attended | Number |
| Advocacy campaigns delivered | Number |
| Stakeholder engagement activities | Number |
| Policy or practice changes influenced | Qualitative / Quantitative |
| Media coverage | Number |

Lessons learned should inform future advocacy efforts.

---

# 14. Roles and Responsibilities

## Board of Trustees

- Provides strategic oversight for significant advocacy positions.

---

## Executive Director

- Approves major advocacy initiatives.
- Represents TJF in high-level engagements or delegates authority.

---

## Communications & Public Relations Department

Responsible for:

- Coordinating advocacy communications.
- Supporting campaigns.
- Managing media engagement.
- Protecting brand consistency.

---

## Programme Teams

Responsible for:

- Providing technical expertise.
- Contributing evidence and case studies.
- Engaging with stakeholders within approved mandates.

---

## All Staff

All personnel should:

- Follow this Framework.
- Avoid unauthorized public positions.
- Respect TJF's non-partisan stance.
- Support evidence-based advocacy.

---

# 15. Review

This Framework should be reviewed:

- Every three years.
- Following significant legislative or policy changes.
- After major advocacy campaigns.
- When organizational priorities evolve.

---

# Conclusion

Responsible advocacy strengthens systems, empowers communities and contributes to sustainable development.

Through this Framework, The Jopetnah Foundation commits to constructive, evidence-based and non-partisan engagement that advances its mission while maintaining public trust and institutional integrity.

---

> **Advocacy is most effective when it amplifies evidence, respects dialogue and empowers communities to shape their own future.**
