# Website Content Governance

## Executive Summary

The official website of The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) is the Foundation's primary digital information platform. It communicates the Foundation's mission, programmes, impact, governance, partnership opportunities and public resources.

This document establishes governance standards for planning, creating, reviewing, approving, publishing and maintaining website content to ensure accuracy, accessibility, security and consistency.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Governance Framework aims to:

- Ensure website content is accurate and current.
- Protect TJF's reputation and credibility.
- Promote accessibility and inclusion.
- Support transparency and accountability.
- Improve user experience.
- Strengthen cybersecurity coordination.
- Support fundraising and partnership development.

---

# 2. Scope

This Framework applies to all content published on:

- Official TJF websites.
- Microsites.
- Campaign landing pages.
- Online knowledge repositories.
- Web-based donor portals maintained by TJF.

Content includes:

- Text.
- Images.
- Videos.
- Downloadable publications.
- Forms.
- Interactive tools.
- Event information.
- News articles.
- Blogs.

---

# 3. Guiding Principles

Website content should be:

- Accurate.
- Timely.
- Relevant.
- Accessible.
- Inclusive.
- Secure.
- Consistent with TJF branding.
- Audience-focused.

---

# 4. Content Categories

Typical website sections include:

- About TJF
- Mission, Vision and Values
- Governance
- Programmes
- News and Events
- Publications
- Careers
- Volunteer Opportunities
- Partnerships
- Procurement Notices
- Contact Information
- Donate
- Privacy Notice
- Policies and Resources

---

# 5. Content Ownership

Each section should have a designated content owner.

| Website Section | Content Owner |
|----------------|---------------|
| About TJF | Communications Department |
| Governance | Executive Office |
| Programmes | Programme Managers |
| Publications | Communications Department |
| Careers | Human Resources |
| Procurement | Procurement Unit |
| News | Communications Department |

Content owners are responsible for ensuring that information remains accurate and up to date.

---

# 6. Content Approval Workflow

All new content should follow this workflow:

```
Draft Content
      │
      ▼
Technical Review
      │
      ▼
Communications Review
      │
      ▼
Executive Approval (where required)
      │
      ▼
Publication
      │
      ▼
Periodic Review
```

Major strategic publications should receive Executive Director approval before publication.

---

# 7. Editorial Standards

Website content should:

- Use plain language where appropriate.
- Follow the TJF Publications Style Guide.
- Be concise and audience-focused.
- Include clear calls to action where relevant.
- Avoid outdated information.

---

# 8. Accessibility

The website should strive to meet recognized accessibility standards.

Content creators should:

- Use descriptive headings.
- Provide alternative text for images.
- Caption videos where practical.
- Ensure sufficient colour contrast.
- Avoid using images as the sole method of conveying important information.
- Design content for mobile and desktop users.

---

# 9. Search Engine Optimization (SEO)

Content should:

- Use meaningful page titles.
- Include descriptive headings.
- Use relevant keywords naturally.
- Provide concise meta descriptions.
- Include descriptive link text.
- Avoid duplicate content.

SEO should never compromise readability or accuracy.

---

# 10. Multilingual Content

Where resources permit, priority information may be translated into relevant languages used by TJF's stakeholders.

Translations should:

- Be professionally reviewed.
- Preserve the intended meaning.
- Be updated alongside the original content.

---

# 11. Privacy and Data Protection

Forms that collect personal information should:

- Explain why data is collected.
- Request only necessary information.
- Comply with applicable data protection laws.
- Link to TJF's Privacy Notice.

---

# 12. Cybersecurity Coordination

Website administrators should:

- Apply security updates promptly.
- Monitor for unauthorized changes.
- Use secure authentication methods.
- Restrict administrative access.
- Maintain regular backups.
- Coordinate with the ICT Department on security incidents.

---

# 13. Content Review Schedule

Content should be reviewed at least:

| Content Type | Review Frequency |
|-------------|------------------|
| News | As needed |
| Programme Pages | Every 6 months |
| Governance Information | Annually |
| Policies | Following revision |
| Contact Information | Quarterly |
| Publications | As new editions are released |

Outdated content should be updated, archived or removed.

---

# 14. Archiving

Historical content should be archived when it is no longer current but remains valuable for institutional records.

Archived content should be clearly identified to avoid confusion.

---

# 15. Analytics

Website performance should be monitored using indicators such as:

- Visitors.
- Page views.
- Downloads.
- Time on page.
- Bounce rate.
- Donation conversions.
- Contact form submissions.

Analytics should inform continuous improvement.

---

# 16. Roles and Responsibilities

## Executive Director

- Approves significant strategic website content.
- Provides oversight for major announcements.

---

## Communications Department

Responsible for:

- Editorial quality.
- Brand consistency.
- Content scheduling.
- Website updates.
- Analytics.
- Publication management.

---

## ICT Department

Responsible for:

- Hosting.
- Technical maintenance.
- Security.
- System backups.
- Platform availability.

---

## Programme Teams

Responsible for:

- Providing accurate programme information.
- Reviewing technical content.
- Notifying Communications of updates.

---

# 17. Monitoring and Compliance

Periodic reviews should assess:

- Content accuracy.
- Accessibility.
- Security.
- Brand consistency.
- User experience.
- Regulatory compliance.

Findings should be documented and acted upon.

---

# 18. Review

This Governance Framework should be reviewed:

- Annually.
- Following major website redesigns.
- After significant cybersecurity incidents.
- When communication technologies or legal requirements change.

---

# Conclusion

The TJF website is a strategic communication and accountability platform.

By applying structured governance, clear editorial standards and effective collaboration between programme, communications and ICT teams, The Jopetnah Foundation ensures that its website remains trusted, informative and aligned with its mission.

---

> **A well-governed website is more than a digital presence—it is a public expression of transparency, professionalism and impact.**
