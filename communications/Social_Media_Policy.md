# Social Media Policy

## Executive Summary

The Jopetnah Foundation (TJF) uses social media to inform, educate, engage and collaborate with communities, partners, donors and the public.

This Policy establishes standards for the responsible management of official social media accounts and provides guidance for staff and volunteers whose online activities may be associated with the Foundation.

The Policy supports transparency, professionalism, digital security and constructive public engagement.

---

# 1. Purpose

This Policy aims to:

- Protect TJF's reputation.
- Promote consistent and accurate messaging.
- Encourage meaningful stakeholder engagement.
- Reduce digital communication risks.
- Safeguard confidential information.
- Support accessible and inclusive communication.

---

# 2. Scope

This Policy applies to:

- Official TJF social media accounts.
- Employees.
- Volunteers.
- Board members.
- Consultants.
- Contractors.
- Individuals authorized to post on behalf of TJF.

It also provides guidance for personal social media use where there is a clear connection to the Foundation.

---

# 3. Official Platforms

TJF may maintain official accounts on platforms such as:

- LinkedIn
- Facebook
- Instagram
- X (formerly Twitter)
- YouTube
- TikTok
- WhatsApp Channels (where appropriate)
- Other platforms approved by the Communications Department

Only authorized accounts may use TJF's name, logo or official branding.

---

# 4. Guiding Principles

Social media activity should be:

- Accurate.
- Respectful.
- Inclusive.
- Timely.
- Transparent.
- Professional.
- Evidence-based.
- Consistent with TJF's mission and values.

---

# 5. Account Governance

The Communications & Public Relations Department is responsible for:

- Creating official accounts.
- Maintaining account credentials.
- Assigning administrative access.
- Monitoring activity.
- Coordinating content.
- Archiving content where required.

Administrative access should be reviewed regularly and removed promptly when no longer required.

---

# 6. Content Standards

Content should:

- Align with TJF's mission and strategic priorities.
- Use approved branding.
- Be factually accurate.
- Respect copyright and intellectual property.
- Be written in clear and accessible language.
- Protect privacy and confidentiality.

Content may include:

- Programme updates.
- Community stories.
- Research findings.
- Event announcements.
- Volunteer opportunities.
- Donor acknowledgements (where appropriate).
- Educational campaigns.

---

# 7. Community Engagement

TJF encourages respectful dialogue.

The Communications Department should:

- Respond to reasonable enquiries in a timely manner.
- Correct factual inaccuracies where appropriate.
- Encourage constructive discussion.
- Avoid engaging in hostile or abusive exchanges.

---

# 8. Moderation

Content may be removed or hidden where it:

- Contains hate speech.
- Includes harassment or abuse.
- Promotes discrimination.
- Contains spam.
- Encourages violence.
- Shares confidential information.
- Violates applicable laws.

Moderation decisions should be documented where appropriate.

---

# 9. Personal Use

Employees and volunteers may use personal social media accounts freely.

However, when referencing TJF they should:

- Make it clear when expressing personal opinions.
- Avoid implying official endorsement.
- Respect confidentiality.
- Avoid sharing unpublished organizational information.
- Conduct themselves in a manner consistent with TJF's values.

---

# 10. Photography and Video

Images and videos shared through official accounts should:

- Be relevant to TJF's work.
- Respect the dignity of individuals.
- Comply with consent requirements.
- Avoid misleading editing or captions.
- Reflect diversity and inclusion.

Refer to the **Photography and Video Policy** for additional guidance.

---

# 11. Accessibility

Where practical, digital content should be accessible by:

- Using plain language.
- Adding alternative text for images.
- Providing captions for videos.
- Using readable colour contrast.
- Avoiding excessive text within images.

---

# 12. Security

To protect official accounts:

- Use strong, unique passwords.
- Enable multi-factor authentication (MFA).
- Limit administrative access.
- Monitor for unauthorized activity.
- Report suspected security incidents immediately.

---

# 13. Crisis Communications

During emergencies or reputational incidents:

- Only approved messages should be published.
- Scheduled content may be paused.
- Public responses should be coordinated with the Executive Director and Communications Department.

Refer to the **Crisis Communication Plan** for detailed procedures.

---

# 14. Monitoring and Analytics

The Communications Department should monitor:

- Audience growth.
- Reach.
- Engagement.
- Website referrals.
- Campaign performance.
- Community feedback.

Analytics should inform future content planning.

---

# 15. Record Keeping

Official posts, campaigns and significant interactions should be retained where required under TJF's Records Management Policy.

---

# 16. Roles and Responsibilities

## Executive Director

- Approves strategic digital communications.
- Authorizes responses to significant reputational issues.

---

## Communications & Public Relations Department

Responsible for:

- Content planning.
- Publishing.
- Community management.
- Analytics.
- Brand consistency.
- Platform governance.

---

## Programme Teams

- Provide verified programme information.
- Support storytelling.
- Supply photographs and success stories in accordance with TJF policies.

---

## All Staff and Volunteers

Individuals should:

- Follow this Policy.
- Protect confidential information.
- Use respectful language.
- Avoid unauthorized statements on behalf of TJF.

---

# 17. Review

This Policy should be reviewed:

- Every three years.
- Following major platform changes.
- After significant digital incidents.
- When communication technologies or legal requirements evolve.

---

# Conclusion

Responsible social media engagement strengthens transparency, builds relationships and expands the Foundation's ability to share knowledge and inspire action.

By following this Policy, The Jopetnah Foundation ensures that its digital presence reflects professionalism, integrity and a genuine commitment to the communities it serves.

---

> **Every post, comment and conversation contributes to the reputation and impact of The Jopetnah Foundation.**
