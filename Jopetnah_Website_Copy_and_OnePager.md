### Homepage — Mission Strip

### Homepage — Four Pillars Section
**Education & Youth Development**
Scholarships, mentorship, and digital-skills labs that keep kids in school and prepare them for the jobs of tomorrow.

**Health & Wellbeing**
Community health volunteers, mobile clinics, and mental-health education that keep families well enough to thrive — including malaria prevention, a leading cause of death in sub-Saharan Africa, responsible for 95% of the world's malaria deaths in 2024 (WHO, World Malaria Report 2025).

**Livelihoods**
Micro-grants, vocational training, and savings circles that turn a skill into a sustainable income.

**Environment & Community Development**
Clean water, sanitation, and climate-smart farming that protect the ground families depend on.

## PART B — "Send Home Hope" Diaspora Giving Circle: One-Pager

### Headline
**Send Home Hope.**
*A giving circle for the Jopetnah diaspora community — because home is never as far as it feels.*

### The Pitch
You left, but your community didn't leave your heart. The Send Home Hope Giving Circle lets you turn that pull you feel — the one that shows up every time you hear news from home — into something concrete: a child staying in school, a mother getting a check-up, a neighbor starting a small business.

### How It Works
1. **Join** — Sign up for a recurring monthly gift, starting at just $15/month
2. **Connect** — Join our private diaspora WhatsApp/Facebook community for real-time updates, direct from the field
3. **See it land** — Receive a quarterly "Where Your Gift Went" update with photos and stories from the specific program your tier supports
4. **Grow it** — Invite five friends to join your circle; circles that reach 10+ members get a shoutout and a dedicated update call with our Executive Director

### Membership Tiers
| Tier | Monthly Gift | What it funds |
|---|---|---|
| Seedling | $15 | One month of school supplies for one child |
| Rooted | $40 | One month of a Community Health Champion's outreach |
| Grove | $100 | One family's monthly "whole-family" bundled support |
| Elder | $250+ | Recognition in our diaspora newsletter + invite to virtual town halls |

### Why Recurring Giving (not one-time)?
A single $500 gift helps one family for one moment. A $40/month gift helps one family for a whole year — and lets Jopetnah plan programs with confidence instead of guessing what next quarter's funding will look like.

### Trust & Transparency
Every Send Home Hope member receives a direct quarterly report — not just a thank-you email, but real numbers: how many people were reached, what changed, and where every dollar went. You're not sending money into a void. You're watching it land.

### Call to Action
**Join the circle today at [website]/sendhomehope — because home is never as far as it feels.**
