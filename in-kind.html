Give in kind

Give what is already in your hands.

Your contribution does not have to be cash to change a life. TJF welcomes in-kind support: materials, equipment, skills, time and connections, matched to real needs across our four pillars.

Offer an in-kind giftDownload the guide (PDF)
Please contact us before sending anything. Do not ship or deliver items without our confirmation. We do not collect items at random; every gift is matched to a real family or community need. Online cash donations are not active. See giving status.

What in-kind giving makes possible

When a family is struggling, the missing piece is rarely only money. Sometimes it is a book that keeps a child learning, medical supplies, tools or training, or a connection that turns empty hands into productive ones. An in-kind gift becomes part of a connected response, woven into the whole-family model we are building.

Priority need: community libraries. Books, shelving and reading resources for Read to Lead are especially welcome. Read the one-page call (PDF) or learn about the community centre and library.

Ways you can give

Education & Youth Development

  • Books, learning materials and school supplies
  • Laptops, tablets or digital devices for skills labs
  • Mentorship time or curriculum support
  • Library resources or reading programmes

Health & Wellbeing

  • Medical supplies, basic medicines or first-aid kits
  • Mosquito nets and malaria prevention materials
  • Hygiene kits and nutrition support
  • Professional time from health workers or counsellors

Livelihoods & Skills

  • Tools for farming, tailoring, carpentry or small trades
  • Training sessions or vocational expertise
  • Business mentorship or financial literacy support
  • Equipment that helps someone start earning

Environment & Community

  • Seeds, farming tools or climate-smart agriculture materials
  • Water and sanitation supplies
  • Support for tree-planting or clean-up initiatives
  • Technical knowledge in sustainable practices

Foundational & organisational support

  • Legal, accounting or governance expertise
  • Design, branding or website help
  • Technology tools and software access
  • Meeting space, logistics or professional networks
  • Vaccines and cold-chain equipment for the Animal Health Initiative, through suitable partners

How in-kind gifts are handled

  • We say clearly what is most useful at each stage.
  • Offers are reviewed first. We may decline items we cannot use safely or well, such as medicines that are near or past expiry, or goods that cannot yet be stored or distributed responsibly.
  • Accepted gifts are documented, and we are committed to accounting for how they are used.
  • We share updates so you can see how your gift became part of a larger story.

TJF is in its founding stage and is not yet an organisation with warehouses and complex systems. We would rather receive the right things at the right time than accumulate what cannot yet be used well.

How to offer a gift

  1. Tell us what you would like to offer: what it is, how much, where it is, and any conditions.
  2. We reply with whether and when it can be used, and how it should be delivered.
  3. Only then do you send or hand over the gift. We confirm receipt in writing.
